MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.7.4 - Diagnostic Media Inspection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to check media containing diagnostic programs for malicious code before use, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.4.

2. SCOPE
All portable media (USB drives, DVDs, external drives) used for maintenance or diagnostics on Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All diagnostic media is scanned for malware before use on any CUI system.
b. Scanning is performed on a quarantine system not connected to the CUI network.
c. Vendor-provided diagnostic media is treated as untrusted until scanned and cleared.
d. Scan results are documented before media is connected to CUI systems.
e. Media that fails scanning is quarantined and reported to Randy Bowles.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, scans and documents all diagnostic media before use.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unscanned media on CUI systems is a security violation requiring immediate investigation.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
