SECURITY ASSESSMENT POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.12.4 - System Security Plan
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to develop, document, and periodically update System Security Plans (SSPs) that describe the system boundary, security requirements, and security controls, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.12.4.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. An SSP is maintained that documents the CUI system boundary, users, and controls.
b. The SSP describes all 110 NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 controls and their implementation status.
c. The SSP is reviewed and updated at least annually.
d. Keith Merritt approves the SSP.
e. The SSP is protected from unauthorized access.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, authors and maintains the SSP. Keith Merritt reviews and approves it annually.

5. ENFORCEMENT
An outdated SSP (>1 year old) is treated as a security compliance finding.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
