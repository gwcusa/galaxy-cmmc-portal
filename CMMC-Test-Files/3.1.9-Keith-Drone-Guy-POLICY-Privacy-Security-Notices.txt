1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures that privacy and security notices
consistent with CUI rules are displayed to users before
they log in to systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

2. REQUIRED NOTICES

2.1 System Login Banner
  All company workstations and servers display the following
  banner at login (before authentication):

  "THIS IS A PRIVATE COMPUTER SYSTEM. Use is restricted to
  authorized personnel only. All activity on this system
  is monitored and recorded. Unauthorized use is prohibited
  and may be subject to criminal prosecution under applicable
  laws. By logging in, you acknowledge that you have no
  expectation of privacy on this system and consent to
  monitoring. This system may contain Controlled Unclassified
  Information (CUI). Handle CUI in accordance with company
  policy and applicable federal regulations."

2.2 Remote Access / VPN Banner
  VPN connections display an equivalent notice prior to
  authentication completion.

2.3 Annual Acknowledgment
  All staff review and sign the CUI Handling Agreement
  annually, which incorporates CUI privacy and handling
  requirements per 32 CFR Part 2002.

3. IMPLEMENTATION
  Windows logon banner is enforced via GPO:
    Computer Config > Windows Settings > Security Settings
    > Local Policies > Security Options:
    Interactive logon: Message title for users attempting to log on
    Interactive logon: Message text for users attempting to log on
