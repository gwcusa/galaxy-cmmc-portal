ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.4 - Separation of Duties
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy separates duties of individuals at Bumpass Fire & Rescue to reduce the risk of malevolent activity without collusion, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.4.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue personnel with access to CUI systems or privileged administrative functions.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. No single individual shall control all phases of a critical business or IT operation.
b. Account creation/approval and account provisioning roles are assigned to different individuals.
c. Mike Sadler administers accounts; Randy Bowles approves new privileged access requests.
d. System audit log access is restricted to personnel independent of daily operations.
e. Dual authorization is required for deletion of CUI data.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler implements technical controls. Randy Bowles ensures organizational enforcement.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Deliberate circumvention of separation-of-duties controls is grounds for immediate termination.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
