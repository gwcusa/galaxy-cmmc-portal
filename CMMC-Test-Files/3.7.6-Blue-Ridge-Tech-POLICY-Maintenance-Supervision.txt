MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.7.6 - Maintenance Personnel Supervision
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to supervise maintenance activities of personnel without required access authorization, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.6.

2. SCOPE
All maintenance personnel who do not have CUI access authorization but require physical or logical access to perform maintenance.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Unauthorized maintenance personnel are supervised by Nathan Cruz at all times.
b. Supervision includes monitoring all activities within CUI areas.
c. Supervised personnel may not be left unattended with CUI systems.
d. If supervision is not possible, CUI systems are powered off and locked before maintenance begins.
e. Supervision activities are documented in the maintenance log.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, is responsible for supervising all unauthorized maintenance personnel.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unattended unauthorized maintenance in CUI areas is a security incident.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
