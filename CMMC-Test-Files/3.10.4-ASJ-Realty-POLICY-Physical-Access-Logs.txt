PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.10.4 - Physical Access Audit Logging
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to maintain audit logs of physical access to CUI facilities, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.4.

2. SCOPE
All physical access points to ASJ Realty CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Physical access logs record each entry and exit from CUI areas.
b. Logs include: individual identity, date, time, and access point.
c. Physical access logs are reviewed monthly by Sandra Okonkwo.
d. Anomalous physical access (after hours, weekends, unknown individuals) generates immediate review.
e. Physical access logs are retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, maintains and reviews physical access logs.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized physical access detected in logs is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
