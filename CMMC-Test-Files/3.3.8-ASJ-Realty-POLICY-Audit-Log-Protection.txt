AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.3.8 - Audit Log Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy protects ASJ Realty audit information and tools from unauthorized access, modification, and deletion, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.8.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty audit records, logging infrastructure, and audit management tools.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logs are stored in a system where operational users cannot delete or modify records.
b. Access to raw audit log data is restricted to Sandra Okonkwo and authorized assessors.
c. Audit log storage uses append-only or immutable storage mechanisms.
d. Backup copies of audit logs are stored separately from production systems.
e. Log access attempts are themselves logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, manages audit log access controls and immutability settings.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any modification or deletion of audit records is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
