PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.10.1 - Physical Access Authorization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits physical access to ASJ Realty CUI systems to authorized individuals, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.1.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty facilities where CUI systems are located.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI system areas are access-controlled with electronic key card or coded entry.
b. A list of authorized personnel with physical access is maintained by Anthony James.
c. The authorized access list is reviewed and updated quarterly.
d. Visitors are not permitted unescorted access to CUI areas.
e. Access rights are revoked within 24 hours of termination.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Anthony James, Office Manager, approves physical access authorizations. Sandra Okonkwo maintains technical controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized physical access to CUI areas is a security incident.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
