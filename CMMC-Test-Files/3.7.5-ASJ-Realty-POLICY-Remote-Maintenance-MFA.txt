MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.7.5 - Remote Maintenance MFA
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to require MFA to establish remote maintenance sessions and to terminate such connections after maintenance, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.5.

2. SCOPE
All remote maintenance sessions on ASJ Realty CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Remote maintenance sessions require MFA before the session is established.
b. Session credentials are unique to the maintenance session and not reused.
c. Remote maintenance connections are terminated immediately after the session ends.
d. Remote sessions are logged with start time, end time, and actions performed.
e. Sandra Okonkwo monitors all remote maintenance sessions in real-time.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, manages and monitors remote maintenance sessions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Remote maintenance sessions without MFA are blocked and investigated.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
