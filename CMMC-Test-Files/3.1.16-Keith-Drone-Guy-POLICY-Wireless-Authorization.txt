ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.16 - Wireless Access Authorization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to authorize wireless access connections prior to allowing them, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.16.

2. SCOPE
All wireless access to Keith the Drone Guy LLC networks and systems that process or store CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Wireless access points are registered and approved by Keith Merritt before deployment.
b. Only authorized devices may connect to the Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI wireless network.
c. MAC address filtering and 802.1X authentication are used for CUI network WiFi.
d. Guest wireless networks are isolated from CUI systems.
e. Unauthorized wireless access points (rogue APs) are detected and blocked.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, manages wireless infrastructure and authorization processes.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized wireless access points must be removed within 2 hours of detection.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
