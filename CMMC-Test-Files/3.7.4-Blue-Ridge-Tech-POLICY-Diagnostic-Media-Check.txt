MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.7.4 - Diagnostic Media Inspection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to check media containing diagnostic programs for malicious code before use, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.4.

2. SCOPE
All portable media (USB drives, DVDs, external drives) used for maintenance or diagnostics on Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All diagnostic media is scanned for malware before use on any CUI system.
b. Scanning is performed on a quarantine system not connected to the CUI network.
c. Vendor-provided diagnostic media is treated as untrusted until scanned and cleared.
d. Scan results are documented before media is connected to CUI systems.
e. Media that fails scanning is quarantined and reported to Victor Osei.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, scans and documents all diagnostic media before use.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unscanned media on CUI systems is a security violation requiring immediate investigation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
