IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.5.1 - User Identification
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Keith the Drone Guy LLC's requirements for identifying all information system users, processes, and devices, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.1.

2. SCOPE
All users, devices, and processes that access Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Every user account is assigned a unique username tied to a specific individual.
b. Every device is assigned a unique device identity registered in the asset inventory.
c. Shared or anonymous accounts are prohibited for CUI system access.
d. Service accounts are documented with a named owner responsible for the account.
e. Keith Merritt maintains and reviews the user and device identity registry quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, maintains user and device identity records.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Use of anonymous or unregistered identities on CUI systems is prohibited.
