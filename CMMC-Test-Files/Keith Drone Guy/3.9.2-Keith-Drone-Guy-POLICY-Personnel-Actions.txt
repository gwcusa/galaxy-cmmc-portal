PERSONNEL SECURITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.9.2 - Personnel Termination and Transfer
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Keith the Drone Guy LLC takes protective measures when personnel are terminated or transferred, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.9.2.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC personnel departures, transfers, and changes in role affecting CUI access.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Upon termination, all CUI access (logical and physical) is revoked within 24 hours.
b. Keith the Drone Guy LLC property including devices, keys, and media is collected at or before departure.
c. Terminated personnel are briefed on continuing CUI confidentiality obligations.
d. Transfer to a new role triggers access review to ensure alignment with new responsibilities.
e. Termination procedures are documented and tracked via a termination checklist.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, initiates termination procedures. Keith Merritt executes logical access revocation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to complete termination procedures within 24 hours is a policy violation.
