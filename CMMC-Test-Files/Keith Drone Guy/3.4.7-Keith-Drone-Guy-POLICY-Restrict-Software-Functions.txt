CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.4.7 - Restrict Software Functions
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy restricts, disables, or prevents the use of programs, functions, ports, protocols, and services not required at Keith the Drone Guy LLC, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.7.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems, endpoints, and network devices.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Firewall rules block all ports and protocols not required for business operations.
b. Software restriction policies prevent execution of unapproved applications.
c. Removable media autorun is disabled on all endpoints.
d. Macro execution in Office documents is restricted to digitally signed macros only.
e. Keith Merritt reviews firewall rules and software restriction policies quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures and maintains software and network function restrictions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized software functions discovered in audits are disabled within 24 hours.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
