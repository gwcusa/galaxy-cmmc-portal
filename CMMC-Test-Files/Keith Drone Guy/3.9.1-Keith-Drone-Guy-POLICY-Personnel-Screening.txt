PERSONNEL SECURITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.9.1 - Personnel Screening
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Keith the Drone Guy LLC's requirements for screening individuals prior to granting access to CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.9.1.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC employees, contractors, and other personnel who will have access to CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All personnel with CUI access must complete a background check before access is granted.
b. Background check includes: criminal history, employment verification, and reference checks.
c. Results are reviewed and approved by Keith Merritt before CUI access is granted.
d. Contractors with CUI access must provide evidence of background screening by their employer.
e. Background check results are retained in a secure personnel file for 5 years.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, reviews and approves background check results. Keith Merritt grants CUI access only after approval.

5. ENFORCEMENT
No individual may receive CUI access without a completed and approved background check.
