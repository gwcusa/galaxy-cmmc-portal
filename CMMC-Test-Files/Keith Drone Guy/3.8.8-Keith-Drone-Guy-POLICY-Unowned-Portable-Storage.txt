MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.8.8 - Unowned Portable Storage Prohibition
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy prohibits the use of portable storage devices when such devices have no identifiable owner, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.8.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI endpoints and removable storage found in or around Keith the Drone Guy LLC facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Unidentified or ownerless portable storage devices found in Keith the Drone Guy LLC facilities are quarantined and reported to Keith Merritt.
b. Found storage devices are never connected to Keith the Drone Guy LLC systems without malware scanning and approval.
c. The approved media register identifies the owner of every authorized device.
d. Personnel are trained not to connect found USB drives or storage devices.
e. Intune device control ensures only allowlisted devices connect to CUI endpoints.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, enforces the no-unowned-media policy and trains personnel.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Connecting an unowned device to a CUI endpoint is a security incident.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
