1. PURPOSE
Keith the Drone Guy LLC performs aerial survey and mapping
services under federal contracts, including work for the Army
Corps of Engineers and USDA. Personnel with access to CUI
data, drone flight logs, and federal imagery must understand
security risks and how to protect sensitive information.

2. SCOPE
Applies to Keith Anderson (owner), all subcontractors, and
any part-time staff who access company systems or handle
federal contract data.

3. TRAINING REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Initial Training
All new employees and subcontractors must complete security
awareness training BEFORE receiving system access. Training
covers:
  - What is CUI and how to identify it
  - Acceptable use of company systems
  - Password security and MFA setup
  - Phishing and social engineering awareness
  - How and when to report security incidents
  - Physical security of drone equipment containing CUI data

3.2 Annual Refresher Training
All personnel complete annual refresher training by January 31
of each year. Training is delivered via KnowBe4 platform and
takes approximately 45 minutes.

3.3 Phishing Simulations
Quarterly simulated phishing emails are sent to all personnel.
Any employee who clicks a simulated phishing link is required
to complete additional targeted training within 5 business days.

3.4 New Threat Training
When a significant new threat is identified (e.g., new malware
campaign targeting drone operators), Keith Anderson will notify
all personnel via email and require acknowledgment within 48 hrs.

4. TRAINING RECORDS
Completion certificates and training records are maintained by
Keith Anderson in the company SharePoint training folder for
a minimum of 3 years.

5. ACKNOWLEDGMENT
All personnel sign an Annual Security Awareness Acknowledgment
Form confirming they have read and understood this policy and
completed the required training.

6. ENFORCEMENT
Personnel who fail to complete required training within the
allotted time will have system access suspended until training
is complete.
