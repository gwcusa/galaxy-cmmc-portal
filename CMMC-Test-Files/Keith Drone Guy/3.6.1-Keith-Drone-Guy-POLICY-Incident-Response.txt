INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.6.1 - Incident Response Capability
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Keith the Drone Guy LLC's capability to handle information security incidents, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.1.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC information systems and personnel.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Keith the Drone Guy LLC maintains a documented Incident Response Plan (IRP).
b. The IRP defines roles, responsibilities, and procedures for incident handling.
c. Keith Merritt is the primary incident responder; Keith Merritt provides management escalation.
d. The IRP is tested annually via tabletop exercise.
e. Incident response contacts include US-CERT reporting capability for CUI-related incidents.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, leads incident response. Keith Merritt approves the IRP and provides executive escalation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
All personnel must report suspected security incidents to Keith Merritt within 1 hour of discovery.
