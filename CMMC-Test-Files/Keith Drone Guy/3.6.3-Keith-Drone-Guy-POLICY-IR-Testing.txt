INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.6.3 - Incident Response Testing
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to test incident response capabilities to determine their effectiveness, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.3.

2. SCOPE
The Keith the Drone Guy LLC Incident Response Plan and all personnel with IR responsibilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. An IR tabletop exercise is conducted annually.
b. The exercise tests at least one realistic threat scenario relevant to Keith the Drone Guy LLC's environment.
c. Exercise results are documented and reviewed by Keith Merritt.
d. Identified gaps are tracked in a remediation plan.
e. Remediation items are completed within 90 days of the exercise.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, designs and facilitates IR exercises. Keith Merritt participates and approves results.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Annual IR testing is mandatory; failure to test results in a security program finding.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
