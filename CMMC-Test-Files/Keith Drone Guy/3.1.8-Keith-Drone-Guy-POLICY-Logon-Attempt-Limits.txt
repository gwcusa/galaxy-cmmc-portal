ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.8 - Unsuccessful Logon Attempt Limits
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits unsuccessful logon attempts at Keith the Drone Guy LLC to protect against brute-force attacks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.8.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC systems and devices that authenticate users with CUI system access.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed logon attempts.
b. Lockout duration is 30 minutes or until manually unlocked by Keith Merritt.
c. Account lockout thresholds are enforced via Group Policy and Azure AD.
d. Lockout events generate a security alert reviewed by Keith Merritt.
e. Failed logon attempts are retained in audit logs for 90 days.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures and monitors account lockout settings.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Tampering with lockout configurations is a security policy violation.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
