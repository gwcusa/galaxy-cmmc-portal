MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.7.5 - Remote Maintenance MFA
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to require MFA to establish remote maintenance sessions and to terminate such connections after maintenance, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.5.

2. SCOPE
All remote maintenance sessions on Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Remote maintenance sessions require MFA before the session is established.
b. Session credentials are unique to the maintenance session and not reused.
c. Remote maintenance connections are terminated immediately after the session ends.
d. Remote sessions are logged with start time, end time, and actions performed.
e. Keith Merritt monitors all remote maintenance sessions in real-time.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, manages and monitors remote maintenance sessions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Remote maintenance sessions without MFA are blocked and investigated.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
