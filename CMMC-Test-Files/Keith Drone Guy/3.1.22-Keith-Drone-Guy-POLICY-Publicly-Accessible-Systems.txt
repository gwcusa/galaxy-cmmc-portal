ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.22 - CUI on Publicly Accessible Systems
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls CUI posted or processed on publicly accessible Keith the Drone Guy LLC systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.22.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC publicly accessible systems including websites, social media, and public-facing portals.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI is prohibited from being posted on any publicly accessible system.
b. Keith Merritt must review and approve all content before it is published publicly.
c. Keith Merritt conducts quarterly scans of public-facing systems for inadvertent CUI exposure.
d. Discovered CUI on public systems is removed within 2 hours and reported to management.
e. Staff are trained annually on CUI handling and prohibition of public posting.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt scans public systems. Keith Merritt approves public content. All staff are responsible for not posting CUI.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Posting CUI to publicly accessible systems is a serious policy violation subject to immediate investigation.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
