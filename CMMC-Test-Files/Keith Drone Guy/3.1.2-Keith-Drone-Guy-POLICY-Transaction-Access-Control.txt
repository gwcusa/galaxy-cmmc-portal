ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.2 - Transaction and Function Access Limits
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits Keith the Drone Guy LLC system access to the types of transactions and functions that authorized users are permitted to execute, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.2.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC information systems and users who access CUI or CUI-supporting systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. User accounts are assigned roles that restrict access to only required functions.
b. Role assignments are documented and reviewed semi-annually by Keith Merritt.
c. No user shall have access beyond what is required for their job function.
d. Administrative roles are restricted to personnel with a documented operational need.
e. Role changes require written approval before implementation.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, manages role assignments. Keith Merritt approves elevated access requests.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized function access is treated as a policy violation subject to disciplinary action.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
