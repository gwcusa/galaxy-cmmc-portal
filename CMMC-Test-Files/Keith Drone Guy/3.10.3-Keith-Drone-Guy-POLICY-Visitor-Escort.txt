PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.10.3 - Visitor Escort and Monitoring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to escort visitors and monitor visitor activity in CUI areas, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.3.

2. SCOPE
All visitors (non-Keith the Drone Guy LLC employees) entering CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All visitors are signed in via the visitor log before entering CUI areas.
b. Visitors are escorted by authorized Keith the Drone Guy LLC personnel at all times in CUI areas.
c. Visitors are never left unattended with CUI systems or documents.
d. Visitor log records name, organization, purpose, escort, entry/exit times.
e. Visitor log is retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt or Keith Merritt escorts all visitors in CUI areas. Keith Merritt maintains visitor log.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unescorted visitors in CUI areas are a security incident.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
