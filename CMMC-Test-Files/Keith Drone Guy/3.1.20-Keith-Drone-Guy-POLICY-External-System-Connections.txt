ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.20 - External System Connection Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to verify and control connections to external systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.20.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC connections to systems, networks, or cloud services outside of direct organizational control.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. External system connections require a documented Interconnection Security Agreement (ISA).
b. Keith Merritt must approve all new external system connections before establishment.
c. Keith Merritt maintains a register of all approved external connections.
d. External connections are reviewed annually and when operational needs change.
e. Connections to external systems are terminated when no longer operationally required.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, maintains the external connection register. Keith Merritt approves connections.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved external connections are blocked and investigated as security incidents.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
