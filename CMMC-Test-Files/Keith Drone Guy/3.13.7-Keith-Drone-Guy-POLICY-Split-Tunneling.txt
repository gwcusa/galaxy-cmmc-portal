SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.13.7 - Split Tunneling Prohibition
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy prevents Keith the Drone Guy LLC remote access VPN clients from using split tunneling, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.7.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC remote access VPN connections.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Split tunneling is disabled on all VPN profiles.
b. When VPN is connected, all traffic (including internet) routes through the Keith the Drone Guy LLC gateway.
c. VPN configuration is enforced by managed Intune profiles and cannot be changed by users.
d. Keith Merritt verifies split tunneling is disabled quarterly.
e. Any detected split tunnel configuration is immediately remediated.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures VPN profiles with split tunneling disabled.

5. ENFORCEMENT
VPN connections with split tunneling enabled are disconnected and reconfigured.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
