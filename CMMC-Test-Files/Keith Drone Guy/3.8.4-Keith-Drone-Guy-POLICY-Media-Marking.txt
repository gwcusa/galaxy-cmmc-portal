MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.8.4 - CUI Media Marking
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to mark media with necessary CUI markings and distribution limitations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.4.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC physical and digital media containing CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Physical media containing CUI is labeled with "CUI" on the media and its container.
b. CUI documents include CUI header/footer markings.
c. Digital files containing CUI include CUI designation in the file name or metadata.
d. Media labels are applied by Keith Merritt before the media is used to store CUI.
e. Keith Merritt verifies CUI markings quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, applies and verifies CUI markings on all CUI media.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unmarked CUI media is a compliance finding requiring immediate remediation.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
