1. PURPOSE
This policy trains personnel to recognize and report potential
indicators of insider threat activity at Keith the Drone Guy LLC.

2. INSIDER THREAT INDICATORS - STAFF ARE TRAINED TO RECOGNIZE
  Technical indicators:
    - Accessing data not required for job duties
    - Downloading unusually large volumes of files
    - Attempting to access systems after termination
    - Inserting unauthorized removable media
    - Using personal email for work files
    - Disabling security software
  Behavioral indicators:
    - Expressing intent to harm the company or government
    - Disgruntlement combined with data access changes
    - Unexplained wealth or lifestyle changes
    - Working unusual hours with unusual data access

3. REPORTING REQUIREMENTS
  Any observed indicator must be reported to Keith Merritt
  within 24 hours. Reports are made via:
    - Direct communication (preferred)
    - Email to keith@keiththedroneguy.com (confidential)

4. TRAINING DELIVERY
  Training is delivered via KnowBe4 annually and reinforced
  during quarterly team meetings where one security topic
  is reviewed. Insider threat is covered each Q3 meeting.

5. ANTI-RETALIATION
  No employee will face retaliation for good-faith reporting
  of suspected insider threat activity.
