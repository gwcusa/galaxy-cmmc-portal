1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes how CUI is permitted to flow between
systems, people, and locations at Keith the Drone Guy LLC,
ensuring that CUI is not transmitted to unauthorized
destinations or through unapproved channels.

2. APPROVED CUI FLOW PATHS

2.1 Internal Flows (Permitted)
  - CUI may flow from the government client portal to the
    secure CUI project folder on the company NAS only
  - CUI may be shared between authorized staff via the
    company Microsoft 365 tenant (encrypted in transit)
  - CUI may flow to OneDrive for Business (CUI subfolder only)
    but NOT to personal OneDrive or consumer cloud services

2.2 External Flows (Permitted Only With Authorization)
  - CUI may be transmitted to subcontractors holding an active
    DFARS 252.204-7012 flow-down agreement
  - CUI may be returned to the government client via SFTP or
    the approved government portal (DIBBS, DoD SAFE, etc.)
  - All external CUI transfers must be logged in the CUI
    Transfer Log (SharePoint: CUI-Logs)

3. PROHIBITED FLOWS
  - Personal email (Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, etc.): PROHIBITED
  - Consumer cloud storage (Dropbox, Google Drive): PROHIBITED
  - Unencrypted FTP or HTTP: PROHIBITED
  - Personal mobile devices not enrolled in Intune: PROHIBITED
  - Printing CUI to uncontrolled network printers: PROHIBITED

4. TECHNICAL CONTROLS ENFORCING FLOW
  - Microsoft 365 DLP policy blocks CUI from leaving the tenant
    to personal email addresses or consumer domains
  - Conditional Access blocks CUI SharePoint access from
    non-Intune-enrolled devices
  - Network firewall blocks outbound SMTP except via M365

5. MONITORING
  Keith Merritt reviews M365 DLP incident reports weekly.
  Any unauthorized CUI flow triggers immediate incident response.
