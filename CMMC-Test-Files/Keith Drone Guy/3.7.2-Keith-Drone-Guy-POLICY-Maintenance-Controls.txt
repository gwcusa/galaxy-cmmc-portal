MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.7.2 - Controlled Maintenance
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to provide controls on tools, techniques, mechanisms, and personnel who perform maintenance, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.2.

2. SCOPE
All maintenance activities on Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems by internal staff or vendors.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Vendor maintenance personnel are pre-approved by Keith Merritt before being granted access.
b. Vendor access is escorted by Keith Merritt throughout the maintenance activity.
c. Vendor tools and diagnostic software are inspected before use.
d. Remote vendor access is monitored in real-time by Keith Merritt.
e. All vendor maintenance is logged with contact details, scope, and outcome.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, controls and supervises all maintenance activities.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unsupervised vendor access to CUI systems is prohibited.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
