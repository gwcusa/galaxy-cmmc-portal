AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.3.2 - User Activity Audit Traceability
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Keith the Drone Guy LLC audit logs can be traced to individual users and processes to support accountability, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.2.

2. SCOPE
All CUI systems at Keith the Drone Guy LLC where user activity occurs.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit records include user identity, timestamp, event type, source IP, and outcome.
b. Shared accounts are prohibited to ensure individual traceability.
c. User session IDs are included in all audit events.
d. Audit records are sufficient to reconstruct user actions during an investigation.
e. Keith Merritt tests traceability during quarterly log reviews.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, ensures log fields capture sufficient data for traceability.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with insufficient audit traceability must be remediated within 30 days.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
