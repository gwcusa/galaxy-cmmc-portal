INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.6.2 - Incident Tracking and Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to track, document, and report incidents to appropriate officials, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.2.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC security incidents involving CUI systems or data.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All incidents are tracked in the IT helpdesk ticketing system.
b. Incidents are classified by severity (Low/Medium/High/Critical).
c. High and Critical incidents are reported to Keith Merritt within 1 hour.
d. CUI-related incidents are reported to DoD/US-CERT as required.
e. Incident records are retained for 3 years.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, tracks and documents all incidents. Keith Merritt receives escalation reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to report a known incident is a policy violation subject to disciplinary action.
