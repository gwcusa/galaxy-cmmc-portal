1. PURPOSE
This policy requires periodic risk assessments of KDG
organizational operations, assets, and personnel to identify
and respond to information security risks.

2. RISK ASSESSMENT FREQUENCY
  - Formal risk assessment: annually (each March)
  - Ad-hoc assessment: after any significant incident,
    major infrastructure change, or new CUI contract award

3. RISK ASSESSMENT SCOPE
Each assessment covers:
  - All systems that store, process, or transmit CUI
  - Personnel with access to CUI systems
  - Physical facilities housing CUI systems
  - Third-party connections and service providers

4. RISK ASSESSMENT METHODOLOGY
KDG uses a simplified risk framework based on NIST SP 800-30:
  Step 1: Identify threat sources and threat events
  Step 2: Determine likelihood of each threat event
  Step 3: Determine impact of each threat event
  Step 4: Calculate risk = Likelihood x Impact
  Step 5: Prioritize risks and identify mitigations
  Step 6: Document in Risk Register

5. RISK REGISTER
  Risks are tracked in the KDG Risk Register
  (SharePoint: IT/Risk-Register.xlsx).
  Each risk includes: description, likelihood, impact,
  risk level, owner, mitigation, and target resolution date.

6. REVIEW AND ACCEPTANCE
  Keith Merritt reviews and accepts the risk register annually.
  Risks rated HIGH require mitigation plan within 30 days.
