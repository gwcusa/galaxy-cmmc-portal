ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.1 - Authorized User Access
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits Keith the Drone Guy LLC information system access to authorized users, processes acting on behalf of authorized users, and authorized devices, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.1.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC systems, personnel, and contractors that process, store, or transmit Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Maintain a current authorized user list for each CUI system.
b. Require documented approval before provisioning any account.
c. Keith Merritt reviews the authorized user list quarterly and removes stale entries.
d. Shared or generic accounts are prohibited without documented exception.
e. Disable accounts within 24 hours of termination or contract expiration.
f. All system access events are logged and monitored.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, is responsible for implementation. Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, holds overall accountability.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Policy violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
