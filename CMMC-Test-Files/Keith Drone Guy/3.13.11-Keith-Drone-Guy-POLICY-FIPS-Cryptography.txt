SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.13.11 - FIPS-Validated Cryptography
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to employ FIPS-validated cryptography when protecting CUI, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.11.

2. SCOPE
All cryptographic functions protecting CUI at Keith the Drone Guy LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. FIPS 140-2 mode is enabled on all endpoints processing CUI.
b. Only FIPS-validated cryptographic modules are used for CUI protection.
c. Non-FIPS algorithms (MD5, RC4, DES, 3DES, SHA-1) are prohibited for CUI protection.
d. FIPS mode is enforced via Group Policy.
e. FIPS compliance is verified quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, enables and maintains FIPS mode on all CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with FIPS mode disabled must be remediated within 24 hours.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
