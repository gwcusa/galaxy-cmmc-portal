ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.18 - Mobile Device Connection Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the connection of mobile devices to Keith the Drone Guy LLC systems and CUI networks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.18.

2. SCOPE
All mobile devices (smartphones, tablets, laptops) connecting to Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All mobile devices connecting to Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI resources must be enrolled in Intune MDM.
b. Non-enrolled personal devices may not access CUI systems or networks.
c. Mobile devices must meet compliance requirements (encrypted, PIN-locked, current OS).
d. Keith Merritt approves mobile device enrollment requests from staff.
e. Lost or stolen devices are remotely wiped within 4 hours of report.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, manages the MDM solution and mobile device compliance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unenrolled mobile devices detected on CUI networks are blocked and reported.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
