SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.14.6 - Security Monitoring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to monitor information systems to detect attacks and indicators of potential attacks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.6.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems and the networks through which CUI flows.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Microsoft Sentinel provides continuous security monitoring and attack detection.
b. Sentinel analytics rules are configured to detect known attack patterns (TTPs).
c. Defender for Endpoint monitors endpoint behavior for attack indicators.
d. Keith Merritt triages Sentinel incidents within 2 hours during business hours.
e. After-hours critical incidents trigger automated alert to Keith Merritt via SMS/email.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures and monitors attack detection controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unreviewed Sentinel incidents beyond SLA are escalated to Keith Merritt.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
