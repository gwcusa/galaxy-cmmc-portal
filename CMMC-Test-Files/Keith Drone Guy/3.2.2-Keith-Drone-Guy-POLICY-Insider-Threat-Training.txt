AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.2.2 - Insider Threat Awareness Training
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Keith the Drone Guy LLC trains personnel to recognize and report potential indicators of insider threat, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.2.2.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC personnel with access to CUI systems or facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Insider threat awareness training is completed annually by all personnel.
b. Training covers behavioral indicators, reporting channels, and non-retaliation protections.
c. Personnel are trained on the Keith the Drone Guy LLC anonymous reporting mechanism.
d. Training records are maintained for 3 years.
e. Keith Merritt receives quarterly insider threat awareness briefings.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, administers insider threat training. Keith Merritt oversees the insider threat program.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to complete insider threat training results in CUI access suspension.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
