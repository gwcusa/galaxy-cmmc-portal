PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.10.5 - Physical Access Device Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to control and manage physical access devices (keys, key cards, PINs), per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.5.

2. SCOPE
All physical access devices used to access Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A register of all physical access devices (keys, PINs, badges) is maintained.
b. Physical access devices are issued only to authorized personnel.
c. Lost or stolen access devices are reported and deactivated within 2 hours.
d. Access PINs are changed quarterly or upon compromise.
e. Access devices are collected upon personnel termination.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, approves access device issuance. Keith Merritt maintains the access device register.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unreported loss of physical access devices is a policy violation.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
