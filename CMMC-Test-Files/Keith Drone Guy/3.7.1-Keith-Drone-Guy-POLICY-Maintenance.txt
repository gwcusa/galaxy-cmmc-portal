MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.7.1 - System Maintenance
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Keith the Drone Guy LLC's requirements for performing maintenance on information systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.1.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC hardware and software systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Maintenance activities are scheduled and documented in advance.
b. Security patches are applied within 30 days of release (critical: 72 hours).
c. Maintenance windows are communicated to Keith Merritt before scheduled downtime.
d. Post-maintenance verification confirms system functionality and security settings.
e. All maintenance activities are logged with date, type, and personnel involved.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, schedules and performs all system maintenance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Overdue patches trigger automated alerts and escalation to Keith Merritt after 30 days.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
