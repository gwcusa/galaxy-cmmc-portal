IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.5.9 - Temporary Password Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy allows Keith the Drone Guy LLC to employ temporary passwords with an immediate change requirement upon first use, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.9.

2. SCOPE
All temporary, one-time, or provisioning passwords issued by Keith the Drone Guy LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Temporary passwords require immediate change upon first login.
b. Temporary passwords expire within 24 hours if not used.
c. Temporary passwords are communicated to users via secure channel (encrypted email or in-person).
d. Keith Merritt logs all temporary password issuances.
e. Accounts with temporary passwords that expire unused are reviewed within 48 hours.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, issues and tracks temporary passwords.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Temporary passwords that are not changed within 24 hours result in account lockout.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
