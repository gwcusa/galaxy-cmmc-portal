ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.13 - Remote Access Encryption
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to employ cryptographic mechanisms to protect the confidentiality of remote access sessions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.13.

2. SCOPE
All remote access connections to Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All remote access sessions use TLS 1.2 or higher encryption.
b. VPN connections use AES-256 encryption minimum.
c. Weak cipher suites (RC4, DES, 3DES, TLS 1.0, TLS 1.1) are disabled.
d. Remote desktop sessions require NLA and TLS encryption.
e. Keith Merritt reviews VPN and TLS cipher configurations quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures and maintains encryption settings for remote access.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted remote access connections are blocked and reported.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
