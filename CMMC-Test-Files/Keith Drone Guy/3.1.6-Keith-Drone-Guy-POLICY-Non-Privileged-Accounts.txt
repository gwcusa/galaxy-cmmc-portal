ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.6 - Use of Non-Privileged Accounts
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC personnel to use non-privileged accounts when accessing non-security functions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.6.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC personnel who hold or have access to administrative or privileged accounts.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Users with administrative accounts must maintain separate standard accounts for daily work.
b. Email, web browsing, and productivity applications must be used via non-privileged accounts.
c. Privileged accounts shall not be used to access social media or personal email.
d. Keith Merritt shall enforce dual-account policy through technical controls where feasible.
e. Compliance with this policy is verified during quarterly access reviews.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, maintains dual-account configurations. Keith Merritt enforces the policy organizationally.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Using a privileged account for non-administrative tasks is a policy violation subject to corrective action.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
