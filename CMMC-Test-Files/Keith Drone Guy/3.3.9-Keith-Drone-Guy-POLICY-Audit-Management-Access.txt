AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.3.9 - Audit Management Access
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits management of audit logging to a subset of privileged users, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.9.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC audit logging infrastructure and SIEM tools.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Management of audit log configuration is restricted to Keith Merritt only.
b. Standard users have no access to configure, modify, or disable audit logging.
c. Audit management access is reviewed quarterly.
d. Any changes to audit logging configuration require documented justification.
e. Changes to logging configuration generate alerts to Keith Merritt.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, is the sole administrator of the audit logging system.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized changes to audit logging configuration are security incidents.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
