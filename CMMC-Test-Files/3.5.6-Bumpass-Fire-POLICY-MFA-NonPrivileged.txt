IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.5.6 - MFA for Non-Privileged Accounts
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to employ multifactor authentication for network access to non-privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.6.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue standard (non-privileged) user accounts that access CUI systems over a network.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. MFA is required for all standard user accounts when accessing CUI resources over a network.
b. This includes access to Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Teams, and the corporate VPN.
c. MFA enrollment is mandatory for all staff within 24 hours of account creation.
d. Users who fail to complete MFA enrollment lose network CUI access until enrolled.
e. MFA status is audited monthly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, enforces MFA enrollment and compliance for all standard user accounts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Network access to CUI systems without MFA is technically blocked.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
