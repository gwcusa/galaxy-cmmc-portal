PERSONNEL SECURITY POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.9.2 - Personnel Termination and Transfer
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures ASJ Realty takes protective measures when personnel are terminated or transferred, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.9.2.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty personnel departures, transfers, and changes in role affecting CUI access.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Upon termination, all CUI access (logical and physical) is revoked within 24 hours.
b. ASJ Realty property including devices, keys, and media is collected at or before departure.
c. Terminated personnel are briefed on continuing CUI confidentiality obligations.
d. Transfer to a new role triggers access review to ensure alignment with new responsibilities.
e. Termination procedures are documented and tracked via a termination checklist.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Anthony James, Office Manager, initiates termination procedures. Sandra Okonkwo executes logical access revocation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to complete termination procedures within 24 hours is a policy violation.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
