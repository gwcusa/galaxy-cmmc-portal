SECURITY ASSESSMENT POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.12.2 - Plan of Action and Milestones
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to develop and implement plans of action to correct deficiencies and reduce vulnerabilities in CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.12.2.

2. SCOPE
All identified deficiencies in Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI system security controls.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A POA&M is maintained for all identified security control deficiencies.
b. Each POA&M entry includes: finding, remediation plan, responsible party, and milestone dates.
c. POA&M is reviewed and updated quarterly.
d. Completed items are marked closed with evidence of remediation.
e. Keith Merritt approves the POA&M quarterly and accepts residual risk.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, maintains the POA&M. Keith Merritt reviews and approves it quarterly.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Overdue POA&M items without approved extensions are escalated immediately to Keith Merritt.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
