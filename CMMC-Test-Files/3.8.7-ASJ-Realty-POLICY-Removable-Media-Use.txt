MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.8.7 - Removable Media Use Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the use of removable media on system components, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.7.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty CUI endpoints and the removable media used with them.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Only Sandra Okonkwo-approved removable media may be used on CUI endpoints.
b. Approved media must be encrypted (AES-256 hardware or software).
c. Intune device control policies block unapproved USB storage devices.
d. A register of approved removable media is maintained by Sandra Okonkwo.
e. Approved media is inventoried and audited quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, manages the approved media register and Intune device control policies.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved removable media connected to CUI endpoints is blocked and generates a security alert.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
