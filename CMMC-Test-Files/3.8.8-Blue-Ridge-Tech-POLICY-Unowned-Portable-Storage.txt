MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.8.8 - Unowned Portable Storage Prohibition
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy prohibits the use of portable storage devices when such devices have no identifiable owner, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.8.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI endpoints and removable storage found in or around Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Unidentified or ownerless portable storage devices found in Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC facilities are quarantined and reported to Nathan Cruz.
b. Found storage devices are never connected to Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC systems without malware scanning and approval.
c. The approved media register identifies the owner of every authorized device.
d. Personnel are trained not to connect found USB drives or storage devices.
e. Intune device control ensures only allowlisted devices connect to CUI endpoints.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, enforces the no-unowned-media policy and trains personnel.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Connecting an unowned device to a CUI endpoint is a security incident.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
