MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.7.1 - System Maintenance
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Bumpass Fire & Rescue's requirements for performing maintenance on information systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.1.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue hardware and software systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Maintenance activities are scheduled and documented in advance.
b. Security patches are applied within 30 days of release (critical: 72 hours).
c. Maintenance windows are communicated to Randy Bowles before scheduled downtime.
d. Post-maintenance verification confirms system functionality and security settings.
e. All maintenance activities are logged with date, type, and personnel involved.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, schedules and performs all system maintenance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Overdue patches trigger automated alerts and escalation to Randy Bowles after 30 days.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
