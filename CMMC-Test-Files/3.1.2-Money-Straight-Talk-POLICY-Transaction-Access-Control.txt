ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.2 - Transaction and Function Access Limits
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits Money Straight Talk LLC system access to the types of transactions and functions that authorized users are permitted to execute, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.2.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC information systems and users who access CUI or CUI-supporting systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. User accounts are assigned roles that restrict access to only required functions.
b. Role assignments are documented and reviewed semi-annually by Terrell Banks.
c. No user shall have access beyond what is required for their job function.
d. Administrative roles are restricted to personnel with a documented operational need.
e. Role changes require written approval before implementation.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages role assignments. Camille Odom approves elevated access requests.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized function access is treated as a policy violation subject to disciplinary action.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
