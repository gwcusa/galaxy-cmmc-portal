SECURITY ASSESSMENT POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.12.1 - Security Control Assessment
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to periodically assess the security controls in systems to determine their effectiveness, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.12.1.

2. SCOPE
All security controls implemented in Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Security controls are assessed at least annually.
b. Assessment may be self-assessment or conducted by an independent third party.
c. Assessment results are documented in a Security Assessment Report (SAR).
d. Findings from the assessment are tracked to remediation.
e. Assessment results inform updates to the System Security Plan.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, coordinates security assessments. Randy Bowles reviews and accepts the SAR.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Gaps identified in security assessments are added to the POA&M and tracked to closure.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
