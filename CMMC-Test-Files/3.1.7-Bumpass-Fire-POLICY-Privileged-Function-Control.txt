ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.7 - Privileged Function Execution Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy prevents non-privileged users at Bumpass Fire & Rescue from executing privileged functions and captures all privileged function executions in audit logs, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.7.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue information systems and personnel.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Non-privileged accounts are technically blocked from executing privileged functions.
b. All privileged function executions are captured in audit logs.
c. Audit logs of privileged activity are reviewed by Mike Sadler monthly.
d. Attempts by non-privileged users to escalate privileges generate security alerts.
e. Windows UAC and Azure AD RBAC enforce privilege boundaries.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and monitors privileged function controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized privilege escalation attempts are investigated as security incidents.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
