SECURITY ASSESSMENT POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.12.4 - System Security Plan
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to develop, document, and periodically update System Security Plans (SSPs) that describe the system boundary, security requirements, and security controls, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.12.4.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. An SSP is maintained that documents the CUI system boundary, users, and controls.
b. The SSP describes all 110 NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 controls and their implementation status.
c. The SSP is reviewed and updated at least annually.
d. Anthony James approves the SSP.
e. The SSP is protected from unauthorized access.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, authors and maintains the SSP. Anthony James reviews and approves it annually.

5. ENFORCEMENT
An outdated SSP (>1 year old) is treated as a security compliance finding.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
