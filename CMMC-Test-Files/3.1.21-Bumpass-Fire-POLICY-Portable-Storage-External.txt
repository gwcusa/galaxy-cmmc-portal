ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.21 - Portable Storage on External Systems
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits the use of portable storage devices on external systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.21.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue personnel who use portable storage devices (USB drives, external HDDs, SD cards) on non-Bumpass Fire & Rescue systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Bumpass Fire & Rescue-owned portable storage devices may not be connected to external (non-Bumpass Fire & Rescue) systems.
b. Portable storage used on external systems may not subsequently be connected to CUI systems without approval and scanning.
c. Personal portable storage devices are prohibited on Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI endpoints.
d. Intune device control policies block unauthorized USB storage on CUI endpoints.
e. Any exemption requires written approval from Randy Bowles and is logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, enforces USB device control policies. Randy Bowles approves exceptions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Violations of this policy are investigated and may result in disciplinary action.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
