SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.13.15 - Session Authenticity
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to protect the authenticity of communications sessions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.15.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC communications sessions, including email, web sessions, and API connections.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Email authenticity is enforced via SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records.
b. Web sessions use TLS certificates with proper authentication.
c. DMARC policy enforces rejection of spoofed email from blueridgetech.com.
d. DKIM is configured for all outbound email.
e. Email authentication records are reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and maintains email and session authentication controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failed email authentication checks result in mail rejection per DMARC policy.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
