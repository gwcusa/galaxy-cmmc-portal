AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.3.8 - Audit Log Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy protects Keith the Drone Guy LLC audit information and tools from unauthorized access, modification, and deletion, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.8.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC audit records, logging infrastructure, and audit management tools.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logs are stored in a system where operational users cannot delete or modify records.
b. Access to raw audit log data is restricted to Keith Merritt and authorized assessors.
c. Audit log storage uses append-only or immutable storage mechanisms.
d. Backup copies of audit logs are stored separately from production systems.
e. Log access attempts are themselves logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, manages audit log access controls and immutability settings.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any modification or deletion of audit records is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
