MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.8.5 - Media Transport Controls
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the transport of CUI media outside of controlled areas, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.5.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI media transported outside of secure office areas.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI media transport outside of secured areas requires advance approval from Randy Bowles.
b. CUI media in transit is encrypted and kept in physical possession of the transporter.
c. Transport is documented in the media transport log.
d. CUI media is never checked in luggage during air travel.
e. Lost or stolen CUI media is reported to Mike Sadler and Randy Bowles within 1 hour of discovery.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, manages media transport approvals and documentation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved CUI media transport is a security violation.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
