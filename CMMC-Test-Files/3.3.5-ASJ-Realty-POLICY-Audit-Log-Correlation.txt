AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.3.5 - Audit Log Correlation and Analysis
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to correlate audit record review, analysis, and reporting across organizational processes to gain situational awareness, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.5.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty audit log sources and CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit records from multiple sources are correlated in a centralized SIEM.
b. Correlation rules detect multi-source attack patterns.
c. Sandra Okonkwo reviews correlation findings weekly.
d. Cross-source incidents are escalated immediately to Anthony James.
e. Correlation rules are reviewed and updated quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, maintains and monitors the SIEM correlation infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Gaps in log correlation coverage are remediated within 30 days of identification.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
