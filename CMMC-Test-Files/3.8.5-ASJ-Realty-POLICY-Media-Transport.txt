1. PURPOSE
This policy controls access to media containing CUI and
maintains accountability for media during transport.

2. TRANSPORT AUTHORIZATION
  No CUI media may be removed from ASJ Realty premises
  without prior written (email) authorization from
  Sandra Okonkwo.

3. TRANSPORT REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Digital Media
  - All digital media containing CUI transported off-site
    must be encrypted (BitLocker or equivalent)
  - Transport packaging: sealed, tamper-evident bag or case
  - Chain of custody documented (Media Transport Log)
  - Media must be hand-carried by an authorized ASJ Realty
    employee - not shipped via postal service unescorted

3.2 Laptop Transport
  - Laptops with CUI must have BitLocker active before
    being removed from the office
  - Laptops traveling by air: kept in carry-on luggage only
    (not checked baggage)

3.3 Paper CUI
  - Paper CUI transported in sealed, opaque envelope
  - Envelope marked "CUI - DO NOT OPEN - AUTHORIZED RECIPIENT ONLY"
  - Hand-delivered by ASJ Realty staff only

4. ACCOUNTABILITY
  - The Media Transport Log records every off-site movement:
    media ID, contents description, destination, transporter,
    departure time, return/delivery time
  - Lost media during transport must be reported within 1 hour
