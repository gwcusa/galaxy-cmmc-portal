SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.14.3 - Security Alerts and Advisories
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to monitor security alerts and advisories from authoritative sources and take appropriate actions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.3.

2. SCOPE
All security threat intelligence and advisory monitoring for Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Nathan Cruz subscribes to US-CERT/CISA alerts and advisories.
b. Microsoft Security Response Center (MSRC) advisories are monitored for M365 and Windows.
c. Relevant alerts are reviewed within 24 hours of receipt.
d. Emergency/critical advisories trigger immediate response per the Incident Response Plan.
e. Advisory responses are logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, monitors security alerts and coordinates response.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to respond to critical advisories within 24 hours is escalated to Victor Osei.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
