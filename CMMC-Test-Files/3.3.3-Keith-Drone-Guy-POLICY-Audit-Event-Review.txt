AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.3.3 - Audit Event Review, Analysis, and Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to review, analyze, and report audit logs to identify inappropriate or unusual activity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.3.

2. SCOPE
All CUI system audit logs collected by Keith the Drone Guy LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logs are reviewed weekly by Keith Merritt for anomalous activity.
b. Automated correlation rules detect and alert on suspicious patterns.
c. Anomalies are investigated and documented within 24 hours.
d. Monthly audit summary reports are provided to Keith Merritt.
e. Review findings are retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, conducts weekly log reviews and responds to alerts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to review logs per schedule is reported to Keith Merritt for corrective action.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
