AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.2.2 - Insider Threat Awareness Training
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Money Straight Talk LLC trains personnel to recognize and report potential indicators of insider threat, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.2.2.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC personnel with access to CUI systems or facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Insider threat awareness training is completed annually by all personnel.
b. Training covers behavioral indicators, reporting channels, and non-retaliation protections.
c. Personnel are trained on the Money Straight Talk LLC anonymous reporting mechanism.
d. Training records are maintained for 3 years.
e. Camille Odom receives quarterly insider threat awareness briefings.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, administers insider threat training. Camille Odom oversees the insider threat program.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to complete insider threat training results in CUI access suspension.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
