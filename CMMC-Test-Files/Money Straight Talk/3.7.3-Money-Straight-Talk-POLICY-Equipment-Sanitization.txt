MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.7.3 - Equipment Sanitization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to ensure that equipment removed for off-site maintenance is sanitized before release, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.3.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC CUI system equipment that is removed from the facility for repair or maintenance.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All CUI data is removed from equipment before it leaves Money Straight Talk LLC custody.
b. If data cannot be removed, the equipment is escorted and not left unattended.
c. Sanitization follows NIST SP 800-88 Guidelines for Media Sanitization.
d. Sanitization is documented in the equipment maintenance log.
e. Equipment returning from off-site maintenance is inspected and re-scanned before reconnection.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, performs or verifies equipment sanitization before off-site release.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Equipment leaving the facility with CUI without sanitization is a security incident.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
