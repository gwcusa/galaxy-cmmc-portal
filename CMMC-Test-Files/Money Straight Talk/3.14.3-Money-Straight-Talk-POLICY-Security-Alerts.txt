SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.14.3 - Security Alerts and Advisories
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to monitor security alerts and advisories from authoritative sources and take appropriate actions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.3.

2. SCOPE
All security threat intelligence and advisory monitoring for Money Straight Talk LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Terrell Banks subscribes to US-CERT/CISA alerts and advisories.
b. Microsoft Security Response Center (MSRC) advisories are monitored for M365 and Windows.
c. Relevant alerts are reviewed within 24 hours of receipt.
d. Emergency/critical advisories trigger immediate response per the Incident Response Plan.
e. Advisory responses are logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, monitors security alerts and coordinates response.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to respond to critical advisories within 24 hours is escalated to Camille Odom.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
