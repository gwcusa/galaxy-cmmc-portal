ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.9 - Privacy and Security Notices
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to provide privacy and security notices consistent with CUI rules at system logon, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.9.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC information systems accessible by personnel or contractors.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A logon banner displaying privacy and security notices is displayed before authentication.
b. The banner references CUI handling requirements and consent to monitoring.
c. Terrell Banks reviews and updates banner text annually or upon regulatory change.
d. Banner text is approved by Camille Odom before deployment.
e. Banner is enforced via Group Policy and Azure AD Conditional Access.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, implements and maintains logon banners across all systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems without compliant logon banners must be remediated within 48 hours of identification.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
