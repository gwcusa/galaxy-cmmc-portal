AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.3.8 - Audit Log Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy protects Money Straight Talk LLC audit information and tools from unauthorized access, modification, and deletion, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.8.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC audit records, logging infrastructure, and audit management tools.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logs are stored in a system where operational users cannot delete or modify records.
b. Access to raw audit log data is restricted to Terrell Banks and authorized assessors.
c. Audit log storage uses append-only or immutable storage mechanisms.
d. Backup copies of audit logs are stored separately from production systems.
e. Log access attempts are themselves logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages audit log access controls and immutability settings.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any modification or deletion of audit records is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
