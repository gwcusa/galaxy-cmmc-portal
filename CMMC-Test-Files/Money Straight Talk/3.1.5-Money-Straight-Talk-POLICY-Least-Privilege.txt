ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.5 - Principle of Least Privilege
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to employ the principle of least privilege, including for specific security functions and privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.5.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC personnel, contractors, and systems accessing CUI environments.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Users are granted the minimum access necessary to perform their job functions.
b. Privileged accounts are used only for tasks requiring elevated permissions.
c. Terrell Banks reviews all privileged account assignments quarterly.
d. Privileged access is revoked immediately when no longer operationally required.
e. Just-in-time (JIT) privileged access is used where technically feasible.
f. Default system accounts with excessive privileges are disabled or renamed.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, is responsible for implementing and reviewing access rights.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Violations including unauthorized privilege escalation are subject to termination.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
