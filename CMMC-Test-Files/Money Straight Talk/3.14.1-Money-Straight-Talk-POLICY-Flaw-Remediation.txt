SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.14.1 - Flaw Remediation
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to identify, report, and correct information system flaws in a timely manner, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.1.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC CUI systems and software.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All software and operating systems receive security patches per vulnerability SLA.
b. Critical patches (CVSS 9.0+): applied within 72 hours.
c. High patches (CVSS 7.0-8.9): applied within 7 days.
d. Medium/Low: applied within 30-90 days.
e. Patch status is monitored via Intune and reported monthly to Camille Odom.
f. Unpatched systems are isolated from CUI until patched.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages patch deployment. Camille Odom reviews monthly patch reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with critical unpatched vulnerabilities beyond 72 hours are removed from CUI network.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
