1. PURPOSE
This policy prohibits the use of portable storage devices
that have no identifiable owner at MST, reducing the risk
of malware introduction and unauthorized data exfiltration.

2. IDENTIFIABLE OWNER REQUIREMENT
A portable storage device is considered to have an identifiable
owner if it meets ALL of the following:
  a) It appears on the MST Approved Media Inventory
  b) It has a physical label with the asset ID and owner name
  c) It is assigned to a specific authorized MST employee

3. PROHIBITED DEVICES
The following portable storage devices may NOT be used
on any MST information system:
  - USB drives without an MST asset label
  - USB drives found in common areas (lobby, parking lot,
    breakroom, conference rooms, restrooms)
  - USB drives received as promotional gifts or freebies
  - Any storage device of unknown or uncertain origin
  - USB devices brought in by visitors or contractors
    (contractors use DoD SAFE or SharePoint instead)

4. FOUND MEDIA PROCEDURE
  If a staff member finds an unidentified USB device:
    1. Do NOT insert it into any company device
    2. Bring it to Terrell Banks
    3. Terrell will isolate, examine (in sandbox if needed),
       and dispose of the device
    4. Document in the Incident Register

5. TECHNICAL CONTROL
  Intune Device Control blocks all USB devices not in the
  approved device hardware ID list.
