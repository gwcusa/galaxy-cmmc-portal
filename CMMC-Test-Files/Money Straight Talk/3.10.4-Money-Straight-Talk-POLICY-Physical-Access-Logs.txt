PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.10.4 - Physical Access Audit Logging
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to maintain audit logs of physical access to CUI facilities, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.4.

2. SCOPE
All physical access points to Money Straight Talk LLC CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Physical access logs record each entry and exit from CUI areas.
b. Logs include: individual identity, date, time, and access point.
c. Physical access logs are reviewed monthly by Terrell Banks.
d. Anomalous physical access (after hours, weekends, unknown individuals) generates immediate review.
e. Physical access logs are retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, maintains and reviews physical access logs.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized physical access detected in logs is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
