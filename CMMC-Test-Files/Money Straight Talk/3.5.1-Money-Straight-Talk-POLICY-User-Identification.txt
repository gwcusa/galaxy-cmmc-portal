IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.5.1 - User Identification
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Money Straight Talk LLC's requirements for identifying all information system users, processes, and devices, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.1.

2. SCOPE
All users, devices, and processes that access Money Straight Talk LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Every user account is assigned a unique username tied to a specific individual.
b. Every device is assigned a unique device identity registered in the asset inventory.
c. Shared or anonymous accounts are prohibited for CUI system access.
d. Service accounts are documented with a named owner responsible for the account.
e. Terrell Banks maintains and reviews the user and device identity registry quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, maintains user and device identity records.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Use of anonymous or unregistered identities on CUI systems is prohibited.
