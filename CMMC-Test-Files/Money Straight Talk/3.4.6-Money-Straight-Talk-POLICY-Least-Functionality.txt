CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.4.6 - Least Functionality
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to configure information systems to provide only essential capabilities, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.6.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC information systems and devices in the CUI environment.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Systems are configured with only the functions and services required for their mission.
b. Unnecessary services, ports, and applications are disabled or removed.
c. Default accounts and sample applications are disabled before deployment.
d. Terrell Banks audits running services on all CUI systems quarterly.
e. Any service additions require documented approval from Camille Odom.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, configures systems to least functionality standard and maintains service inventories.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized services discovered during audits are disabled within 24 hours.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
