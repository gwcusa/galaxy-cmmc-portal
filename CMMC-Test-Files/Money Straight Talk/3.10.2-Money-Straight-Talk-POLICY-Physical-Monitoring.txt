PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.10.2 - Physical Monitoring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to protect and monitor the physical facility and support infrastructure for CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.2.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC facilities where CUI systems are located.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Security cameras cover all entry and exit points to CUI areas.
b. Camera footage is retained for 30 days.
c. Terrell Banks reviews camera footage weekly or upon any incident report.
d. A security alarm is installed and armed during non-business hours.
e. Alarm events are responded to within 15 minutes by designated personnel.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages the physical monitoring infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any detected physical security breach is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
