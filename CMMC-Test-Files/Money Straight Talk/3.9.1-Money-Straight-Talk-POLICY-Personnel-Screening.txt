PERSONNEL SECURITY POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.9.1 - Personnel Screening
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Money Straight Talk LLC's requirements for screening individuals prior to granting access to CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.9.1.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC employees, contractors, and other personnel who will have access to CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All personnel with CUI access must complete a background check before access is granted.
b. Background check includes: criminal history, employment verification, and reference checks.
c. Results are reviewed and approved by Camille Odom before CUI access is granted.
d. Contractors with CUI access must provide evidence of background screening by their employer.
e. Background check results are retained in a secure personnel file for 5 years.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Camille Odom, CEO, reviews and approves background check results. Terrell Banks grants CUI access only after approval.

5. ENFORCEMENT
No individual may receive CUI access without a completed and approved background check.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
