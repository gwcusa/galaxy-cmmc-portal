MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.8.7 - Removable Media Use Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the use of removable media on system components, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.7.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC CUI endpoints and the removable media used with them.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Only Terrell Banks-approved removable media may be used on CUI endpoints.
b. Approved media must be encrypted (AES-256 hardware or software).
c. Intune device control policies block unapproved USB storage devices.
d. A register of approved removable media is maintained by Terrell Banks.
e. Approved media is inventoried and audited quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages the approved media register and Intune device control policies.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved removable media connected to CUI endpoints is blocked and generates a security alert.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
