IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.5.9 - Temporary Password Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy allows Money Straight Talk LLC to employ temporary passwords with an immediate change requirement upon first use, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.9.

2. SCOPE
All temporary, one-time, or provisioning passwords issued by Money Straight Talk LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Temporary passwords require immediate change upon first login.
b. Temporary passwords expire within 24 hours if not used.
c. Temporary passwords are communicated to users via secure channel (encrypted email or in-person).
d. Terrell Banks logs all temporary password issuances.
e. Accounts with temporary passwords that expire unused are reviewed within 48 hours.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, issues and tracks temporary passwords.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Temporary passwords that are not changed within 24 hours result in account lockout.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
