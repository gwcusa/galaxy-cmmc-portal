ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.16 - Wireless Access Authorization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to authorize wireless access connections prior to allowing them, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.16.

2. SCOPE
All wireless access to Money Straight Talk LLC networks and systems that process or store CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Wireless access points are registered and approved by Terrell Banks before deployment.
b. Only authorized devices may connect to the Money Straight Talk LLC CUI wireless network.
c. MAC address filtering and 802.1X authentication are used for CUI network WiFi.
d. Guest wireless networks are isolated from CUI systems.
e. Unauthorized wireless access points (rogue APs) are detected and blocked.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages wireless infrastructure and authorization processes.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized wireless access points must be removed within 2 hours of detection.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
