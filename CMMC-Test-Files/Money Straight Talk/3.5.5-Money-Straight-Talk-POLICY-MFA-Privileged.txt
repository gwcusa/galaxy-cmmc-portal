IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.5.5 - MFA for Privileged Accounts
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to employ multifactor authentication for network access to privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.5.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC privileged accounts (Global Admin, Security Admin, IT Administrator roles).

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. MFA is required every time a privileged account authenticates for network access.
b. Privileged accounts are enrolled in MFA before being activated.
c. PIM-gated privileged access requires MFA at activation.
d. No persistent privileged sessions are permitted; re-authentication is required.
e. Failed privileged account MFA attempts generate immediate alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, configures and enforces MFA for all privileged accounts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any privileged account session without verified MFA is blocked and alerted.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
