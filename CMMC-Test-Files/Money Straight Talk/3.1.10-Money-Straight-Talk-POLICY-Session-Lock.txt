SESSION LOCK AND SCREEN TIMEOUT POLICY

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires that all company workstations and
laptops automatically lock after a period of inactivity,
using a pattern-hiding display (screen saver or blank screen)
to prevent unauthorized viewing of CUI.

2. REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Inactivity Timeout
  - Workstations: 15 minutes of inactivity triggers lock
  - Laptops: 10 minutes of inactivity triggers lock
  - Servers (console sessions): 5 minutes triggers lock

2.2 Screen Lock Display
  - Upon lock, screen must display a blank screen or
    pattern (not a transparent/see-through saver)
  - The desktop and any open documents must not be visible
  - Password required to unlock

2.3 Password Requirements
  - User must re-authenticate with their domain password
    to unlock a locked session
  - Multi-factor authentication is not required at unlock
    (MFA is required at initial logon per POL-IA-001)

3. ENFORCEMENT
  Enforced via Group Policy Object (MST-Screen-Lock-Policy).
  Users cannot override the timeout setting.

4. EXCEPTIONS
  No exceptions are permitted for CUI-handling workstations.
