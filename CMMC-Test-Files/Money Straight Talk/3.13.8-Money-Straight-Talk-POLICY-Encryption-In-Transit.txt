SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.13.8 - Encryption of CUI in Transit
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to implement cryptographic mechanisms to prevent unauthorized disclosure of CUI during transmission, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.8.

2. SCOPE
All CUI transmitted across Money Straight Talk LLC networks and to/from external parties.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All CUI in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher.
b. Weak protocols (SSL, TLS 1.0, TLS 1.1) and weak cipher suites are disabled.
c. VPN traffic uses AES-256 minimum.
d. Email containing CUI uses TLS enforcement or S/MIME encryption.
e. Encryption configuration is reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, configures and verifies all CUI transmission encryption.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted CUI transmission is a critical security violation.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
