PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.10.3 - Visitor Escort and Monitoring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to escort visitors and monitor visitor activity in CUI areas, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.3.

2. SCOPE
All visitors (non-Money Straight Talk LLC employees) entering CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All visitors are signed in via the visitor log before entering CUI areas.
b. Visitors are escorted by authorized Money Straight Talk LLC personnel at all times in CUI areas.
c. Visitors are never left unattended with CUI systems or documents.
d. Visitor log records name, organization, purpose, escort, entry/exit times.
e. Visitor log is retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Camille Odom or Terrell Banks escorts all visitors in CUI areas. Terrell Banks maintains visitor log.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unescorted visitors in CUI areas are a security incident.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
