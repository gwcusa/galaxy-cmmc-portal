EXERCISE SCENARIO
-------------------------------------------------
Scenario Title: "Phishing to Ransomware"

Description: An employee (Angela Price, simulated) receives a
phishing email appearing to be from a DoD contracting officer.
She clicks a malicious link and unknowingly installs ransomware.
The next morning, three workstations are encrypted and a ransom
note appears on screen. CUI files on the shared drive may be
at risk.

EXERCISE OBJECTIVES
-------------------------------------------------
1. Test activation of the Incident Response Team (IRT)
2. Test containment procedures for ransomware
3. Test CUI breach notification procedures
4. Identify gaps in current IR capability

EXERCISE TIMELINE (Simulated)
-------------------------------------------------

T+0:15   Terrell Banks reviews alert, confirms ransomware
