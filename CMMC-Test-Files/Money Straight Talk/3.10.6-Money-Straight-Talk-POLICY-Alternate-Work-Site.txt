PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.10.6 - Alternate Work Site Security
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes security requirements for Money Straight Talk LLC personnel working at alternate work sites (e.g., home offices), per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.6.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC personnel who work remotely or from alternate sites with access to CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Personnel must sign a remote work agreement before accessing CUI from alternate sites.
b. Remote work environments must include: locked workspace, encrypted devices, VPN use.
c. Printers at alternate sites may not be used to print CUI without explicit approval.
d. CUI documents may not be left visible in unsecured home office areas.
e. Terrell Banks conducts annual remote work security reviews for all remote-capable staff.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, provides remote work security guidance and Camille Odom approves alternate work arrangements.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Personnel working from unsecured alternate sites with CUI are in violation of this policy.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
