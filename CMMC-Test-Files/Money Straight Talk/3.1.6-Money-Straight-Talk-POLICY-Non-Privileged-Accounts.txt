ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.6 - Use of Non-Privileged Accounts
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC personnel to use non-privileged accounts when accessing non-security functions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.6.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC personnel who hold or have access to administrative or privileged accounts.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Users with administrative accounts must maintain separate standard accounts for daily work.
b. Email, web browsing, and productivity applications must be used via non-privileged accounts.
c. Privileged accounts shall not be used to access social media or personal email.
d. Terrell Banks shall enforce dual-account policy through technical controls where feasible.
e. Compliance with this policy is verified during quarterly access reviews.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, maintains dual-account configurations. Camille Odom enforces the policy organizationally.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Using a privileged account for non-administrative tasks is a policy violation subject to corrective action.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
