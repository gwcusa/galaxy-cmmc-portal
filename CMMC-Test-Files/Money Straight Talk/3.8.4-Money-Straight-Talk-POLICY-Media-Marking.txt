MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.8.4 - CUI Media Marking
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to mark media with necessary CUI markings and distribution limitations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.4.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC physical and digital media containing CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Physical media containing CUI is labeled with "CUI" on the media and its container.
b. CUI documents include CUI header/footer markings.
c. Digital files containing CUI include CUI designation in the file name or metadata.
d. Media labels are applied by Terrell Banks before the media is used to store CUI.
e. Terrell Banks verifies CUI markings quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, applies and verifies CUI markings on all CUI media.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unmarked CUI media is a compliance finding requiring immediate remediation.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
