1. PURPOSE
This policy requires that malicious code protection mechanisms
are updated when new releases are available to maintain
effective protection against evolving threats.

2. APPROVED PROTECTION SOFTWARE
  Primary: Microsoft Defender for Endpoint (Plan 2)
  Secondary (email): Microsoft Defender for Office 365

3. UPDATE REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Signature Updates
  - Defender security intelligence updates: automatic
  - Update frequency: Every 2 hours (Defender default)
  - Maximum signature age allowed: 24 hours
  - Devices offline > 24 hours: priority update on reconnection

3.2 Platform and Engine Updates
  - Defender platform updates: deployed via Windows Update
    monthly (Patch Tuesday) or sooner if critical
  - Engine updates: automatic (separate from signature updates)

3.3 Intune Compliance Enforcement
  - Intune compliance policy checks:
    * Defender signature age <= 24 hours (required)
    * Defender real-time protection = ON (required)
    * Non-compliant device: blocked from company resources

4. MONITORING
  - Terrell Banks monitors Defender update status in the
    Defender portal and Intune compliance dashboard weekly
  - Alerts are generated if any device falls behind on updates

5. OUT-OF-DATE RESPONSE
  If a device has signatures older than 24 hours:
    - Intune compliance: NON-COMPLIANT flag
    - Device blocked from M365 access until updated
    - Terrell Banks investigates root cause
