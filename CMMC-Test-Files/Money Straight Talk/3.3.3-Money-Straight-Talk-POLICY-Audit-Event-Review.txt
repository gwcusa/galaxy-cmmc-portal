AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.3.3 - Audit Event Review, Analysis, and Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to review, analyze, and report audit logs to identify inappropriate or unusual activity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.3.

2. SCOPE
All CUI system audit logs collected by Money Straight Talk LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logs are reviewed weekly by Terrell Banks for anomalous activity.
b. Automated correlation rules detect and alert on suspicious patterns.
c. Anomalies are investigated and documented within 24 hours.
d. Monthly audit summary reports are provided to Camille Odom.
e. Review findings are retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, conducts weekly log reviews and responds to alerts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to review logs per schedule is reported to Camille Odom for corrective action.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
