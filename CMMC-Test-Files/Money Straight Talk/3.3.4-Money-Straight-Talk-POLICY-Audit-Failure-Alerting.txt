AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.3.4 - Audit Failure Alerting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to alert personnel in the event of an audit logging failure, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.4.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC audit logging infrastructure and CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Automated alerts notify Terrell Banks if any log source stops sending events for more than 30 minutes.
b. Disk space alerts fire when log storage exceeds 80% capacity.
c. Audit logging failures are treated as priority-1 incidents requiring immediate response.
d. Response to logging failure alerts must begin within 15 minutes.
e. Alert handling and resolution is documented.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, configures and responds to audit failure alerts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unacknowledged audit failure alerts beyond 15 minutes escalate automatically to Camille Odom.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
