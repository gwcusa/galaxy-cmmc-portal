MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.7.2 - Controlled Maintenance
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to provide controls on tools, techniques, mechanisms, and personnel who perform maintenance, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.2.

2. SCOPE
All maintenance activities on Money Straight Talk LLC CUI systems by internal staff or vendors.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Vendor maintenance personnel are pre-approved by Camille Odom before being granted access.
b. Vendor access is escorted by Terrell Banks throughout the maintenance activity.
c. Vendor tools and diagnostic software are inspected before use.
d. Remote vendor access is monitored in real-time by Terrell Banks.
e. All vendor maintenance is logged with contact details, scope, and outcome.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, controls and supervises all maintenance activities.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unsupervised vendor access to CUI systems is prohibited.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
