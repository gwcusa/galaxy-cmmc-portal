ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.1 - Authorized User Access
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits Money Straight Talk LLC information system access to authorized users, processes acting on behalf of authorized users, and authorized devices, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.1.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC systems, personnel, and contractors that process, store, or transmit Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Maintain a current authorized user list for each CUI system.
b. Require documented approval before provisioning any account.
c. Terrell Banks reviews the authorized user list quarterly and removes stale entries.
d. Shared or generic accounts are prohibited without documented exception.
e. Disable accounts within 24 hours of termination or contract expiration.
f. All system access events are logged and monitored.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, is responsible for implementation. Camille Odom, CEO, holds overall accountability.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Policy violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
