SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.13.14 - Voice over IP
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to protect the authenticity of communications sessions for VoIP and other session-based communications, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.14.

2. SCOPE
All VoIP and collaboration communications at Money Straight Talk LLC involving CUI discussion.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. VoIP communications use encrypted channels (TLS/SRTP minimum).
b. Microsoft Teams is the approved VoIP and collaboration platform for CUI discussions.
c. Unapproved VoIP applications may not be used for CUI-related communications.
d. Meeting recordings involving CUI require explicit consent from all participants.
e. Teams meeting settings are configured to prevent unauthorized guest access.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, configures and manages Teams VoIP security settings.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Use of unapproved VoIP for CUI discussions is a policy violation and security incident.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
