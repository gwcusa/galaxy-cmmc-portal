ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.13 - Remote Access Encryption
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to employ cryptographic mechanisms to protect the confidentiality of remote access sessions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.13.

2. SCOPE
All remote access connections to Money Straight Talk LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All remote access sessions use TLS 1.2 or higher encryption.
b. VPN connections use AES-256 encryption minimum.
c. Weak cipher suites (RC4, DES, 3DES, TLS 1.0, TLS 1.1) are disabled.
d. Remote desktop sessions require NLA and TLS encryption.
e. Terrell Banks reviews VPN and TLS cipher configurations quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, configures and maintains encryption settings for remote access.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted remote access connections are blocked and reported.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
