SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.14.1 - Flaw Remediation
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to identify, report, and correct information system flaws in a timely manner, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.1.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems and software.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All software and operating systems receive security patches per vulnerability SLA.
b. Critical patches (CVSS 9.0+): applied within 72 hours.
c. High patches (CVSS 7.0-8.9): applied within 7 days.
d. Medium/Low: applied within 30-90 days.
e. Patch status is monitored via Intune and reported monthly to Victor Osei.
f. Unpatched systems are isolated from CUI until patched.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, manages patch deployment. Victor Osei reviews monthly patch reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with critical unpatched vulnerabilities beyond 72 hours are removed from CUI network.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
