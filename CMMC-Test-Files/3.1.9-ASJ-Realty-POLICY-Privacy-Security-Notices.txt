ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.9 - Privacy and Security Notices
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to provide privacy and security notices consistent with CUI rules at system logon, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.9.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty information systems accessible by personnel or contractors.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A logon banner displaying privacy and security notices is displayed before authentication.
b. The banner references CUI handling requirements and consent to monitoring.
c. Sandra Okonkwo reviews and updates banner text annually or upon regulatory change.
d. Banner text is approved by Anthony James before deployment.
e. Banner is enforced via Group Policy and Azure AD Conditional Access.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, implements and maintains logon banners across all systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems without compliant logon banners must be remediated within 48 hours of identification.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
