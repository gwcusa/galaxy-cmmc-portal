ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.17 - Wireless Access Authentication and Encryption
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to protect wireless access using authentication and encryption, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.17.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue wireless networks that support access to CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI wireless networks use WPA3-Enterprise (WPA2-Enterprise minimum) with 802.1X.
b. Encryption uses AES-CCMP (128-bit minimum).
c. WEP and TKIP are prohibited.
d. Wireless authentication certificates are issued by the Bumpass Fire & Rescue internal PKI.
e. Mike Sadler reviews wireless encryption configurations quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and maintains wireless encryption and authentication.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any wireless network in violation of these standards must be taken offline immediately.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
