ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.20 - External System Connection Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to verify and control connections to external systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.20.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue connections to systems, networks, or cloud services outside of direct organizational control.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. External system connections require a documented Interconnection Security Agreement (ISA).
b. Randy Bowles must approve all new external system connections before establishment.
c. Mike Sadler maintains a register of all approved external connections.
d. External connections are reviewed annually and when operational needs change.
e. Connections to external systems are terminated when no longer operationally required.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, maintains the external connection register. Randy Bowles approves connections.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved external connections are blocked and investigated as security incidents.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
