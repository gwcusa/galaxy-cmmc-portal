MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.7.3 - Equipment Sanitization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to ensure that equipment removed for off-site maintenance is sanitized before release, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.3.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI system equipment that is removed from the facility for repair or maintenance.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All CUI data is removed from equipment before it leaves Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC custody.
b. If data cannot be removed, the equipment is escorted and not left unattended.
c. Sanitization follows NIST SP 800-88 Guidelines for Media Sanitization.
d. Sanitization is documented in the equipment maintenance log.
e. Equipment returning from off-site maintenance is inspected and re-scanned before reconnection.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, performs or verifies equipment sanitization before off-site release.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Equipment leaving the facility with CUI without sanitization is a security incident.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
