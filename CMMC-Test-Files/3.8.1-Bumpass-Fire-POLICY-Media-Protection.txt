1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes controls for physically protecting and
securely storing system media (both paper and digital) that
contains CUI at Bumpass Fire and Rescue.

2. DIGITAL MEDIA PROTECTION

2.1 Servers and Workstations
  - All digital media (hard drives, SSDs) in servers and
    workstations are physically secured within:
    * Server room (locked, keypad access - Mike Sadler only)
    * Locked workstations in secured building (alarm system)
  - No removable drives or SSDs leave the server room without
    logging and authorization

2.2 Portable Storage
  - BFR-issued USB drives (Kingston IronKey - encrypted):
    stored in locked IT cabinet when not in use
  - No unencrypted digital media containing CUI is permitted

2.3 Mobile Devices
  - Company iPhones stored in building or personally held
    by assigned officer at all times

3. PAPER MEDIA PROTECTION
  - Paper documents containing CUI stored in locked
    filing cabinet in the Chief's office (combination lock)
  - Key/combo held by Chief Bowles and Mike Sadler
  - Paper CUI is not left unattended on desks
  - Clean desk policy: CUI papers locked away at end of day

4. INVENTORY
  Mike Sadler maintains a media inventory (SharePoint:
  IT/Media-Inventory.xlsx) of all removable digital media.
  Inventory is reconciled quarterly.
