MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.8.9 - CUI Backup Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to protect the confidentiality of backup CUI at storage locations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.9.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC backup media and backup data storage locations containing CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI backups are encrypted at rest using AES-256 or equivalent.
b. Backup storage locations are access-controlled and logged.
c. Backup media is stored in physically secured locations.
d. Cloud backup storage uses encryption and RBAC equivalent to production systems.
e. Backup encryption keys are protected and backed up separately from data.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, manages CUI backup systems and encryption.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted CUI backups are a critical security finding requiring immediate remediation.
