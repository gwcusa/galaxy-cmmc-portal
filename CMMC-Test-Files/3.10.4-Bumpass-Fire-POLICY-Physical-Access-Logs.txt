PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.10.4 - Physical Access Audit Logging
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to maintain audit logs of physical access to CUI facilities, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.4.

2. SCOPE
All physical access points to Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Physical access logs record each entry and exit from CUI areas.
b. Logs include: individual identity, date, time, and access point.
c. Physical access logs are reviewed monthly by Mike Sadler.
d. Anomalous physical access (after hours, weekends, unknown individuals) generates immediate review.
e. Physical access logs are retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, maintains and reviews physical access logs.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized physical access detected in logs is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
