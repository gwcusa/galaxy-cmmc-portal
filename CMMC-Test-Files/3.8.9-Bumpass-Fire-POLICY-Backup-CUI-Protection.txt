MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.8.9 - CUI Backup Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to protect the confidentiality of backup CUI at storage locations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.9.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue backup media and backup data storage locations containing CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI backups are encrypted at rest using AES-256 or equivalent.
b. Backup storage locations are access-controlled and logged.
c. Backup media is stored in physically secured locations.
d. Cloud backup storage uses encryption and RBAC equivalent to production systems.
e. Backup encryption keys are protected and backed up separately from data.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, manages CUI backup systems and encryption.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted CUI backups are a critical security finding requiring immediate remediation.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
