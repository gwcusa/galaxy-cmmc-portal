AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.3.4 - Audit Failure Alerting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to alert personnel in the event of an audit logging failure, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.4.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC audit logging infrastructure and CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Automated alerts notify Nathan Cruz if any log source stops sending events for more than 30 minutes.
b. Disk space alerts fire when log storage exceeds 80% capacity.
c. Audit logging failures are treated as priority-1 incidents requiring immediate response.
d. Response to logging failure alerts must begin within 15 minutes.
e. Alert handling and resolution is documented.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and responds to audit failure alerts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unacknowledged audit failure alerts beyond 15 minutes escalate automatically to Victor Osei.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
