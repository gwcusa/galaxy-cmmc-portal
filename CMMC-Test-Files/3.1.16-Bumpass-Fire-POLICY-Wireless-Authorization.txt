ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.16 - Wireless Access Authorization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to authorize wireless access connections prior to allowing them, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.16.

2. SCOPE
All wireless access to Bumpass Fire & Rescue networks and systems that process or store CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Wireless access points are registered and approved by Mike Sadler before deployment.
b. Only authorized devices may connect to the Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI wireless network.
c. MAC address filtering and 802.1X authentication are used for CUI network WiFi.
d. Guest wireless networks are isolated from CUI systems.
e. Unauthorized wireless access points (rogue APs) are detected and blocked.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, manages wireless infrastructure and authorization processes.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized wireless access points must be removed within 2 hours of detection.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
