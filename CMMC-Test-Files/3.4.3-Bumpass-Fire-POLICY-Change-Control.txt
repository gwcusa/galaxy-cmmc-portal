CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.4.3 - Configuration Change Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Bumpass Fire & Rescue's requirements for controlling and documenting changes to information system configurations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.3.

2. SCOPE
All changes to hardware, software, and configuration of Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All configuration changes require a documented change request.
b. Changes are reviewed and approved by Randy Bowles before implementation.
c. Emergency changes are documented within 24 hours of implementation.
d. All changes are tested in a non-production environment when feasible.
e. Change records are retained for 3 years.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, submits and implements change requests. Randy Bowles approves changes.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized changes are rolled back and investigated as security incidents.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
