AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.3.9 - Audit Management Access
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits management of audit logging to a subset of privileged users, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.9.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC audit logging infrastructure and SIEM tools.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Management of audit log configuration is restricted to Nathan Cruz only.
b. Standard users have no access to configure, modify, or disable audit logging.
c. Audit management access is reviewed quarterly.
d. Any changes to audit logging configuration require documented justification.
e. Changes to logging configuration generate alerts to Victor Osei.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, is the sole administrator of the audit logging system.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized changes to audit logging configuration are security incidents.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
