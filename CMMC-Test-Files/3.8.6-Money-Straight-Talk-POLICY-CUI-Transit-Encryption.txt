MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.8.6 - CUI Encryption in Transit
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to implement cryptographic mechanisms to protect CUI during transmission, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.6.

2. SCOPE
All CUI transmitted electronically or physically by Money Straight Talk LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI transmitted over networks uses TLS 1.2 or higher encryption.
b. CUI emailed externally uses S/MIME or is protected via encrypted secure file transfer.
c. CUI on portable media uses hardware encryption (AES-256 minimum).
d. Unencrypted transmission of CUI is prohibited.
e. Terrell Banks verifies transmission encryption controls quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, configures and verifies encryption for all CUI transmission methods.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted CUI transmission is a serious policy violation.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
