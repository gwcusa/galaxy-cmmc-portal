IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.5.10 - Cryptographic Password Storage
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to store and transmit only cryptographically protected passwords, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.10.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC systems that store or transmit user passwords.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.
b. Password storage uses strong one-way hashing (bcrypt, PBKDF2, or Argon2).
c. Reversible encryption of passwords is prohibited and technically disabled.
d. Passwords are transmitted only over encrypted channels (TLS 1.2+).
e. Keith Merritt verifies cryptographic password protection settings quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, ensures password storage and transmission uses cryptographic protection.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems storing passwords in plaintext or reversible encryption are immediately remediated.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
