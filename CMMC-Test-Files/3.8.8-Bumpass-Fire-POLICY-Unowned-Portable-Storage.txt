MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.8.8 - Unowned Portable Storage Prohibition
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy prohibits the use of portable storage devices when such devices have no identifiable owner, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.8.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI endpoints and removable storage found in or around Bumpass Fire & Rescue facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Unidentified or ownerless portable storage devices found in Bumpass Fire & Rescue facilities are quarantined and reported to Mike Sadler.
b. Found storage devices are never connected to Bumpass Fire & Rescue systems without malware scanning and approval.
c. The approved media register identifies the owner of every authorized device.
d. Personnel are trained not to connect found USB drives or storage devices.
e. Intune device control ensures only allowlisted devices connect to CUI endpoints.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, enforces the no-unowned-media policy and trains personnel.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Connecting an unowned device to a CUI endpoint is a security incident.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
