ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.3 - CUI Information Flow Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the flow of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) at Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC in accordance with approved authorizations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.3.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC systems, users, and processes that create, receive, transmit, or store CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI shall only flow between systems and users with documented authorization.
b. Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies are configured to block unauthorized CUI exfiltration.
c. CUI transmission to external parties requires approval from Victor Osei.
d. Email DLP rules prevent CUI from being sent to non-blueridgetech.com addresses without encryption.
e. File transfers to removable media are logged and require supervisor approval.
f. Nathan Cruz reviews DLP policy alerts monthly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz configures and monitors DLP controls. Victor Osei approves external CUI sharing.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized CUI flow events are investigated and may result in termination.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
