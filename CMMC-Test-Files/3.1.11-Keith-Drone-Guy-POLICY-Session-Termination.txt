ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.11 - Automatic Session Termination
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to automatically terminate user sessions after a defined condition is met, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.11.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems, remote sessions, and web-based applications.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Interactive sessions terminate after 60 minutes of inactivity.
b. Web application sessions expire after 30 minutes without activity.
c. VPN sessions are terminated after 8 hours regardless of activity.
d. Terminated sessions require full re-authentication including MFA.
e. Session timeout settings are enforced by system configuration, not user preference.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures session timeout policies across all CUI-connected systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems without compliant session termination settings must be remediated within 72 hours.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
