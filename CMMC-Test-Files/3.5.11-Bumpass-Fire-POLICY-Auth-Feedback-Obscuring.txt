IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.5.11 - Authentication Feedback Obscuring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to obscure authentication feedback information to protect it from exploitation, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.11.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI system login interfaces.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Password fields display asterisks or dots instead of the actual characters typed.
b. Login failure messages do not reveal whether the username or password was incorrect.
c. Authentication error messages provide no information that aids guessing.
d. Debug authentication information is never displayed to end users.
e. Mike Sadler reviews authentication interface feedback annually.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, ensures all login interfaces obscure authentication feedback.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any interface that reveals password characters or specific credential errors must be immediately remediated.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
