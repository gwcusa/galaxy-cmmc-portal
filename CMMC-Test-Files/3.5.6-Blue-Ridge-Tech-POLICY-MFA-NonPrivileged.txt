IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.5.6 - MFA for Non-Privileged Accounts
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to employ multifactor authentication for network access to non-privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.6.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC standard (non-privileged) user accounts that access CUI systems over a network.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. MFA is required for all standard user accounts when accessing CUI resources over a network.
b. This includes access to Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Teams, and the corporate VPN.
c. MFA enrollment is mandatory for all staff within 24 hours of account creation.
d. Users who fail to complete MFA enrollment lose network CUI access until enrolled.
e. MFA status is audited monthly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, enforces MFA enrollment and compliance for all standard user accounts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Network access to CUI systems without MFA is technically blocked.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
