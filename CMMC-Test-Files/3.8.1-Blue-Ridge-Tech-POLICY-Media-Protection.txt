MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.8.1 - System Media Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC's requirements for protecting system media containing CUI, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.1.

2. SCOPE
All physical and digital media at Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC that contain or have contained CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI media is stored in locked, access-controlled locations when not in use.
b. Physical media inventory is maintained and reviewed quarterly.
c. Only authorized personnel may access physical CUI media.
d. Digital media (hard drives, USB drives) containing CUI is encrypted.
e. Media is labeled with CUI designation when it contains CUI data.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, maintains the media inventory and enforces physical controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unlabeled or unprotected CUI media is treated as a security finding.
