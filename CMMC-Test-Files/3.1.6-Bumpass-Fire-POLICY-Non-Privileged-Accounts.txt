ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.6 - Use of Non-Privileged Accounts
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue personnel to use non-privileged accounts when accessing non-security functions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.6.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue personnel who hold or have access to administrative or privileged accounts.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Users with administrative accounts must maintain separate standard accounts for daily work.
b. Email, web browsing, and productivity applications must be used via non-privileged accounts.
c. Privileged accounts shall not be used to access social media or personal email.
d. Mike Sadler shall enforce dual-account policy through technical controls where feasible.
e. Compliance with this policy is verified during quarterly access reviews.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, maintains dual-account configurations. Randy Bowles enforces the policy organizationally.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Using a privileged account for non-administrative tasks is a policy violation subject to corrective action.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
