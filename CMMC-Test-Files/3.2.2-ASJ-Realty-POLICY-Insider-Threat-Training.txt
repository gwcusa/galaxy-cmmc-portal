AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.2.2 - Insider Threat Awareness Training
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures ASJ Realty trains personnel to recognize and report potential indicators of insider threat, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.2.2.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty personnel with access to CUI systems or facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Insider threat awareness training is completed annually by all personnel.
b. Training covers behavioral indicators, reporting channels, and non-retaliation protections.
c. Personnel are trained on the ASJ Realty anonymous reporting mechanism.
d. Training records are maintained for 3 years.
e. Anthony James receives quarterly insider threat awareness briefings.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, administers insider threat training. Anthony James oversees the insider threat program.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to complete insider threat training results in CUI access suspension.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
