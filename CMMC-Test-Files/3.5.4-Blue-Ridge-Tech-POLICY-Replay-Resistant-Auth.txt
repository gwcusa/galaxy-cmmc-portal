IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.5.4 - Replay-Resistant Authentication
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to employ replay-resistant authentication mechanisms, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.4.

2. SCOPE
All authentication mechanisms used to access Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Authentication protocols use challenge-response or cryptographic nonce mechanisms.
b. Kerberos v5 is used for domain authentication (replay protection via timestamps and nonces).
c. Azure AD authentication uses OAuth 2.0 tokens with expiration and nonce.
d. NTLM v1 is disabled; NTLMv2 may be used only where Kerberos is not supported.
e. Authentication tokens are short-lived and cannot be replayed after expiration.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures authentication protocols with replay resistance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Authentication protocols without replay protection are prohibited.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
