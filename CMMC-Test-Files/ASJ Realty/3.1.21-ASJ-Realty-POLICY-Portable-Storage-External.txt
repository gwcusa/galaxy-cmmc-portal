ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.21 - Portable Storage on External Systems
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits the use of portable storage devices on external systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.21.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty personnel who use portable storage devices (USB drives, external HDDs, SD cards) on non-ASJ Realty systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. ASJ Realty-owned portable storage devices may not be connected to external (non-ASJ Realty) systems.
b. Portable storage used on external systems may not subsequently be connected to CUI systems without approval and scanning.
c. Personal portable storage devices are prohibited on ASJ Realty CUI endpoints.
d. Intune device control policies block unauthorized USB storage on CUI endpoints.
e. Any exemption requires written approval from Anthony James and is logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, enforces USB device control policies. Anthony James approves exceptions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Violations of this policy are investigated and may result in disciplinary action.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
