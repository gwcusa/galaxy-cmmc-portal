1. PURPOSE
This policy requires that maintenance activities performed by
personnel without required access authorization are supervised
to protect CUI and system integrity.

2. SCOPE
Applies to any maintenance activity performed by:
  - External contractors and vendors
  - Any third-party technician
  - Any individual who has not been granted general access
    to ASJR systems and facilities

3. SUPERVISION REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Physical Maintenance
  - External technicians performing hardware work must be
    escorted by Sandra Okonkwo (or Anthony James if Sandra
    is unavailable) from the moment they arrive
  - The escort does not need to be a technical expert but
    must maintain visual oversight of all technician actions
  - No unescorted access to server rooms, network equipment
    areas, or workstations holding CUI

3.2 Software / Remote Maintenance
  - Remote maintenance sessions by external parties are
    monitored by Sandra Okonkwo in real time
  - Sandra must be able to terminate the remote session
    immediately if unauthorized activity is observed

3.3 Cleared Maintenance Personnel
  If maintenance is performed by a vendor whose personnel
  have appropriate background checks and have signed an NDA
  with ASJ Realty, supervision may be reduced to periodic
  check-ins (every 15 minutes) rather than continuous oversight.

4. DOCUMENTATION
  All external maintenance visits are logged with: vendor,
  technician name, escorting staff member, time in/out.
