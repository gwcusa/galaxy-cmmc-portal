PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.10.5 - Physical Access Device Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to control and manage physical access devices (keys, key cards, PINs), per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.5.

2. SCOPE
All physical access devices used to access ASJ Realty CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A register of all physical access devices (keys, PINs, badges) is maintained.
b. Physical access devices are issued only to authorized personnel.
c. Lost or stolen access devices are reported and deactivated within 2 hours.
d. Access PINs are changed quarterly or upon compromise.
e. Access devices are collected upon personnel termination.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Anthony James, Office Manager, approves access device issuance. Sandra Okonkwo maintains the access device register.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unreported loss of physical access devices is a policy violation.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
