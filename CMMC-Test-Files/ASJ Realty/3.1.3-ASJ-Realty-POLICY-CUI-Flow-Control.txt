ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.3 - CUI Information Flow Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the flow of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) at ASJ Realty in accordance with approved authorizations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.3.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty systems, users, and processes that create, receive, transmit, or store CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI shall only flow between systems and users with documented authorization.
b. Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies are configured to block unauthorized CUI exfiltration.
c. CUI transmission to external parties requires approval from Anthony James.
d. Email DLP rules prevent CUI from being sent to non-asjrealty.com addresses without encryption.
e. File transfers to removable media are logged and require supervisor approval.
f. Sandra Okonkwo reviews DLP policy alerts monthly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo configures and monitors DLP controls. Anthony James approves external CUI sharing.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized CUI flow events are investigated and may result in termination.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
