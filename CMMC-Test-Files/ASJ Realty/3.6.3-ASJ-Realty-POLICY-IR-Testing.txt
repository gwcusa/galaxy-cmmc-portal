INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.6.3 - Incident Response Testing
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to test incident response capabilities to determine their effectiveness, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.3.

2. SCOPE
The ASJ Realty Incident Response Plan and all personnel with IR responsibilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. An IR tabletop exercise is conducted annually.
b. The exercise tests at least one realistic threat scenario relevant to ASJ Realty's environment.
c. Exercise results are documented and reviewed by Anthony James.
d. Identified gaps are tracked in a remediation plan.
e. Remediation items are completed within 90 days of the exercise.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, designs and facilitates IR exercises. Anthony James participates and approves results.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Annual IR testing is mandatory; failure to test results in a security program finding.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
