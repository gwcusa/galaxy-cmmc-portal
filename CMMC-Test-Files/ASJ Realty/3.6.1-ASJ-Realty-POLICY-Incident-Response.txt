INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.6.1 - Incident Response Capability
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes ASJ Realty's capability to handle information security incidents, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.1.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty information systems and personnel.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. ASJ Realty maintains a documented Incident Response Plan (IRP).
b. The IRP defines roles, responsibilities, and procedures for incident handling.
c. Sandra Okonkwo is the primary incident responder; Anthony James provides management escalation.
d. The IRP is tested annually via tabletop exercise.
e. Incident response contacts include US-CERT reporting capability for CUI-related incidents.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, leads incident response. Anthony James approves the IRP and provides executive escalation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
All personnel must report suspected security incidents to Sandra Okonkwo within 1 hour of discovery.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
