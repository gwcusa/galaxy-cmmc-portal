PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.10.6 - Alternate Work Site Security
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes security requirements for ASJ Realty personnel working at alternate work sites (e.g., home offices), per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.6.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty personnel who work remotely or from alternate sites with access to CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Personnel must sign a remote work agreement before accessing CUI from alternate sites.
b. Remote work environments must include: locked workspace, encrypted devices, VPN use.
c. Printers at alternate sites may not be used to print CUI without explicit approval.
d. CUI documents may not be left visible in unsecured home office areas.
e. Sandra Okonkwo conducts annual remote work security reviews for all remote-capable staff.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, provides remote work security guidance and Anthony James approves alternate work arrangements.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Personnel working from unsecured alternate sites with CUI are in violation of this policy.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
