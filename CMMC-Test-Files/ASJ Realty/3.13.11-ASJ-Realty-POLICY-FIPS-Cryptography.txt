SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.13.11 - FIPS-Validated Cryptography
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to employ FIPS-validated cryptography when protecting CUI, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.11.

2. SCOPE
All cryptographic functions protecting CUI at ASJ Realty.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. FIPS 140-2 mode is enabled on all endpoints processing CUI.
b. Only FIPS-validated cryptographic modules are used for CUI protection.
c. Non-FIPS algorithms (MD5, RC4, DES, 3DES, SHA-1) are prohibited for CUI protection.
d. FIPS mode is enforced via Group Policy.
e. FIPS compliance is verified quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, enables and maintains FIPS mode on all CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with FIPS mode disabled must be remediated within 24 hours.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
