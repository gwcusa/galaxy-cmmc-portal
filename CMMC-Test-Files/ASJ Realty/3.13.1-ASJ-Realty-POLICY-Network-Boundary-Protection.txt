SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.13.1 - Network Boundary Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to monitor, control, and protect communications at external and internal network boundaries, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.1.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty network boundaries separating CUI systems from external networks.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Firewalls protect all network boundaries between CUI systems and external networks.
b. Intrusion Prevention System (IPS) monitors traffic at network boundaries.
c. Traffic is logged and reviewed for anomalies.
d. Default-deny rules are in place; only approved traffic is allowed.
e. Firewall rules are reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, manages all boundary protection devices and configurations.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized network boundary changes are security incidents requiring immediate investigation.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
