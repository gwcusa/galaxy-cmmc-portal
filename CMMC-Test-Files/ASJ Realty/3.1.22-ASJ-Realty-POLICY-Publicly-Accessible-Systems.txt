1. PURPOSE
This policy controls CUI posted or processed on publicly
accessible information systems (websites, public portals,
social media, public listing platforms).

2. POLICY STATEMENT
  CUI must NOT be posted, published, or processed on any
  publicly accessible system without review and authorization.

3. ASJ REALTY PUBLIC SYSTEMS
  The following systems are considered publicly accessible:
    - ASJ Realty website (www.asjrealty.com)
    - Zillow, Realtor.com, and MLS listing platforms
    - Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn company pages
    - Yelp and Google Business Profile

4. CUI PROHIBITION ON PUBLIC SYSTEMS
  The following information must NEVER appear on public systems:
    - Contract details or CUI from DoD/federal clients
    - Personnel information beyond names/roles
    - Security configurations or network information
    - Client PII beyond what clients have consented to publish
    - Any data marked as CUI, FOUO, or Sensitive

5. REVIEW PROCESS
  - Any content posted to public platforms must be reviewed by
    Sandra Okonkwo before posting if it involves contract info
  - Annual review of all public-facing content for accidental
    CUI disclosure (Sandra Okonkwo, each January)
  - Employees are trained that CUI cannot appear in listing
    descriptions, social media posts, or website content

6. INCIDENT RESPONSE
  If CUI is discovered on a public platform:
    - Remove content immediately
    - Report to Anthony James within 1 hour
    - Document incident per POL-IR-001
