CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.4.7 - Restrict Software Functions
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy restricts, disables, or prevents the use of programs, functions, ports, protocols, and services not required at ASJ Realty, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.7.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty CUI systems, endpoints, and network devices.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Firewall rules block all ports and protocols not required for business operations.
b. Software restriction policies prevent execution of unapproved applications.
c. Removable media autorun is disabled on all endpoints.
d. Macro execution in Office documents is restricted to digitally signed macros only.
e. Sandra Okonkwo reviews firewall rules and software restriction policies quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, configures and maintains software and network function restrictions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized software functions discovered in audits are disabled within 24 hours.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
