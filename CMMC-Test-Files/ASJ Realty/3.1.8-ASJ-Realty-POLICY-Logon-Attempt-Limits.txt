ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.8 - Unsuccessful Logon Attempt Limits
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits unsuccessful logon attempts at ASJ Realty to protect against brute-force attacks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.8.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty systems and devices that authenticate users with CUI system access.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed logon attempts.
b. Lockout duration is 30 minutes or until manually unlocked by Sandra Okonkwo.
c. Account lockout thresholds are enforced via Group Policy and Azure AD.
d. Lockout events generate a security alert reviewed by Sandra Okonkwo.
e. Failed logon attempts are retained in audit logs for 90 days.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, configures and monitors account lockout settings.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Tampering with lockout configurations is a security policy violation.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
