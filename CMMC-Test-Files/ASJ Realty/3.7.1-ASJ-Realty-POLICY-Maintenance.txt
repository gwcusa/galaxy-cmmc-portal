1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes requirements for performing maintenance
on ASJ Realty information systems in a controlled manner.

2. TYPES OF MAINTENANCE

2.1 Scheduled Maintenance
  - Software patching: Second Tuesday of each month (Patch Tuesday)
    Patches applied via Microsoft Intune/Windows Update for Business
  - Hardware inspection: Quarterly (Sandra Okonkwo)
  - Antivirus signature updates: Automatic (daily)
  - Firmware updates: As released, within 30 days for critical
    patches and 90 days for non-critical

2.2 Unscheduled/Emergency Maintenance
  - Emergency patches (zero-day or critical severity): within 72 hours
  - Hardware failures: addressed within 24 hours for systems
    holding CUI

3. MAINTENANCE RECORDS
  All maintenance activities are logged in the IT Maintenance Log
  (SharePoint: IT/Maintenance-Log.xlsx) including:
    - Date and time
    - Description of work performed
    - Systems affected
    - Personnel performing maintenance
    - Outcome

4. PATCH MANAGEMENT
  Patches are tested on a non-production device before broad
  deployment when possible. Emergency patches may be deployed
  directly to production with rollback plan documented.

5. VENDOR MAINTENANCE
  Third-party maintenance visits require prior approval from
  Sandra Okonkwo and are escorted at all times.
