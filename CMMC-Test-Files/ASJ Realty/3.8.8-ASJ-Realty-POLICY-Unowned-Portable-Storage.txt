MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.8.8 - Unowned Portable Storage Prohibition
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy prohibits the use of portable storage devices when such devices have no identifiable owner, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.8.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty CUI endpoints and removable storage found in or around ASJ Realty facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Unidentified or ownerless portable storage devices found in ASJ Realty facilities are quarantined and reported to Sandra Okonkwo.
b. Found storage devices are never connected to ASJ Realty systems without malware scanning and approval.
c. The approved media register identifies the owner of every authorized device.
d. Personnel are trained not to connect found USB drives or storage devices.
e. Intune device control ensures only allowlisted devices connect to CUI endpoints.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, enforces the no-unowned-media policy and trains personnel.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Connecting an unowned device to a CUI endpoint is a security incident.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
