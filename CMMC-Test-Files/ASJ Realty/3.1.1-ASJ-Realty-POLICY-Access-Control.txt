1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes requirements for controlling access to ASJ
Realty Group information systems, networks, and data — including
all Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) related to federal
real estate transactions and DoD housing contracts.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all ASJ Realty employees, contractors,
part-time agents, and third-party vendors who access company
systems, including the MLS platform, contract management system,
and internal file servers.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS

3.1 System Access Authorization
- Access to all ASJ Realty systems requires prior written
  authorization from the IT Director and the employee's
  direct supervisor.
- A completed User Access Request Form (FORM-IT-007) must be
  submitted before any account is created.
- Access is granted on a need-to-know basis only.

3.2 User Account Requirements
- Every individual receives a unique username in the format:
  firstname.lastname@asjrealty.com
- Shared accounts are strictly prohibited.
- Service accounts must be documented and approved annually.

3.3 Authorized Users
- Only employees appearing on the Active Roster maintained
  by Human Resources are eligible for system access.
- Contract agents receive limited access profiles restricted
  to listing tools only.
- All access is reviewed quarterly by the IT Director.

3.4 Unauthorized Access
- Any attempt to access systems without proper authorization
  is grounds for immediate termination and possible legal action.
- Suspicious access attempts must be reported to IT within 1 hour.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES
- IT Director: Approve access requests, maintain user directory
- HR Manager: Notify IT of new hires, transfers, and terminations
  within 24 hours
- Employees: Report any unauthorized access attempts immediately
- Supervisors: Approve access requests for direct reports

5. ENFORCEMENT
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to
and including termination and referral to law enforcement.

6. REVIEW
This policy is reviewed annually or following any significant
security incident. Last reviewed: January 10, 2026.
