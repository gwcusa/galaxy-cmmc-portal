MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.8.6 - CUI Encryption in Transit
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to implement cryptographic mechanisms to protect CUI during transmission, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.6.

2. SCOPE
All CUI transmitted electronically or physically by ASJ Realty.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI transmitted over networks uses TLS 1.2 or higher encryption.
b. CUI emailed externally uses S/MIME or is protected via encrypted secure file transfer.
c. CUI on portable media uses hardware encryption (AES-256 minimum).
d. Unencrypted transmission of CUI is prohibited.
e. Sandra Okonkwo verifies transmission encryption controls quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, configures and verifies encryption for all CUI transmission methods.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted CUI transmission is a serious policy violation.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
