ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.20 - External System Connection Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to verify and control connections to external systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.20.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty connections to systems, networks, or cloud services outside of direct organizational control.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. External system connections require a documented Interconnection Security Agreement (ISA).
b. Anthony James must approve all new external system connections before establishment.
c. Sandra Okonkwo maintains a register of all approved external connections.
d. External connections are reviewed annually and when operational needs change.
e. Connections to external systems are terminated when no longer operationally required.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, maintains the external connection register. Anthony James approves connections.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved external connections are blocked and investigated as security incidents.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
