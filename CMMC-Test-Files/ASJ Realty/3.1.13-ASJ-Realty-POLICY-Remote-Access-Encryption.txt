ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.13 - Remote Access Encryption
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to employ cryptographic mechanisms to protect the confidentiality of remote access sessions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.13.

2. SCOPE
All remote access connections to ASJ Realty CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All remote access sessions use TLS 1.2 or higher encryption.
b. VPN connections use AES-256 encryption minimum.
c. Weak cipher suites (RC4, DES, 3DES, TLS 1.0, TLS 1.1) are disabled.
d. Remote desktop sessions require NLA and TLS encryption.
e. Sandra Okonkwo reviews VPN and TLS cipher configurations quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, configures and maintains encryption settings for remote access.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted remote access connections are blocked and reported.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
