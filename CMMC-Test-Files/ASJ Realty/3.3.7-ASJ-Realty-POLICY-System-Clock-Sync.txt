AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.3.7 - System Clock Synchronization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to provide a system capability that compares and synchronizes internal system clocks with an authoritative source, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.7.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty information systems that generate audit records.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All systems synchronize clocks with an authoritative NTP time source.
b. NTP source: time.windows.com or NIST time servers (time.nist.gov).
c. Maximum clock drift is 1 second from authoritative source.
d. NTP configuration is enforced via Group Policy on Windows endpoints.
e. Sandra Okonkwo verifies clock synchronization quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, configures and verifies NTP synchronization on all systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with clock drift exceeding 5 minutes are quarantined from CUI access.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
