MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.8.2 - CUI Media Access Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits access to CUI on digital and physical media to authorized users, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.2.

2. SCOPE
All digital and physical media containing CUI at ASJ Realty.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Access to CUI media is restricted to personnel with documented CUI access authorization.
b. File and share permissions restrict CUI access to authorized accounts only.
c. CUI network shares require authentication and are not publicly accessible.
d. Physical CUI media is stored in locked locations inaccessible to unauthorized personnel.
e. Access to CUI media is logged and reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, configures access permissions for digital CUI media.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized access to CUI media is a security incident requiring immediate investigation.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
