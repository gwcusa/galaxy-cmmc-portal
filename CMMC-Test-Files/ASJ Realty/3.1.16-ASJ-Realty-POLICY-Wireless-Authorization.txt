ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.16 - Wireless Access Authorization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to authorize wireless access connections prior to allowing them, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.16.

2. SCOPE
All wireless access to ASJ Realty networks and systems that process or store CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Wireless access points are registered and approved by Sandra Okonkwo before deployment.
b. Only authorized devices may connect to the ASJ Realty CUI wireless network.
c. MAC address filtering and 802.1X authentication are used for CUI network WiFi.
d. Guest wireless networks are isolated from CUI systems.
e. Unauthorized wireless access points (rogue APs) are detected and blocked.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, manages wireless infrastructure and authorization processes.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized wireless access points must be removed within 2 hours of detection.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
