MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.8.1 - System Media Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes ASJ Realty's requirements for protecting system media containing CUI, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.1.

2. SCOPE
All physical and digital media at ASJ Realty that contain or have contained CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI media is stored in locked, access-controlled locations when not in use.
b. Physical media inventory is maintained and reviewed quarterly.
c. Only authorized personnel may access physical CUI media.
d. Digital media (hard drives, USB drives) containing CUI is encrypted.
e. Media is labeled with CUI designation when it contains CUI data.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, maintains the media inventory and enforces physical controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unlabeled or unprotected CUI media is treated as a security finding.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
