SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.13.7 - Split Tunneling Prohibition
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy prevents ASJ Realty remote access VPN clients from using split tunneling, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.7.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty remote access VPN connections.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Split tunneling is disabled on all VPN profiles.
b. When VPN is connected, all traffic (including internet) routes through the ASJ Realty gateway.
c. VPN configuration is enforced by managed Intune profiles and cannot be changed by users.
d. Sandra Okonkwo verifies split tunneling is disabled quarterly.
e. Any detected split tunnel configuration is immediately remediated.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, configures VPN profiles with split tunneling disabled.

5. ENFORCEMENT
VPN connections with split tunneling enabled are disconnected and reconfigured.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
