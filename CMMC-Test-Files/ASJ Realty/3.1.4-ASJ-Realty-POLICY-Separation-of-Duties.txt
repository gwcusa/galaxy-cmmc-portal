ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.4 - Separation of Duties
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy separates duties of individuals at ASJ Realty to reduce the risk of malevolent activity without collusion, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.4.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty personnel with access to CUI systems or privileged administrative functions.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. No single individual shall control all phases of a critical business or IT operation.
b. Account creation/approval and account provisioning roles are assigned to different individuals.
c. Sandra Okonkwo administers accounts; Anthony James approves new privileged access requests.
d. System audit log access is restricted to personnel independent of daily operations.
e. Dual authorization is required for deletion of CUI data.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo implements technical controls. Anthony James ensures organizational enforcement.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Deliberate circumvention of separation-of-duties controls is grounds for immediate termination.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
