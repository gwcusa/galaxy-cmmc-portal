ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.18 - Mobile Device Connection Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the connection of mobile devices to ASJ Realty systems and CUI networks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.18.

2. SCOPE
All mobile devices (smartphones, tablets, laptops) connecting to ASJ Realty CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All mobile devices connecting to ASJ Realty CUI resources must be enrolled in Intune MDM.
b. Non-enrolled personal devices may not access CUI systems or networks.
c. Mobile devices must meet compliance requirements (encrypted, PIN-locked, current OS).
d. Sandra Okonkwo approves mobile device enrollment requests from staff.
e. Lost or stolen devices are remotely wiped within 4 hours of report.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, manages the MDM solution and mobile device compliance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unenrolled mobile devices detected on CUI networks are blocked and reported.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
