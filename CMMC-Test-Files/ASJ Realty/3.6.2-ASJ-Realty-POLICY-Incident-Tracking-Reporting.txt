INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.6.2 - Incident Tracking and Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to track, document, and report incidents to appropriate officials, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.2.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty security incidents involving CUI systems or data.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All incidents are tracked in the IT helpdesk ticketing system.
b. Incidents are classified by severity (Low/Medium/High/Critical).
c. High and Critical incidents are reported to Anthony James within 1 hour.
d. CUI-related incidents are reported to DoD/US-CERT as required.
e. Incident records are retained for 3 years.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, tracks and documents all incidents. Anthony James receives escalation reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to report a known incident is a policy violation subject to disciplinary action.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
