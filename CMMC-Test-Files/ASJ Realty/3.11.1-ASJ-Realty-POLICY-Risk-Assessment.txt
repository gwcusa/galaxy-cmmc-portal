RISK ASSESSMENT POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.11.1 - Risk Assessment
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to periodically assess the risk to organizational operations from the operation of information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.11.1.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty information systems and processes involving CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A formal risk assessment is conducted at least annually.
b. The risk assessment identifies threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and impact.
c. Risks are documented in a Risk Register maintained by Sandra Okonkwo.
d. Anthony James reviews and approves the risk assessment findings.
e. Risk assessment results drive remediation priorities.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, conducts the risk assessment. Anthony James reviews and approves results.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to complete annual risk assessment is reported to Anthony James as a compliance gap.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
