SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.14.3 - Security Alerts and Advisories
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to monitor security alerts and advisories from authoritative sources and take appropriate actions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.3.

2. SCOPE
All security threat intelligence and advisory monitoring for ASJ Realty CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Sandra Okonkwo subscribes to US-CERT/CISA alerts and advisories.
b. Microsoft Security Response Center (MSRC) advisories are monitored for M365 and Windows.
c. Relevant alerts are reviewed within 24 hours of receipt.
d. Emergency/critical advisories trigger immediate response per the Incident Response Plan.
e. Advisory responses are logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, monitors security alerts and coordinates response.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to respond to critical advisories within 24 hours is escalated to Anthony James.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
