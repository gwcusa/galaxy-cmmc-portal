1. PURPOSE
This policy requires that individuals are screened prior to
being authorized access to ASJ Realty systems containing CUI.

2. SCREENING REQUIREMENTS
All personnel who will access CUI systems must undergo:
  a) Criminal background check (nationwide)
  b) Identity verification (government-issued ID)
  c) Reference check (minimum 2 professional references)
  d) Employment history verification (last 5 years)

3. TIMING
  - Screening is completed BEFORE granting access to CUI
  - New hires may access non-CUI systems during the screening
    period, but access to CUI systems is withheld until
    screening is complete and results reviewed by Anthony James

4. SCREENING PROVIDER
  ASJ Realty uses HireRight (third-party background check
  service) for all employment screening. Results are shared
  only with Anthony James and Sandra Okonkwo.

5. ADVERSE RESULTS
  - Any adverse finding is reviewed by Anthony James
  - A determination is made case-by-case based on the
    nature, recency, and relevance of the adverse finding
  - Access to CUI is not granted until Anthony James
    approves after reviewing adverse results

6. CONTRACTORS AND VENDORS
  Third-party contractors who require regular unsupervised
  access to CUI systems must undergo equivalent screening.
  Contractors under supervision (short-term) require ID
  verification only.

7. RECORDS
  Screening records retained per HireRight for 7 years.
  Internal results summary retained in HR files (locked).
