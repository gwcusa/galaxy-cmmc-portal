SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.13.10 - Cryptographic Key Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to establish and manage cryptographic keys for required cryptography, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.10.

2. SCOPE
All cryptographic keys used in Money Straight Talk LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Cryptographic keys are generated, stored, and managed in Azure Key Vault.
b. Keys are rotated at least annually or upon personnel departure.
c. Access to Key Vault is restricted to Terrell Banks and specific service principals.
d. Key operations are logged and audited.
e. Compromised keys are immediately revoked and replaced.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages all cryptographic keys for CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unmanaged or expired keys are a critical finding requiring immediate remediation.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
