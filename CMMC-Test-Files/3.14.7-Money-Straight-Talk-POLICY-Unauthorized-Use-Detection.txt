SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.14.7 - Unauthorized Use Detection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to identify unauthorized use of CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.7.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC CUI systems and user accounts.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Microsoft Sentinel and Defender detect unauthorized or anomalous system use.
b. User behavior analytics (UBA) identifies deviations from normal usage patterns.
c. System banners and acceptable use notices inform users of monitoring.
d. Reports of unauthorized use are investigated within 4 hours.
e. Confirmed unauthorized use triggers immediate account suspension and incident response.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, identifies and investigates unauthorized use of CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Confirmed unauthorized use results in immediate account lock and escalation to Camille Odom and legal counsel.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
