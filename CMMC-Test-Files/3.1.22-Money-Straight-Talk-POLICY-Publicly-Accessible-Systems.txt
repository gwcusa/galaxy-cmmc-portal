ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.22 - CUI on Publicly Accessible Systems
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls CUI posted or processed on publicly accessible Money Straight Talk LLC systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.22.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC publicly accessible systems including websites, social media, and public-facing portals.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI is prohibited from being posted on any publicly accessible system.
b. Camille Odom must review and approve all content before it is published publicly.
c. Terrell Banks conducts quarterly scans of public-facing systems for inadvertent CUI exposure.
d. Discovered CUI on public systems is removed within 2 hours and reported to management.
e. Staff are trained annually on CUI handling and prohibition of public posting.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks scans public systems. Camille Odom approves public content. All staff are responsible for not posting CUI.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Posting CUI to publicly accessible systems is a serious policy violation subject to immediate investigation.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
