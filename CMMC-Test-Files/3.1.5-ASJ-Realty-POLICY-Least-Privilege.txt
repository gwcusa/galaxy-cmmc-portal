ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.5 - Principle of Least Privilege
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to employ the principle of least privilege, including for specific security functions and privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.5.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty personnel, contractors, and systems accessing CUI environments.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Users are granted the minimum access necessary to perform their job functions.
b. Privileged accounts are used only for tasks requiring elevated permissions.
c. Sandra Okonkwo reviews all privileged account assignments quarterly.
d. Privileged access is revoked immediately when no longer operationally required.
e. Just-in-time (JIT) privileged access is used where technically feasible.
f. Default system accounts with excessive privileges are disabled or renamed.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, is responsible for implementing and reviewing access rights.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Violations including unauthorized privilege escalation are subject to termination.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
