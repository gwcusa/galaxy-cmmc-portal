PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.10.2 - Physical Monitoring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to protect and monitor the physical facility and support infrastructure for CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.2.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue facilities where CUI systems are located.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Security cameras cover all entry and exit points to CUI areas.
b. Camera footage is retained for 30 days.
c. Mike Sadler reviews camera footage weekly or upon any incident report.
d. A security alarm is installed and armed during non-business hours.
e. Alarm events are responded to within 15 minutes by designated personnel.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, manages the physical monitoring infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any detected physical security breach is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
