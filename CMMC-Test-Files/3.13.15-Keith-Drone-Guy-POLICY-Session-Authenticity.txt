SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.13.15 - Session Authenticity
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to protect the authenticity of communications sessions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.15.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC communications sessions, including email, web sessions, and API connections.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Email authenticity is enforced via SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records.
b. Web sessions use TLS certificates with proper authentication.
c. DMARC policy enforces rejection of spoofed email from keithdroney.com.
d. DKIM is configured for all outbound email.
e. Email authentication records are reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures and maintains email and session authentication controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failed email authentication checks result in mail rejection per DMARC policy.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
