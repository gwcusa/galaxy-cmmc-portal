1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures that equipment sent off-site for maintenance
or repair is sanitized of CUI and sensitive data before departure.

2. SANITIZATION REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Before Off-Site Maintenance
  Before any device is sent off-site (to a repair facility,
  vendor, or shipping for manufacturer warranty repair):
    a) All CUI and sensitive data must be removed or sanitized
    b) Keith Merritt reviews the device to confirm CUI removal
    c) Sanitization must use NIST SP 800-88 approved methods:
       - For SSDs/Flash storage: Cryptographic Erase (preferred)
         using BitLocker key destruction or secure erase command
       - For HDDs: DoD 3-pass overwrite (DBAN or equivalent)
       - If sanitization is not possible: device stays on-site
         and vendor performs maintenance on-site under escort

2.2 On-Site Maintenance (Preferred)
  Whenever possible, maintenance is performed on-site to
  avoid the need for off-site data sanitization.

3. DOCUMENTATION
  All equipment sent off-site is logged in the Equipment
  Sanitization Log (SharePoint: IT/MA-Sanitization-Log.xlsx)
  including: device, sanitization method, date, and who
  performed the sanitization.

4. DATA RECOVERY RISK
  If a device cannot be sanitized before off-site service
  (e.g., failed drive that cannot be erased), the device
  is physically escorted by Keith Merritt or the data storage
  component is removed and retained before shipping.
