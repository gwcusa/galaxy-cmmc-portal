INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.6.1 - Incident Response Capability
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC's capability to handle information security incidents, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.1.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC information systems and personnel.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC maintains a documented Incident Response Plan (IRP).
b. The IRP defines roles, responsibilities, and procedures for incident handling.
c. Nathan Cruz is the primary incident responder; Victor Osei provides management escalation.
d. The IRP is tested annually via tabletop exercise.
e. Incident response contacts include US-CERT reporting capability for CUI-related incidents.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, leads incident response. Victor Osei approves the IRP and provides executive escalation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
All personnel must report suspected security incidents to Nathan Cruz within 1 hour of discovery.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
