SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.13.7 - Split Tunneling Prohibition
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy prevents Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC remote access VPN clients from using split tunneling, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.7.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC remote access VPN connections.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Split tunneling is disabled on all VPN profiles.
b. When VPN is connected, all traffic (including internet) routes through the Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC gateway.
c. VPN configuration is enforced by managed Intune profiles and cannot be changed by users.
d. Nathan Cruz verifies split tunneling is disabled quarterly.
e. Any detected split tunnel configuration is immediately remediated.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures VPN profiles with split tunneling disabled.

5. ENFORCEMENT
VPN connections with split tunneling enabled are disconnected and reconfigured.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
