SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.13.15 - Session Authenticity
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to protect the authenticity of communications sessions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.15.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC communications sessions, including email, web sessions, and API connections.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Email authenticity is enforced via SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records.
b. Web sessions use TLS certificates with proper authentication.
c. DMARC policy enforces rejection of spoofed email from moneystraighttalk.com.
d. DKIM is configured for all outbound email.
e. Email authentication records are reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, configures and maintains email and session authentication controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failed email authentication checks result in mail rejection per DMARC policy.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
