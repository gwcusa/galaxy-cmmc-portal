MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.8.5 - Media Transport Controls
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the transport of CUI media outside of controlled areas, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.5.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC CUI media transported outside of secure office areas.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI media transport outside of secured areas requires advance approval from Camille Odom.
b. CUI media in transit is encrypted and kept in physical possession of the transporter.
c. Transport is documented in the media transport log.
d. CUI media is never checked in luggage during air travel.
e. Lost or stolen CUI media is reported to Terrell Banks and Camille Odom within 1 hour of discovery.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages media transport approvals and documentation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved CUI media transport is a security violation.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
