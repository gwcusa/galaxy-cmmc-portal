ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.21 - Portable Storage on External Systems
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits the use of portable storage devices on external systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.21.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC personnel who use portable storage devices (USB drives, external HDDs, SD cards) on non-Keith the Drone Guy LLC systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Keith the Drone Guy LLC-owned portable storage devices may not be connected to external (non-Keith the Drone Guy LLC) systems.
b. Portable storage used on external systems may not subsequently be connected to CUI systems without approval and scanning.
c. Personal portable storage devices are prohibited on Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI endpoints.
d. Intune device control policies block unauthorized USB storage on CUI endpoints.
e. Any exemption requires written approval from Keith Merritt and is logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, enforces USB device control policies. Keith Merritt approves exceptions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Violations of this policy are investigated and may result in disciplinary action.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
