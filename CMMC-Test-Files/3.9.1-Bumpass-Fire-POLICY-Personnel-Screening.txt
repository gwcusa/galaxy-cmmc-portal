PERSONNEL SECURITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.9.1 - Personnel Screening
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Bumpass Fire & Rescue's requirements for screening individuals prior to granting access to CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.9.1.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue employees, contractors, and other personnel who will have access to CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All personnel with CUI access must complete a background check before access is granted.
b. Background check includes: criminal history, employment verification, and reference checks.
c. Results are reviewed and approved by Randy Bowles before CUI access is granted.
d. Contractors with CUI access must provide evidence of background screening by their employer.
e. Background check results are retained in a secure personnel file for 5 years.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Randy Bowles, Fire Chief, reviews and approves background check results. Mike Sadler grants CUI access only after approval.

5. ENFORCEMENT
No individual may receive CUI access without a completed and approved background check.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
