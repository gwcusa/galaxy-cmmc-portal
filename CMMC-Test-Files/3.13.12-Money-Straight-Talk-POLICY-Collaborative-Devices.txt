SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.13.12 - Collaborative Computing Devices
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to prohibit remote activation of collaborative computing devices and provide indication of use to present users, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.12.

2. SCOPE
All collaborative computing devices (cameras, microphones, screen sharing) at Money Straight Talk LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Cameras and microphones cannot be remotely activated without user awareness.
b. Physical privacy covers are used on CUI workstations.
c. Camera hardware indicators (LED) must be active when in use.
d. Screen sharing requires explicit user action and consent.
e. CUI spaces do not have always-on listening devices (smart speakers, voice assistants).

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, enforces collaborative device policies through GPO and physical controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized camera/microphone activation is a critical incident.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
