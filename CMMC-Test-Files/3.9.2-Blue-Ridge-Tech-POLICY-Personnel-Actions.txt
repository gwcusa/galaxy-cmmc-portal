PERSONNEL SECURITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.9.2 - Personnel Termination and Transfer
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC takes protective measures when personnel are terminated or transferred, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.9.2.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC personnel departures, transfers, and changes in role affecting CUI access.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Upon termination, all CUI access (logical and physical) is revoked within 24 hours.
b. Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC property including devices, keys, and media is collected at or before departure.
c. Terminated personnel are briefed on continuing CUI confidentiality obligations.
d. Transfer to a new role triggers access review to ensure alignment with new responsibilities.
e. Termination procedures are documented and tracked via a termination checklist.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Victor Osei, Director of Operations, initiates termination procedures. Nathan Cruz executes logical access revocation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to complete termination procedures within 24 hours is a policy violation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
