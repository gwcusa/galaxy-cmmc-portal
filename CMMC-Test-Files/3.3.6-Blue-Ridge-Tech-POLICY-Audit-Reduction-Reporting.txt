AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.3.6 - Audit Reduction and Report Generation
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to provide audit reduction and report generation capabilities that support on-demand analysis and reporting without altering the original audit records, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.6.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC audit log sources and SIEM infrastructure.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A SIEM is deployed capable of filtering, sorting, and summarizing audit records.
b. Reports are generated without altering original log records.
c. Nathan Cruz can produce audit reports on demand for specific users, timeframes, or events.
d. Audit reduction tools operate on copies, not originals.
e. Report generation capability is tested quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, maintains audit reduction and reporting capabilities.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Audit reduction tools must not modify original log data under any circumstances.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
