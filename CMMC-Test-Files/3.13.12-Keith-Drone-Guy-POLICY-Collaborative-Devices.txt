1. PURPOSE
This policy prohibits remote activation of collaborative
computing devices (cameras, microphones, screen sharing) and
provides indication of use to users.

2. COLLABORATIVE COMPUTING DEVICES AT KDG
  - Laptop webcams (KDG-LT-001, KDG-LT-002)
  - Laptop microphones
  - Microsoft Teams screen sharing
  - Conference room camera (Logitech Rally - meeting room only)

3. REMOTE ACTIVATION PROHIBITION
  Collaborative computing devices must not be remotely
  activated without the knowledge and consent of the user:
    - Webcam access by remote applications: prohibited unless
      the application is approved and the user initiates the session
    - Microphone access by background processes: prohibited
    - Screen sharing: user must explicitly initiate (Teams)

4. TECHNICAL CONTROLS
  - Camera and microphone access controlled by Windows
    privacy settings (apps must request and be approved)
  - Intune policy blocks camera/microphone access for
    unapproved applications
  - Physical webcam covers provided to all laptop users

5. INDICATOR OF USE
  - Windows shows a camera/microphone indicator light (orange LED)
    when camera or microphone is active
  - Teams shows an on-screen indicator when screen sharing is active
  - Users are trained to watch for unexpected camera/mic activation

6. CONFERENCE ROOM CAMERA
  - Physical power switch on conference room camera
  - Camera is powered off when room is not in use
