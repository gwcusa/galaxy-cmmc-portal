MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.7.2 - Controlled Maintenance
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to provide controls on tools, techniques, mechanisms, and personnel who perform maintenance, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.2.

2. SCOPE
All maintenance activities on ASJ Realty CUI systems by internal staff or vendors.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Vendor maintenance personnel are pre-approved by Anthony James before being granted access.
b. Vendor access is escorted by Sandra Okonkwo throughout the maintenance activity.
c. Vendor tools and diagnostic software are inspected before use.
d. Remote vendor access is monitored in real-time by Sandra Okonkwo.
e. All vendor maintenance is logged with contact details, scope, and outcome.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, controls and supervises all maintenance activities.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unsupervised vendor access to CUI systems is prohibited.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
