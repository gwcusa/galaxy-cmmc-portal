1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to monitor information systems
to detect attacks and indicators of potential attacks.

2. MONITORING TOOLS AND TECHNIQUES

2.1 Microsoft Sentinel (SIEM)
  - Centralized log aggregation from all ASJ systems
  - Analytic rules detect attack patterns:
    * Brute force / credential stuffing
    * Lateral movement indicators
    * Privilege escalation followed by data access
    * Data exfiltration (large download events)
    * Malware command-and-control (C2) communication
    * Impossible travel / impossible access patterns

2.2 Microsoft Defender for Endpoint (EDR)
  - Behavioral detection on all endpoints
  - Attack surface reduction (ASR) rules active
  - Automated investigation and response (AIR)
  - Detects: ransomware behavior, fileless attacks,
    living-off-the-land techniques, injection attacks

2.3 Azure AD Identity Protection
  - Risk-based detections: leaked credentials, anonymous IP,
    malware-linked IP, impossible travel, suspicious browser

2.4 Network-Level Detection
  - Cisco Meraki IDS/IPS: network intrusion signatures
  - Cisco Umbrella: DNS anomaly detection

3. ALERT RESPONSE
  Alerts are categorized by severity:
    High/Critical: Sandra Okonkwo responds within 2 hours
    Medium: investigated next business day
    Low: reviewed weekly
  Confirmed attacks trigger the ASJ IR Plan (POL-IR-001).

4. THREAT INTELLIGENCE
  Microsoft Sentinel integrates Microsoft Threat Intelligence
  to correlate against known IOCs automatically.
