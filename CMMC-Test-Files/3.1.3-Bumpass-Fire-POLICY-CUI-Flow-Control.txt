ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.3 - CUI Information Flow Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the flow of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) at Bumpass Fire & Rescue in accordance with approved authorizations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.3.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue systems, users, and processes that create, receive, transmit, or store CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI shall only flow between systems and users with documented authorization.
b. Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies are configured to block unauthorized CUI exfiltration.
c. CUI transmission to external parties requires approval from Randy Bowles.
d. Email DLP rules prevent CUI from being sent to non-bumpassfire.org addresses without encryption.
e. File transfers to removable media are logged and require supervisor approval.
f. Mike Sadler reviews DLP policy alerts monthly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler configures and monitors DLP controls. Randy Bowles approves external CUI sharing.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized CUI flow events are investigated and may result in termination.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
