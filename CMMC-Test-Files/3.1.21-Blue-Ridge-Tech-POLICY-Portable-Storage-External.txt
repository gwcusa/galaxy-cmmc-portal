1. PURPOSE
This policy limits the use of company portable storage devices
on external (non-company-owned) systems to prevent
introduction of malware or unauthorized data exfiltration.

2. POLICY STATEMENT
  - Company portable storage devices (USB drives, external
    hard drives) containing CUI must NOT be connected to
    external systems (client systems, personal computers,
    public computers, hotel business center computers, etc.)
    without prior written authorization from Nathan Cruz.

  - If a portable storage device must be used on an external
    system for business necessity (e.g., delivering files to
    a client site), it must:
    a) Be scanned for malware before and after the connection
    b) Contain only the minimum data necessary
    c) Be reported to Nathan Cruz within 24 hours

3. PERSONALLY OWNED STORAGE ON COMPANY SYSTEMS
  Personally owned USB drives are prohibited on company
  systems. Intune enforces USB device restrictions:
  only approved BRTS-issued USB drives are permitted.

4. DATA TRANSFER ALTERNATIVES
  Preferred method for file exchange with external parties:
    - DoD SAFE (for government clients)
    - SharePoint external sharing (with guest access approval)
    - Encrypted email attachment (files < 10MB)

5. ENFORCEMENT
  USB port access is controlled via Intune Device Control
  Policy. Unauthorized USB insertions are blocked and logged.
