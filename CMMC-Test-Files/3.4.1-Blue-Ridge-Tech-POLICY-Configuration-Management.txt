CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.4.1 - Configuration Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC's requirements for maintaining baseline configurations of information systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.1.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC hardware, software, and information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Nathan Cruz maintains an inventory of all hardware and software in the CUI environment.
b. Baseline configurations are documented for all CUI systems.
c. The inventory is reviewed and updated quarterly.
d. New devices must be approved and configured per baseline before connecting to CUI networks.
e. Deviations from baseline require documented justification and approval.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, maintains the asset inventory and baseline configurations.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized devices on the CUI network are removed immediately upon detection.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
