SECURITY ASSESSMENT POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.12.2 - Plan of Action and Milestones
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to develop and implement plans of action to correct deficiencies and reduce vulnerabilities in CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.12.2.

2. SCOPE
All identified deficiencies in Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI system security controls.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A POA&M is maintained for all identified security control deficiencies.
b. Each POA&M entry includes: finding, remediation plan, responsible party, and milestone dates.
c. POA&M is reviewed and updated quarterly.
d. Completed items are marked closed with evidence of remediation.
e. Victor Osei approves the POA&M quarterly and accepts residual risk.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, maintains the POA&M. Victor Osei reviews and approves it quarterly.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Overdue POA&M items without approved extensions are escalated immediately to Victor Osei.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
