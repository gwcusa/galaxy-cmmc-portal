ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.3 - CUI Information Flow Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the flow of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) at Money Straight Talk LLC in accordance with approved authorizations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.3.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC systems, users, and processes that create, receive, transmit, or store CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI shall only flow between systems and users with documented authorization.
b. Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies are configured to block unauthorized CUI exfiltration.
c. CUI transmission to external parties requires approval from Camille Odom.
d. Email DLP rules prevent CUI from being sent to non-moneystraighttalk.com addresses without encryption.
e. File transfers to removable media are logged and require supervisor approval.
f. Terrell Banks reviews DLP policy alerts monthly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks configures and monitors DLP controls. Camille Odom approves external CUI sharing.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized CUI flow events are investigated and may result in termination.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
