IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.5.11 - Authentication Feedback Obscuring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to obscure authentication feedback information to protect it from exploitation, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.11.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI system login interfaces.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Password fields display asterisks or dots instead of the actual characters typed.
b. Login failure messages do not reveal whether the username or password was incorrect.
c. Authentication error messages provide no information that aids guessing.
d. Debug authentication information is never displayed to end users.
e. Nathan Cruz reviews authentication interface feedback annually.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, ensures all login interfaces obscure authentication feedback.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any interface that reveals password characters or specific credential errors must be immediately remediated.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
