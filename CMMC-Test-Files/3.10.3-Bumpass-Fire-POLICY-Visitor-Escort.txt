1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes requirements for escorting visitors
and monitoring their activity within BFR facilities.

2. VISITOR DEFINITION
A visitor is any person who is not an active BFR employee
or authorized contractor with permanent access. This includes:
  - Vendors and service technicians
  - Government inspectors and auditors
  - Media and journalists
  - Family members of staff
  - Job applicants

3. VISITOR CHECK-IN
  All visitors must:
    a) Check in at the main reception desk
    b) Provide government-issued photo ID
    c) Sign the Visitor Log (paper, at reception)
    d) Receive a "VISITOR" badge
    e) State their purpose and the staff member they are visiting

4. ESCORT REQUIREMENTS
  - Visitors must be escorted by a BFR employee at all times
  - Visitors are NOT permitted unescorted in:
    * Server room / IT areas
    * Records rooms containing CUI
    * Dispatch center
    * Command staff offices
  - The escorting employee is responsible for visitor behavior

5. VISITOR ACTIVITY MONITORING
  - Visitors are covered by BFR security cameras
  - Escort must remain in visual contact at all times in
    sensitive areas

6. VISITOR DEPARTURE
  Visitor badge is collected and Visitor Log updated with
  departure time upon exit.
