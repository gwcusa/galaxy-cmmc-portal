MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.8.4 - CUI Media Marking
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to mark media with necessary CUI markings and distribution limitations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.4.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue physical and digital media containing CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Physical media containing CUI is labeled with "CUI" on the media and its container.
b. CUI documents include CUI header/footer markings.
c. Digital files containing CUI include CUI designation in the file name or metadata.
d. Media labels are applied by Mike Sadler before the media is used to store CUI.
e. Mike Sadler verifies CUI markings quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, applies and verifies CUI markings on all CUI media.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unmarked CUI media is a compliance finding requiring immediate remediation.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
