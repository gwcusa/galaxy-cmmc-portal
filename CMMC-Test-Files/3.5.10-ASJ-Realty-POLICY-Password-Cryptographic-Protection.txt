IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.5.10 - Cryptographic Password Storage
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to store and transmit only cryptographically protected passwords, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.10.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty systems that store or transmit user passwords.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.
b. Password storage uses strong one-way hashing (bcrypt, PBKDF2, or Argon2).
c. Reversible encryption of passwords is prohibited and technically disabled.
d. Passwords are transmitted only over encrypted channels (TLS 1.2+).
e. Sandra Okonkwo verifies cryptographic password protection settings quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, ensures password storage and transmission uses cryptographic protection.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems storing passwords in plaintext or reversible encryption are immediately remediated.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
