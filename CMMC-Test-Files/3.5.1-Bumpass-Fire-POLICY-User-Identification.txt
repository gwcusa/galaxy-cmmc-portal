IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.5.1 - User Identification
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Bumpass Fire & Rescue's requirements for identifying all information system users, processes, and devices, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.1.

2. SCOPE
All users, devices, and processes that access Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Every user account is assigned a unique username tied to a specific individual.
b. Every device is assigned a unique device identity registered in the asset inventory.
c. Shared or anonymous accounts are prohibited for CUI system access.
d. Service accounts are documented with a named owner responsible for the account.
e. Mike Sadler maintains and reviews the user and device identity registry quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, maintains user and device identity records.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Use of anonymous or unregistered identities on CUI systems is prohibited.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
