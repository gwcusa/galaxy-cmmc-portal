AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.3.4 - Audit Failure Alerting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to alert personnel in the event of an audit logging failure, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.4.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue audit logging infrastructure and CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Automated alerts notify Mike Sadler if any log source stops sending events for more than 30 minutes.
b. Disk space alerts fire when log storage exceeds 80% capacity.
c. Audit logging failures are treated as priority-1 incidents requiring immediate response.
d. Response to logging failure alerts must begin within 15 minutes.
e. Alert handling and resolution is documented.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and responds to audit failure alerts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unacknowledged audit failure alerts beyond 15 minutes escalate automatically to Randy Bowles.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
