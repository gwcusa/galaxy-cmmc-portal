SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.13.10 - Cryptographic Key Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to establish and manage cryptographic keys for required cryptography, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.10.

2. SCOPE
All cryptographic keys used in Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Cryptographic keys are generated, stored, and managed in Azure Key Vault.
b. Keys are rotated at least annually or upon personnel departure.
c. Access to Key Vault is restricted to Keith Merritt and specific service principals.
d. Key operations are logged and audited.
e. Compromised keys are immediately revoked and replaced.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, manages all cryptographic keys for CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unmanaged or expired keys are a critical finding requiring immediate remediation.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
