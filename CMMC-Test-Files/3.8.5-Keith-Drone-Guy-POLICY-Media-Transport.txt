MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.8.5 - Media Transport Controls
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the transport of CUI media outside of controlled areas, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.5.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI media transported outside of secure office areas.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI media transport outside of secured areas requires advance approval from Keith Merritt.
b. CUI media in transit is encrypted and kept in physical possession of the transporter.
c. Transport is documented in the media transport log.
d. CUI media is never checked in luggage during air travel.
e. Lost or stolen CUI media is reported to Keith Merritt and Keith Merritt within 1 hour of discovery.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, manages media transport approvals and documentation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved CUI media transport is a security violation.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
