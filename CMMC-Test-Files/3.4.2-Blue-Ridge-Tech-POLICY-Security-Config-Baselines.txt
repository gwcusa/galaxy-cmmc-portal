CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.4.2 - Security Configuration Baselines
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to establish and enforce security configuration settings for information technology products, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.2.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC hardware and software in the CUI environment.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Security configuration baselines are derived from CIS Benchmarks or DISA STIGs.
b. Baselines are applied to all CUI endpoints via Intune configuration profiles.
c. Endpoints that drift from baseline are flagged as non-compliant.
d. Nathan Cruz remediates baseline deviations within 72 hours.
e. Baselines are reviewed and updated annually or after major OS releases.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, creates, applies, and maintains security configuration baselines.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Non-compliant endpoints are blocked from CUI access until remediated.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
