ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.21 - Portable Storage on External Systems
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits the use of portable storage devices on external systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.21.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC personnel who use portable storage devices (USB drives, external HDDs, SD cards) on non-Money Straight Talk LLC systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Money Straight Talk LLC-owned portable storage devices may not be connected to external (non-Money Straight Talk LLC) systems.
b. Portable storage used on external systems may not subsequently be connected to CUI systems without approval and scanning.
c. Personal portable storage devices are prohibited on Money Straight Talk LLC CUI endpoints.
d. Intune device control policies block unauthorized USB storage on CUI endpoints.
e. Any exemption requires written approval from Camille Odom and is logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, enforces USB device control policies. Camille Odom approves exceptions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Violations of this policy are investigated and may result in disciplinary action.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
