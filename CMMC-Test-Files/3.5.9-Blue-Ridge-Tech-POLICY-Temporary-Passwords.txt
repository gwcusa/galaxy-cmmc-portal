IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.5.9 - Temporary Password Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy allows Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to employ temporary passwords with an immediate change requirement upon first use, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.9.

2. SCOPE
All temporary, one-time, or provisioning passwords issued by Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Temporary passwords require immediate change upon first login.
b. Temporary passwords expire within 24 hours if not used.
c. Temporary passwords are communicated to users via secure channel (encrypted email or in-person).
d. Nathan Cruz logs all temporary password issuances.
e. Accounts with temporary passwords that expire unused are reviewed within 48 hours.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, issues and tracks temporary passwords.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Temporary passwords that are not changed within 24 hours result in account lockout.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
