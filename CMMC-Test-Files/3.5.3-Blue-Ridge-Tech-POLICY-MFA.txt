IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.5.3 - Multifactor Authentication
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to use multifactor authentication for local and network access to privileged accounts and network access to non-privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.3.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC personnel accessing CUI systems locally or remotely.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. MFA is required for all network access to CUI systems.
b. MFA is required for local logon to privileged accounts.
c. MFA uses at least two factors from: something you know, something you have, something you are.
d. MFA enrollment is completed within 24 hours of account provisioning.
e. MFA bypass is prohibited except during documented disaster recovery scenarios.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, enforces MFA through Conditional Access policies.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Access without MFA is technically blocked by Conditional Access.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
