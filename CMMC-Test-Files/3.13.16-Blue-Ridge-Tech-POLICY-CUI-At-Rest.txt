SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.13.16 - Protection of CUI at Rest
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to protect the confidentiality of CUI at rest, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.16.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC storage locations containing CUI (endpoints, cloud storage, backups).

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All endpoints storing CUI must have BitLocker full-disk encryption enabled.
b. Microsoft 365 SharePoint stores CUI encrypted at rest (AES-256, Microsoft-managed keys).
c. Removable media containing CUI must be encrypted.
d. BitLocker recovery keys are stored in Azure Key Vault (not in AD only).
e. Encryption status is verified monthly via Intune compliance reports.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, ensures all CUI storage locations have encryption at rest enabled.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted devices with CUI are treated as data breach risk requiring immediate action.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
