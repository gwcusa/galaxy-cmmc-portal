ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.20 - External System Connection Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to verify and control connections to external systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.20.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC connections to systems, networks, or cloud services outside of direct organizational control.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. External system connections require a documented Interconnection Security Agreement (ISA).
b. Victor Osei must approve all new external system connections before establishment.
c. Nathan Cruz maintains a register of all approved external connections.
d. External connections are reviewed annually and when operational needs change.
e. Connections to external systems are terminated when no longer operationally required.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, maintains the external connection register. Victor Osei approves connections.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved external connections are blocked and investigated as security incidents.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
