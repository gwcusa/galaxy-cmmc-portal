CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.4.6 - Least Functionality
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to configure information systems to provide only essential capabilities, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.6.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC information systems and devices in the CUI environment.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Systems are configured with only the functions and services required for their mission.
b. Unnecessary services, ports, and applications are disabled or removed.
c. Default accounts and sample applications are disabled before deployment.
d. Nathan Cruz audits running services on all CUI systems quarterly.
e. Any service additions require documented approval from Victor Osei.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures systems to least functionality standard and maintains service inventories.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized services discovered during audits are disabled within 24 hours.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
