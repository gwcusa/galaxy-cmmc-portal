SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.14.7 - Unauthorized Use Detection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to identify unauthorized use of CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.7.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems and user accounts.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Microsoft Sentinel and Defender detect unauthorized or anomalous system use.
b. User behavior analytics (UBA) identifies deviations from normal usage patterns.
c. System banners and acceptable use notices inform users of monitoring.
d. Reports of unauthorized use are investigated within 4 hours.
e. Confirmed unauthorized use triggers immediate account suspension and incident response.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, identifies and investigates unauthorized use of CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Confirmed unauthorized use results in immediate account lock and escalation to Keith Merritt and legal counsel.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
