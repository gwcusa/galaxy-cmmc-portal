SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.14.5 - System and File Scanning
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to perform periodic scans of information systems and real-time scans of files from external sources as files are downloaded, opened, or executed, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.5.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC CUI endpoints and files.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Real-time scanning is enabled on all CUI endpoints (files scanned on access).
b. Weekly full-disk scans are scheduled on all CUI workstations.
c. Files downloaded from email or internet are scanned before execution.
d. Scan results are reviewed by Terrell Banks weekly.
e. Scan history is retained for 90 days.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, configures and monitors scanning on all CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Endpoints with scanning disabled are isolated from CUI until remediated.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
