RISK ASSESSMENT POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.11.1 - Risk Assessment
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to periodically assess the risk to organizational operations from the operation of information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.11.1.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC information systems and processes involving CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A formal risk assessment is conducted at least annually.
b. The risk assessment identifies threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and impact.
c. Risks are documented in a Risk Register maintained by Nathan Cruz.
d. Victor Osei reviews and approves the risk assessment findings.
e. Risk assessment results drive remediation priorities.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, conducts the risk assessment. Victor Osei reviews and approves results.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to complete annual risk assessment is reported to Victor Osei as a compliance gap.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
