SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.13.8 - Encryption of CUI in Transit
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to implement cryptographic mechanisms to prevent unauthorized disclosure of CUI during transmission, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.8.

2. SCOPE
All CUI transmitted across Bumpass Fire & Rescue networks and to/from external parties.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All CUI in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher.
b. Weak protocols (SSL, TLS 1.0, TLS 1.1) and weak cipher suites are disabled.
c. VPN traffic uses AES-256 minimum.
d. Email containing CUI uses TLS enforcement or S/MIME encryption.
e. Encryption configuration is reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and verifies all CUI transmission encryption.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted CUI transmission is a critical security violation.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
