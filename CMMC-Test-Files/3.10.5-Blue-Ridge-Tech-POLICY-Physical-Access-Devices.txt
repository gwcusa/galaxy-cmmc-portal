PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.10.5 - Physical Access Device Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to control and manage physical access devices (keys, key cards, PINs), per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.5.

2. SCOPE
All physical access devices used to access Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A register of all physical access devices (keys, PINs, badges) is maintained.
b. Physical access devices are issued only to authorized personnel.
c. Lost or stolen access devices are reported and deactivated within 2 hours.
d. Access PINs are changed quarterly or upon compromise.
e. Access devices are collected upon personnel termination.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Victor Osei, Director of Operations, approves access device issuance. Nathan Cruz maintains the access device register.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unreported loss of physical access devices is a policy violation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
