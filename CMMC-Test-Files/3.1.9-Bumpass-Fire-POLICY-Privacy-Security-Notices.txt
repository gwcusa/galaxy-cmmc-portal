ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.9 - Privacy and Security Notices
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to provide privacy and security notices consistent with CUI rules at system logon, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.9.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue information systems accessible by personnel or contractors.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A logon banner displaying privacy and security notices is displayed before authentication.
b. The banner references CUI handling requirements and consent to monitoring.
c. Mike Sadler reviews and updates banner text annually or upon regulatory change.
d. Banner text is approved by Randy Bowles before deployment.
e. Banner is enforced via Group Policy and Azure AD Conditional Access.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, implements and maintains logon banners across all systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems without compliant logon banners must be remediated within 48 hours of identification.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
