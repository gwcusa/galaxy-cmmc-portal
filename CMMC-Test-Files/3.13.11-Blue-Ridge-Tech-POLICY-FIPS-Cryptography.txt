SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.13.11 - FIPS-Validated Cryptography
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to employ FIPS-validated cryptography when protecting CUI, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.11.

2. SCOPE
All cryptographic functions protecting CUI at Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. FIPS 140-2 mode is enabled on all endpoints processing CUI.
b. Only FIPS-validated cryptographic modules are used for CUI protection.
c. Non-FIPS algorithms (MD5, RC4, DES, 3DES, SHA-1) are prohibited for CUI protection.
d. FIPS mode is enforced via Group Policy.
e. FIPS compliance is verified quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, enables and maintains FIPS mode on all CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with FIPS mode disabled must be remediated within 24 hours.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
