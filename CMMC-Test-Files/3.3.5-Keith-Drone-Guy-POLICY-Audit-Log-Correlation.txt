AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.3.5 - Audit Log Correlation and Analysis
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to correlate audit record review, analysis, and reporting across organizational processes to gain situational awareness, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.5.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC audit log sources and CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit records from multiple sources are correlated in a centralized SIEM.
b. Correlation rules detect multi-source attack patterns.
c. Keith Merritt reviews correlation findings weekly.
d. Cross-source incidents are escalated immediately to Keith Merritt.
e. Correlation rules are reviewed and updated quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, maintains and monitors the SIEM correlation infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Gaps in log correlation coverage are remediated within 30 days of identification.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
