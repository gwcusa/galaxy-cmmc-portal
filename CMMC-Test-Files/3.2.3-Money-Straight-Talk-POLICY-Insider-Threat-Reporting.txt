AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.2.3 - Insider Threat Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Money Straight Talk LLC provides security training on recognizing and reporting potential insider threats, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.2.3.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC personnel who may observe or have knowledge of insider threat indicators.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Personnel are trained to recognize physical, behavioral, and technical indicators of insider threat.
b. Reporting mechanisms are clearly communicated and accessible to all personnel.
c. Anonymous reporting is available and confidentiality is maintained.
d. Reports are reviewed by Camille Odom within 24 hours.
e. Training on reporting procedures is provided annually.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks administers technical monitoring. Camille Odom reviews insider threat reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Retaliation against personnel who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
