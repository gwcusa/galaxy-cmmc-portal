AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.3.6 - Audit Reduction and Report Generation
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to provide audit reduction and report generation capabilities that support on-demand analysis and reporting without altering the original audit records, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.6.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty audit log sources and SIEM infrastructure.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A SIEM is deployed capable of filtering, sorting, and summarizing audit records.
b. Reports are generated without altering original log records.
c. Sandra Okonkwo can produce audit reports on demand for specific users, timeframes, or events.
d. Audit reduction tools operate on copies, not originals.
e. Report generation capability is tested quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, maintains audit reduction and reporting capabilities.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Audit reduction tools must not modify original log data under any circumstances.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
