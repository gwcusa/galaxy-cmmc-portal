IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.5.1 - User Identification
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes ASJ Realty's requirements for identifying all information system users, processes, and devices, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.1.

2. SCOPE
All users, devices, and processes that access ASJ Realty CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Every user account is assigned a unique username tied to a specific individual.
b. Every device is assigned a unique device identity registered in the asset inventory.
c. Shared or anonymous accounts are prohibited for CUI system access.
d. Service accounts are documented with a named owner responsible for the account.
e. Sandra Okonkwo maintains and reviews the user and device identity registry quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, maintains user and device identity records.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Use of anonymous or unregistered identities on CUI systems is prohibited.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
