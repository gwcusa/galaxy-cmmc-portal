ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.4 - Separation of Duties
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy separates duties of individuals at Keith the Drone Guy LLC to reduce the risk of malevolent activity without collusion, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.4.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC personnel with access to CUI systems or privileged administrative functions.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. No single individual shall control all phases of a critical business or IT operation.
b. Account creation/approval and account provisioning roles are assigned to different individuals.
c. Keith Merritt administers accounts; Keith Merritt approves new privileged access requests.
d. System audit log access is restricted to personnel independent of daily operations.
e. Dual authorization is required for deletion of CUI data.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt implements technical controls. Keith Merritt ensures organizational enforcement.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Deliberate circumvention of separation-of-duties controls is grounds for immediate termination.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
