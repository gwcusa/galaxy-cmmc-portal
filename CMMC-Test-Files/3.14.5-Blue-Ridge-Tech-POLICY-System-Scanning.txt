1. PURPOSE
This policy requires periodic scans of information systems
and real-time scans of files as they are downloaded, opened,
or executed.

2. PERIODIC SCANNING

2.1 Full System Scans
  - Frequency: Weekly (every Sunday at 2:00 AM)
  - Scope: All local drives, including hidden files and folders
  - Tool: Microsoft Defender for Endpoint (scheduled scan)
  - Scan reports: available in Defender portal

2.2 Quick Scans
  - Frequency: Daily at 12:00 PM
  - Scope: Common malware locations (startup folders, registry)
  - Tool: Microsoft Defender (scheduled quick scan)

2.3 Vulnerability Scans
  - Frequency: Monthly (first Monday of each month)
  - Scope: All BRTS endpoints and servers
  - Tool: Microsoft Defender Vulnerability Management + Nessus

3. REAL-TIME SCANNING

3.1 On-Access Scanning
  - ALL file operations trigger real-time scan:
    * Files downloaded from the internet
    * Files opened from network shares
    * Email attachments opened in Outlook
    * Files copied from removable media
    * Files executed (any .exe, .dll, .ps1, .bat, etc.)

3.2 Email Scanning
  - Defender for Office 365 Safe Attachments:
    Detonates all email attachments in sandbox before delivery
  - Safe Links: all URLs rewritten and checked at click time

4. SCAN RESULTS
  Threats detected are automatically quarantined.
  Nathan Cruz receives alert on any detection.
