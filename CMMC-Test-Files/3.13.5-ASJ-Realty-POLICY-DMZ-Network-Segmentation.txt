SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.13.5 - Network Segmentation
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to implement subnetworks for publicly accessible system components that are physically or logically separated from internal networks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.5.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty network segments in the CUI environment.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI systems reside in a separate, isolated network segment (CUI VLAN).
b. Guest/public WiFi is isolated from CUI network on a separate VLAN.
c. Publicly accessible systems (if any) are placed in a DMZ, separate from CUI.
d. VLAN segmentation is enforced by managed switches and firewall rules.
e. Sandra Okonkwo reviews VLAN configurations quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, designs and maintains network segmentation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
CUI systems on non-CUI network segments must be remediated within 24 hours.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
