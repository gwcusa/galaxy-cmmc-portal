AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.3.1 - System Audit Logging
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to create, protect, and retain information system audit records to enable monitoring, analysis, investigation, and reporting of unlawful or unauthorized activity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.1.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logging is enabled on all CUI systems for login, logout, access, and change events.
b. Audit logs are retained for a minimum of 90 days online and 1 year archived.
c. Log integrity is protected to prevent tampering.
d. Sandra Okonkwo configures log collection to the centralized SIEM.
e. Logging failures generate immediate alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, configures and maintains the logging infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Disabling or tampering with audit logs is a serious security violation.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
