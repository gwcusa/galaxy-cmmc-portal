ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.18 - Mobile Device Connection Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the connection of mobile devices to Money Straight Talk LLC systems and CUI networks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.18.

2. SCOPE
All mobile devices (smartphones, tablets, laptops) connecting to Money Straight Talk LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All mobile devices connecting to Money Straight Talk LLC CUI resources must be enrolled in Intune MDM.
b. Non-enrolled personal devices may not access CUI systems or networks.
c. Mobile devices must meet compliance requirements (encrypted, PIN-locked, current OS).
d. Terrell Banks approves mobile device enrollment requests from staff.
e. Lost or stolen devices are remotely wiped within 4 hours of report.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages the MDM solution and mobile device compliance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unenrolled mobile devices detected on CUI networks are blocked and reported.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
