CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.4.1 - Configuration Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Bumpass Fire & Rescue's requirements for maintaining baseline configurations of information systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.1.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue hardware, software, and information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Mike Sadler maintains an inventory of all hardware and software in the CUI environment.
b. Baseline configurations are documented for all CUI systems.
c. The inventory is reviewed and updated quarterly.
d. New devices must be approved and configured per baseline before connecting to CUI networks.
e. Deviations from baseline require documented justification and approval.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, maintains the asset inventory and baseline configurations.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized devices on the CUI network are removed immediately upon detection.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
