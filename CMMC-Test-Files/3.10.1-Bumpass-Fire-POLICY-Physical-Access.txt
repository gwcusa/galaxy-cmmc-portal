PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.10.1 - Physical Access Authorization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits physical access to Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems to authorized individuals, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.1.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue facilities where CUI systems are located.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI system areas are access-controlled with electronic key card or coded entry.
b. A list of authorized personnel with physical access is maintained by Randy Bowles.
c. The authorized access list is reviewed and updated quarterly.
d. Visitors are not permitted unescorted access to CUI areas.
e. Access rights are revoked within 24 hours of termination.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Randy Bowles, Fire Chief, approves physical access authorizations. Mike Sadler maintains technical controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized physical access to CUI areas is a security incident.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
