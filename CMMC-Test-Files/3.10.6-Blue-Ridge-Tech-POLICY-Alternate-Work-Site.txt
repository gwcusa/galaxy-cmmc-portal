1. PURPOSE
This policy enforces CUI safeguarding measures when BRTS
employees work from alternate locations (home offices, client
sites, hotels, etc.).

2. APPROVED ALTERNATE WORK SITES
BRTS staff may work remotely from:
  - Personal home offices (approved by Nathan Cruz/Victor Osei)
  - Client sites (under client supervision)
Not permitted: public locations (coffee shops, airports, libraries)
unless VPN is active and no CUI is displayed on-screen in public.

3. HOME OFFICE REQUIREMENTS
For staff approved to handle CUI from home:
  a) Dedicated work area with door that can be closed
     (CUI work cannot be done in open shared family spaces)
  b) Private WiFi network (no public/shared WiFi for CUI)
  c) VPN required for all company system access
  d) Screen privacy filter recommended if screen visible
     from windows or common areas
  e) Paper CUI must be stored in locked drawer when not in use
  f) Paper CUI must be shredded on-site (cross-cut) or
     returned to the office for secure shredding

4. TECHNICAL SAFEGUARDS AT ALTERNATE SITES
  - All CUI access over encrypted VPN (enforced)
  - Conditional Access blocks unencrypted or non-Intune access
  - Microsoft Defender for Endpoint active on all remote devices
  - Automatic screen lock after 10 minutes inactivity

5. REMOTE WORK AGREEMENT
  Staff approved for remote CUI work sign the BRTS Remote Work
  and CUI Agreement annually.
