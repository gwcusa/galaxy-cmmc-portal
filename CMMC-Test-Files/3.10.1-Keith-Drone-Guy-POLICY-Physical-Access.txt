PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.10.1 - Physical Access Authorization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits physical access to Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems to authorized individuals, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.1.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC facilities where CUI systems are located.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI system areas are access-controlled with electronic key card or coded entry.
b. A list of authorized personnel with physical access is maintained by Keith Merritt.
c. The authorized access list is reviewed and updated quarterly.
d. Visitors are not permitted unescorted access to CUI areas.
e. Access rights are revoked within 24 hours of termination.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, approves physical access authorizations. Keith Merritt maintains technical controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized physical access to CUI areas is a security incident.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
