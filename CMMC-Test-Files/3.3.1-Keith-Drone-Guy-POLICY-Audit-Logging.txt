AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.3.1 - System Audit Logging
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to create, protect, and retain information system audit records to enable monitoring, analysis, investigation, and reporting of unlawful or unauthorized activity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.1.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logging is enabled on all CUI systems for login, logout, access, and change events.
b. Audit logs are retained for a minimum of 90 days online and 1 year archived.
c. Log integrity is protected to prevent tampering.
d. Keith Merritt configures log collection to the centralized SIEM.
e. Logging failures generate immediate alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures and maintains the logging infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Disabling or tampering with audit logs is a serious security violation.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
