1. PURPOSE
This policy prevents remote devices from simultaneously
using non-remote (VPN) connections with the system and
other connections to external resources (split tunneling),
which could allow data to bypass security controls.

2. POLICY STATEMENT
Split tunneling is PROHIBITED for VPN connections made
by MST employees and authorized users.

3. FULL-TUNNEL VPN REQUIREMENT
When connected to the MST VPN:
  - ALL traffic (to company resources AND to internet)
    must route through the VPN tunnel
  - The VPN client must be configured with
    "Tunnel All Traffic" or equivalent
  - No direct internet access is permitted while VPN is
    active (all internet requests also pass through MST
    perimeter filtering)

4. ENFORCEMENT
  Cisco AnyConnect VPN profile is configured with:
    split-tunnel-policy tunnelall
  This prevents the client from allowing split tunneling
  regardless of client-side settings.

5. MONITORING
  Any VPN client attempting to establish split tunnel
  connections will be denied by the VPN gateway.
  Terrell Banks reviews VPN configuration quarterly.

6. CLOUD SERVICES
  For direct cloud services (M365), Conditional Access
  requires Intune-compliant devices, providing equivalent
  security even when not on VPN (Defender always active).
