1. PURPOSE
This policy requires KDG to maintain audit logs of physical
access to facilities and sensitive areas.

2. LOGGED AREAS
Physical access logs are maintained for:
  - Main office entrance (key fob log)
  - Server/IT closet (keypad access log)
  - NAS/data storage area (same room as IT closet)

3. LOGGING METHODS

3.1 Electronic Key Fob System (Main Entrance, After Hours)
  - Alarm system (DSC Smart Home) logs key fob entries:
    user ID, date, time, and door accessed
  - Log retained: 90 days on the alarm controller
    (downloaded monthly by Keith Merritt to SharePoint)

3.2 IT Closet Keypad
  - Keypad generates an entry log for each access attempt:
    success/failure, date, time
  - Log downloaded monthly and reviewed by Keith Merritt

3.3 Security Camera
  - IP camera covers server closet entrance (visual log)
  - Footage retained 30 days (overwrite)

4. LOG REVIEW
  Keith Merritt reviews access logs monthly.
  Any after-hours or anomalous access is investigated.

5. LOG PROTECTION
  Physical access logs are stored in SharePoint (IT folder)
  and cannot be modified by standard users.
