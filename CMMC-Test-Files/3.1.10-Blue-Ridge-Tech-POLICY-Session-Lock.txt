ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.10 - Session Lock with Pattern-Hiding Display
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to employ session lock with pattern-hiding displays after a period of inactivity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.10.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC endpoints and remote sessions used to access CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Screen lock activates after 15 minutes of user inactivity.
b. Screen lock uses a pattern-hiding screensaver (not a transparent or clear display).
c. Password is required to unlock any locked session.
d. Screen lock settings are enforced via Group Policy and Intune.
e. Users may not configure screen lock settings beyond policy minimums.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and enforces screen lock via MDM and Group Policy.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Tampering with screen lock settings constitutes a policy violation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
