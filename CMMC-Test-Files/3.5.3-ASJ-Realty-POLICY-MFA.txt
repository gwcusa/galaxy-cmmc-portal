IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.5.3 - Multifactor Authentication
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to use multifactor authentication for local and network access to privileged accounts and network access to non-privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.3.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty personnel accessing CUI systems locally or remotely.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. MFA is required for all network access to CUI systems.
b. MFA is required for local logon to privileged accounts.
c. MFA uses at least two factors from: something you know, something you have, something you are.
d. MFA enrollment is completed within 24 hours of account provisioning.
e. MFA bypass is prohibited except during documented disaster recovery scenarios.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, enforces MFA through Conditional Access policies.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Access without MFA is technically blocked by Conditional Access.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
