SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.14.7 - Unauthorized Use Detection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to identify unauthorized use of CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.7.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems and user accounts.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Microsoft Sentinel and Defender detect unauthorized or anomalous system use.
b. User behavior analytics (UBA) identifies deviations from normal usage patterns.
c. System banners and acceptable use notices inform users of monitoring.
d. Reports of unauthorized use are investigated within 4 hours.
e. Confirmed unauthorized use triggers immediate account suspension and incident response.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, identifies and investigates unauthorized use of CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Confirmed unauthorized use results in immediate account lock and escalation to Victor Osei and legal counsel.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
