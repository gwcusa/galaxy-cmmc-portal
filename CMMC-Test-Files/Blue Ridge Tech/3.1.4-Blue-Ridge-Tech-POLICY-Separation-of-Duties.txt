ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.4 - Separation of Duties
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy separates duties of individuals at Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to reduce the risk of malevolent activity without collusion, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.4.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC personnel with access to CUI systems or privileged administrative functions.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. No single individual shall control all phases of a critical business or IT operation.
b. Account creation/approval and account provisioning roles are assigned to different individuals.
c. Nathan Cruz administers accounts; Victor Osei approves new privileged access requests.
d. System audit log access is restricted to personnel independent of daily operations.
e. Dual authorization is required for deletion of CUI data.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz implements technical controls. Victor Osei ensures organizational enforcement.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Deliberate circumvention of separation-of-duties controls is grounds for immediate termination.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
