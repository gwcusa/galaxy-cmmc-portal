SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.13.4 - Shared Resource Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to prevent unauthorized and unintended information transfer via shared system resources, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.4.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC systems with shared resources (memory, storage, network) in the CUI environment.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Virtual machines and containers are isolated to prevent data leakage between workloads.
b. Temporary storage is cleared between user sessions.
c. Printer spooler directories are cleared after each job.
d. Memory is overwritten or not re-used across security boundaries without clearing.
e. Nathan Cruz verifies isolation controls quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and verifies shared resource isolation controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Shared resource isolation failures are treated as potential data leakage incidents.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
