AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.2.1 - Security Awareness Training
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC provides security awareness training to personnel to make them aware of security risks associated with their activities and organizational policies, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.2.1.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC personnel, contractors, and any individual with access to CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All personnel complete security awareness training upon hire and annually thereafter.
b. Training covers CUI handling, phishing recognition, password hygiene, and reporting.
c. Training completion is documented and tracked by Nathan Cruz.
d. Personnel who do not complete training within 30 days of the due date have CUI access suspended.
e. Training content is reviewed and updated annually.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, administers the training program. Victor Osei ensures organizational compliance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
CUI access is revoked for personnel with overdue training.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
