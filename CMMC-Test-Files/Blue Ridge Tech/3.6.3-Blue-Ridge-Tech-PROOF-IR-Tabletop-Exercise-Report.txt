SCENARIO DESCRIPTION
-------------------------------------------------
At 8:45 AM, Marcus Johnson reports that his workstation
(BRTS-WS-JOHNSON) is displaying a ransom note and he
cannot access any files on the shared drive.

RESPONSE WALKTHROUGH
-------------------------------------------------
Step 1 - Detection and Initial Response (0-30 minutes)
  Actions discussed:
    - Marcus isolates BRTS-WS-JOHNSON from network
      (disconnect network cable, disable WiFi)
    - Nathan Cruz notified immediately
    - Nathan logs into Defender portal to assess scope
    - Preliminary check: Which other systems show anomalous
      activity? (Sentinel alert review)
  Gaps identified: Marcus was unsure whether to pull the network
    cable or just turn off the PC. Clarified: pull network cable,
    leave PC on for forensics.

Step 2 - Scope Assessment (30-90 minutes)
  Actions discussed:
    - Identify all affected systems via Sentinel
    - Determine if CUI was on affected shares
    - Contact client accounts to assess exposure
  Gaps identified: No written client contact list for incident
    notification. ACTION: Nathan to create contact card by 6/1.

Step 3 - Containment (1-4 hours)
  Actions discussed:
    - Isolate affected systems to quarantine VLAN
    - Disable compromised accounts
    - Block malware IOCs at firewall

Step 4 - Eradication and Recovery (4-24 hours)
  Actions discussed:
    - Rebuild from known-good image (Veeam backup)
    - Verify backup integrity before restore
    - Restore most recent clean backup (tested: 2-hour RTO)

Step 5 - Reporting
  Actions discussed:
    - DIBNet report within 72 hours if CUI affected
    - Document incident in IR register

FINDINGS AND CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
-------------------------------------------------
GAP 1: Staff unsure about physical isolation steps
  Action: Update IR procedure card. Distribute to all staff.
  Owner: Nathan Cruz | Due: June 1, 2026 | Status: COMPLETED

GAP 2: No written client incident notification contact list
  Action: Create and store in IR binder and SharePoint
  Owner: Nathan Cruz | Due: June 1, 2026 | Status: COMPLETED

GAP 3: Backup restore time not formally documented
  Action: Document RTO/RPO in IR plan (2-hour RTO confirmed)
  Owner: Nathan Cruz | Due: June 15, 2026 | Status: COMPLETED
