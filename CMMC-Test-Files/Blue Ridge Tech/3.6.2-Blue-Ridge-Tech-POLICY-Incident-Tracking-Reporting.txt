INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.6.2 - Incident Tracking and Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to track, document, and report incidents to appropriate officials, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.2.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC security incidents involving CUI systems or data.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All incidents are tracked in the IT helpdesk ticketing system.
b. Incidents are classified by severity (Low/Medium/High/Critical).
c. High and Critical incidents are reported to Victor Osei within 1 hour.
d. CUI-related incidents are reported to DoD/US-CERT as required.
e. Incident records are retained for 3 years.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, tracks and documents all incidents. Victor Osei receives escalation reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to report a known incident is a policy violation subject to disciplinary action.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
