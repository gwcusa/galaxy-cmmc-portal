IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.5.2 - User Authentication
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to authenticate the identities of users, processes, and devices before granting access to CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.2.

2. SCOPE
All access to Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems and resources.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All CUI system access requires successful authentication before access is granted.
b. Authentication is enforced via Azure AD with MFA for all accounts.
c. Passwords must meet complexity requirements as defined in policy 3.5.7.
d. Device authentication uses certificate-based enrollment via Intune.
e. Failed authentication attempts are logged and monitored.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and maintains authentication controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthenticated access to CUI systems is technically blocked and generates security alerts.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
