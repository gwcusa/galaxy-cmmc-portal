CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.4.9 - User-Installed Software Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls and monitors user-installed software on Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC information systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.9.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI endpoints and systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Standard users do not have local administrator rights on CUI endpoints.
b. All software must be installed by Nathan Cruz from the approved software list.
c. Nathan Cruz scans endpoints monthly for unapproved or newly installed software.
d. Unapproved software is removed within 24 hours of detection.
e. Personnel are prohibited from installing personal software on Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC systems.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, controls software installation and conducts monthly software audits.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Personnel who install unauthorized software receive disciplinary action.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
