PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.10.4 - Physical Access Audit Logging
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to maintain audit logs of physical access to CUI facilities, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.4.

2. SCOPE
All physical access points to Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Physical access logs record each entry and exit from CUI areas.
b. Logs include: individual identity, date, time, and access point.
c. Physical access logs are reviewed monthly by Nathan Cruz.
d. Anomalous physical access (after hours, weekends, unknown individuals) generates immediate review.
e. Physical access logs are retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, maintains and reviews physical access logs.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized physical access detected in logs is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
