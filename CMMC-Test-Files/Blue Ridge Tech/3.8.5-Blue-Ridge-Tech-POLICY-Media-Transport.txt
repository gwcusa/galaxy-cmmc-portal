MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.8.5 - Media Transport Controls
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the transport of CUI media outside of controlled areas, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.5.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI media transported outside of secure office areas.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI media transport outside of secured areas requires advance approval from Victor Osei.
b. CUI media in transit is encrypted and kept in physical possession of the transporter.
c. Transport is documented in the media transport log.
d. CUI media is never checked in luggage during air travel.
e. Lost or stolen CUI media is reported to Nathan Cruz and Victor Osei within 1 hour of discovery.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, manages media transport approvals and documentation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved CUI media transport is a security violation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
