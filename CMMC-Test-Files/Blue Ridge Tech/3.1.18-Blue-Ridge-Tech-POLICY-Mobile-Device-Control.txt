ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.18 - Mobile Device Connection Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the connection of mobile devices to Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC systems and CUI networks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.18.

2. SCOPE
All mobile devices (smartphones, tablets, laptops) connecting to Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All mobile devices connecting to Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI resources must be enrolled in Intune MDM.
b. Non-enrolled personal devices may not access CUI systems or networks.
c. Mobile devices must meet compliance requirements (encrypted, PIN-locked, current OS).
d. Nathan Cruz approves mobile device enrollment requests from staff.
e. Lost or stolen devices are remotely wiped within 4 hours of report.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, manages the MDM solution and mobile device compliance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unenrolled mobile devices detected on CUI networks are blocked and reported.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
