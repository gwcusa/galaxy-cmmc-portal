ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.14 - Managed Access Control Points for Remote Access
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to route all remote access through managed access control points, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.14.

2. SCOPE
All remote access to Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems and networks.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All remote access is routed through a centrally managed VPN gateway.
b. Split tunneling is disabled; all traffic routes through Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC gateway.
c. Direct internet access to internal CUI systems from outside the VPN is blocked.
d. The VPN gateway is managed exclusively by Nathan Cruz.
e. Firewall rules enforce that CUI systems are reachable only via VPN.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, maintains the VPN gateway and access control infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Attempts to bypass the managed access point are security incidents.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
