SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.13.1 - Network Boundary Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to monitor, control, and protect communications at external and internal network boundaries, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.1.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC network boundaries separating CUI systems from external networks.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Firewalls protect all network boundaries between CUI systems and external networks.
b. Intrusion Prevention System (IPS) monitors traffic at network boundaries.
c. Traffic is logged and reviewed for anomalies.
d. Default-deny rules are in place; only approved traffic is allowed.
e. Firewall rules are reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, manages all boundary protection devices and configurations.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized network boundary changes are security incidents requiring immediate investigation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
