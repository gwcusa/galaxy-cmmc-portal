AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.3.1 - System Audit Logging
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to create, protect, and retain information system audit records to enable monitoring, analysis, investigation, and reporting of unlawful or unauthorized activity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.1.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logging is enabled on all CUI systems for login, logout, access, and change events.
b. Audit logs are retained for a minimum of 90 days online and 1 year archived.
c. Log integrity is protected to prevent tampering.
d. Nathan Cruz configures log collection to the centralized SIEM.
e. Logging failures generate immediate alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and maintains the logging infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Disabling or tampering with audit logs is a serious security violation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
