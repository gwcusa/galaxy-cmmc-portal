ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.7 - Privileged Function Execution Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy prevents non-privileged users at Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC from executing privileged functions and captures all privileged function executions in audit logs, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.7.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC information systems and personnel.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Non-privileged accounts are technically blocked from executing privileged functions.
b. All privileged function executions are captured in audit logs.
c. Audit logs of privileged activity are reviewed by Nathan Cruz monthly.
d. Attempts by non-privileged users to escalate privileges generate security alerts.
e. Windows UAC and Azure AD RBAC enforce privilege boundaries.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and monitors privileged function controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized privilege escalation attempts are investigated as security incidents.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
