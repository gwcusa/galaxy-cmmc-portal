CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.4.3 - Configuration Change Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC's requirements for controlling and documenting changes to information system configurations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.3.

2. SCOPE
All changes to hardware, software, and configuration of Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All configuration changes require a documented change request.
b. Changes are reviewed and approved by Victor Osei before implementation.
c. Emergency changes are documented within 24 hours of implementation.
d. All changes are tested in a non-production environment when feasible.
e. Change records are retained for 3 years.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, submits and implements change requests. Victor Osei approves changes.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized changes are rolled back and investigated as security incidents.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
