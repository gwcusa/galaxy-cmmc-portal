AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.3.2 - User Activity Audit Traceability
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC audit logs can be traced to individual users and processes to support accountability, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.2.

2. SCOPE
All CUI systems at Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC where user activity occurs.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit records include user identity, timestamp, event type, source IP, and outcome.
b. Shared accounts are prohibited to ensure individual traceability.
c. User session IDs are included in all audit events.
d. Audit records are sufficient to reconstruct user actions during an investigation.
e. Nathan Cruz tests traceability during quarterly log reviews.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, ensures log fields capture sufficient data for traceability.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with insufficient audit traceability must be remediated within 30 days.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
