SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.13.10 - Cryptographic Key Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to establish and manage cryptographic keys for required cryptography, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.10.

2. SCOPE
All cryptographic keys used in Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Cryptographic keys are generated, stored, and managed in Azure Key Vault.
b. Keys are rotated at least annually or upon personnel departure.
c. Access to Key Vault is restricted to Nathan Cruz and specific service principals.
d. Key operations are logged and audited.
e. Compromised keys are immediately revoked and replaced.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, manages all cryptographic keys for CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unmanaged or expired keys are a critical finding requiring immediate remediation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
