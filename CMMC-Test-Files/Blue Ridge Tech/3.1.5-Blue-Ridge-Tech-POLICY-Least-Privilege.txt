ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.5 - Principle of Least Privilege
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to employ the principle of least privilege, including for specific security functions and privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.5.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC personnel, contractors, and systems accessing CUI environments.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Users are granted the minimum access necessary to perform their job functions.
b. Privileged accounts are used only for tasks requiring elevated permissions.
c. Nathan Cruz reviews all privileged account assignments quarterly.
d. Privileged access is revoked immediately when no longer operationally required.
e. Just-in-time (JIT) privileged access is used where technically feasible.
f. Default system accounts with excessive privileges are disabled or renamed.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, is responsible for implementing and reviewing access rights.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Violations including unauthorized privilege escalation are subject to termination.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
