1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the conditions under which user sessions
are automatically terminated to prevent unauthorized access
to CUI through abandoned or unattended sessions.

2. TERMINATION CONDITIONS

2.1 Inactivity-Based Termination
  - Web application sessions (client portal, M365):
    Terminated after 30 minutes of inactivity
  - VPN sessions: Terminated after 60 minutes of inactivity
    OR at end of business day (6:00 PM local time),
    whichever comes first
  - Remote Desktop (RDP) sessions to servers:
    Disconnected after 30 minutes idle, terminated after
    4 hours regardless of activity

2.2 End-of-Day Termination
  - All active VPN sessions are forcibly terminated at
    6:00 PM weekdays and 2:00 PM Saturdays
  - Users working after hours must re-authenticate via VPN

2.3 Role Change or Termination
  - Upon an employee's separation, all active sessions are
    immediately terminated and the account is disabled

2.4 Security Event Termination
  - Upon detection of anomalous activity, the SIEM can
    trigger an automated session kill via Microsoft Defender
    for Identity

3. IMPLEMENTATION
  - Azure AD Conditional Access: token lifetime policies
  - RDP session timeout: GPO (BRTS-RDP-Timeout)
  - VPN: Cisco AnyConnect session timeout configured in
    ASA firewall policy
