1. PURPOSE
This policy controls and monitors the use of Voice over IP
(VoIP) technologies at BRTS to protect CUI discussions and
prevent eavesdropping.

2. APPROVED VOIP TECHNOLOGIES
  - Microsoft Teams Voice (primary): calls made through
    the company M365 tenant
  - Teams calling is encrypted end-to-end (SRTP + TLS)
  - No third-party VoIP clients are approved for CUI discussions

3. PROHIBITED VOIP TECHNOLOGIES
  - Consumer VoIP apps (WhatsApp, FaceTime, Signal) for
    CUI discussions: PROHIBITED
  - Unapproved SIP clients: PROHIBITED
  - VoIP over unencrypted channels: PROHIBITED

4. CUI DISCUSSION CONTROLS
  When discussing CUI over any voice channel:
    - Use Microsoft Teams (encrypted) only
    - Ensure no unauthorized parties can hear the discussion
      (closed door, headphones if in open office)
    - Do not use speakerphone in public areas for CUI calls

5. NETWORK CONSIDERATIONS
  - Teams VoIP traffic uses encrypted SRTP (AES-128 or AES-256)
  - Media traffic flows through Microsoft data centers
    (not directly peer-to-peer in enterprise plans)
  - QoS DSCP markings applied for Teams media traffic
    (no special security risk from QoS marking)

6. MONITORING
  Teams call records are available in the M365 admin center.
  Unusual calling patterns (e.g., bulk external calls) are
  reviewed by Nathan Cruz upon alert from Defender for Cloud Apps.
