PERSONNEL SECURITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.9.1 - Personnel Screening
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC's requirements for screening individuals prior to granting access to CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.9.1.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC employees, contractors, and other personnel who will have access to CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All personnel with CUI access must complete a background check before access is granted.
b. Background check includes: criminal history, employment verification, and reference checks.
c. Results are reviewed and approved by Victor Osei before CUI access is granted.
d. Contractors with CUI access must provide evidence of background screening by their employer.
e. Background check results are retained in a secure personnel file for 5 years.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Victor Osei, Director of Operations, reviews and approves background check results. Nathan Cruz grants CUI access only after approval.

5. ENFORCEMENT
No individual may receive CUI access without a completed and approved background check.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
