SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.14.6 - Security Monitoring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to monitor information systems to detect attacks and indicators of potential attacks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.6.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems and the networks through which CUI flows.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Microsoft Sentinel provides continuous security monitoring and attack detection.
b. Sentinel analytics rules are configured to detect known attack patterns (TTPs).
c. Defender for Endpoint monitors endpoint behavior for attack indicators.
d. Nathan Cruz triages Sentinel incidents within 2 hours during business hours.
e. After-hours critical incidents trigger automated alert to Nathan Cruz via SMS/email.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and monitors attack detection controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unreviewed Sentinel incidents beyond SLA are escalated to Victor Osei.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
