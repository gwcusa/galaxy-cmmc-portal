ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.12 - Remote Access Monitoring and Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC's requirements for monitoring and controlling remote access sessions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.12.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC personnel and contractors who access Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC systems remotely.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All remote access sessions are conducted through the approved VPN solution.
b. Remote sessions are logged including user identity, source IP, and duration.
c. Nathan Cruz reviews remote access logs weekly.
d. Simultaneous remote sessions for a single account are limited to 1.
e. Remote access from non-managed devices is blocked.
f. Anomalous remote access patterns generate immediate security alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, manages the VPN solution and reviews remote access logs.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized remote access attempts are treated as security incidents.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
