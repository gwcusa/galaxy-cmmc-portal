ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.9 - Privacy and Security Notices
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to provide privacy and security notices consistent with CUI rules at system logon, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.9.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC information systems accessible by personnel or contractors.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A logon banner displaying privacy and security notices is displayed before authentication.
b. The banner references CUI handling requirements and consent to monitoring.
c. Nathan Cruz reviews and updates banner text annually or upon regulatory change.
d. Banner text is approved by Victor Osei before deployment.
e. Banner is enforced via Group Policy and Azure AD Conditional Access.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, implements and maintains logon banners across all systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems without compliant logon banners must be remediated within 48 hours of identification.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
