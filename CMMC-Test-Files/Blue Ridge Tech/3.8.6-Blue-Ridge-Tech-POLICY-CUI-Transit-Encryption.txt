MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.8.6 - CUI Encryption in Transit
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to implement cryptographic mechanisms to protect CUI during transmission, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.6.

2. SCOPE
All CUI transmitted electronically or physically by Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI transmitted over networks uses TLS 1.2 or higher encryption.
b. CUI emailed externally uses S/MIME or is protected via encrypted secure file transfer.
c. CUI on portable media uses hardware encryption (AES-256 minimum).
d. Unencrypted transmission of CUI is prohibited.
e. Nathan Cruz verifies transmission encryption controls quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and verifies encryption for all CUI transmission methods.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted CUI transmission is a serious policy violation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
