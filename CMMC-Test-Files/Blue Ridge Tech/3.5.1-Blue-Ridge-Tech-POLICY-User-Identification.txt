1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures all users, processes acting on behalf of
users, and devices are uniquely identified on BRTS systems.

2. USER IDENTIFICATION
  - Every human user has a unique, named Active Directory
    account (e.g., ncruz, vosei, jhartwell)
  - Shared or generic accounts are prohibited
  - User accounts are tied to the individual by HR record
    and are not transferable

3. PROCESS IDENTIFICATION
  - Service accounts are named by function (e.g., svc-backup,
    svc-sentinel-collector)
  - Service accounts are documented in the Service Account
    Register (SharePoint: IT/ServiceAccounts.xlsx)
  - Service accounts cannot be used for interactive logon

4. DEVICE IDENTIFICATION
  - All company devices are enrolled in Microsoft Intune
  - Each device has a unique name (e.g., BRTS-WS-OSEI,
    BRTS-LT-CRUZ) and unique Azure AD Device ID
  - Devices present machine certificates for 802.1X WiFi
    and Conditional Access identification
  - Personal/BYOD devices are not permitted to access
    company CUI systems

5. REVIEW
  Nathan Cruz reviews the user and device inventory
  quarterly against HR records. Departed employees
  have accounts disabled within 24 hours of separation.
