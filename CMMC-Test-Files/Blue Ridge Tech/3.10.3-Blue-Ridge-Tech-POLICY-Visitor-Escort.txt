PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.10.3 - Visitor Escort and Monitoring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to escort visitors and monitor visitor activity in CUI areas, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.3.

2. SCOPE
All visitors (non-Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC employees) entering CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All visitors are signed in via the visitor log before entering CUI areas.
b. Visitors are escorted by authorized Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC personnel at all times in CUI areas.
c. Visitors are never left unattended with CUI systems or documents.
d. Visitor log records name, organization, purpose, escort, entry/exit times.
e. Visitor log is retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Victor Osei or Nathan Cruz escorts all visitors in CUI areas. Nathan Cruz maintains visitor log.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unescorted visitors in CUI areas are a security incident.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
