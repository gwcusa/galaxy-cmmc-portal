1. PURPOSE
This policy requires that media containing CUI is marked with
appropriate CUI markings and distribution limitations.

2. MARKING REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Paper Documents
  - Every page of a paper document containing CUI must be
    marked at the top and bottom: "CUI"
  - The cover page must also include the applicable CUI
    category (e.g., CUI // OPSEC, CUI // PRVCY)
  - Distribution limitation: "DISTRIBUTION LIMITED TO
    AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY"

2.2 Electronic Files
  - Documents in Word, Excel, PDF containing CUI must include
    a "CUI" header in the document header/footer
  - SharePoint CUI site has a default sensitivity label
    "CUI - Confidential" applied to all content
  - Files emailed externally must include "CUI" in the
    email subject line and body

2.3 Physical Media (USB drives, hard drives)
  - CUI-containing USB drives must have a physical label
    affixed: "CUI - HANDLE PER DFARS 252.204-7012"
  - Labels are orange (BRTS standard) to visually distinguish
    CUI media from non-CUI media

2.4 Exclusion
  - Encrypted drives with ONLY operating system files
    (no CUI data) do not require CUI markings
  - Media that has been sanitized of all CUI no longer
    requires markings

3. TRAINING
  Staff are trained annually on CUI marking requirements.
