ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.19 - CUI Encryption on Mobile Devices
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to encrypt CUI on mobile devices and mobile computing platforms, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.19.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC mobile devices and portable computing platforms that store or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All mobile devices storing CUI must enable full-device encryption.
b. Windows laptops use BitLocker with TPM (AES-256).
c. iOS devices use hardware AES encryption (enabled by default with passcode).
d. Android devices enable full-disk encryption.
e. Encryption status is verified through Intune compliance reports.
f. Non-encrypted devices are blocked from CUI access until remediated.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, enforces encryption requirements via Intune compliance policies.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Non-compliant unencrypted devices are quarantined from CUI access within 2 hours.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
