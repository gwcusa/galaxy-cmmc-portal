IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.5.5 - MFA for Privileged Accounts
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to employ multifactor authentication for network access to privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.5.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC privileged accounts (Global Admin, Security Admin, IT Administrator roles).

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. MFA is required every time a privileged account authenticates for network access.
b. Privileged accounts are enrolled in MFA before being activated.
c. PIM-gated privileged access requires MFA at activation.
d. No persistent privileged sessions are permitted; re-authentication is required.
e. Failed privileged account MFA attempts generate immediate alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and enforces MFA for all privileged accounts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any privileged account session without verified MFA is blocked and alerted.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
