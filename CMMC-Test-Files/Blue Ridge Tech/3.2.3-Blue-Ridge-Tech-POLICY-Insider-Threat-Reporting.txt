AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.2.3 - Insider Threat Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC provides security training on recognizing and reporting potential insider threats, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.2.3.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC personnel who may observe or have knowledge of insider threat indicators.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Personnel are trained to recognize physical, behavioral, and technical indicators of insider threat.
b. Reporting mechanisms are clearly communicated and accessible to all personnel.
c. Anonymous reporting is available and confidentiality is maintained.
d. Reports are reviewed by Victor Osei within 24 hours.
e. Training on reporting procedures is provided annually.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz administers technical monitoring. Victor Osei reviews insider threat reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Retaliation against personnel who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
