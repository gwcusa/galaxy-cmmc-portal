IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.5.10 - Cryptographic Password Storage
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to store and transmit only cryptographically protected passwords, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.10.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC systems that store or transmit user passwords.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.
b. Password storage uses strong one-way hashing (bcrypt, PBKDF2, or Argon2).
c. Reversible encryption of passwords is prohibited and technically disabled.
d. Passwords are transmitted only over encrypted channels (TLS 1.2+).
e. Nathan Cruz verifies cryptographic password protection settings quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, ensures password storage and transmission uses cryptographic protection.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems storing passwords in plaintext or reversible encryption are immediately remediated.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
