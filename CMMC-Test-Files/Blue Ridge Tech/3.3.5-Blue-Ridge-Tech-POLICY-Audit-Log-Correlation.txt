AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.3.5 - Audit Log Correlation and Analysis
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to correlate audit record review, analysis, and reporting across organizational processes to gain situational awareness, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.5.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC audit log sources and CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit records from multiple sources are correlated in a centralized SIEM.
b. Correlation rules detect multi-source attack patterns.
c. Nathan Cruz reviews correlation findings weekly.
d. Cross-source incidents are escalated immediately to Victor Osei.
e. Correlation rules are reviewed and updated quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, maintains and monitors the SIEM correlation infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Gaps in log correlation coverage are remediated within 30 days of identification.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
