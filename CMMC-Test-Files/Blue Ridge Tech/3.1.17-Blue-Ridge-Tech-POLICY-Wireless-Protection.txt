ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.17 - Wireless Access Authentication and Encryption
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to protect wireless access using authentication and encryption, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.17.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC wireless networks that support access to CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI wireless networks use WPA3-Enterprise (WPA2-Enterprise minimum) with 802.1X.
b. Encryption uses AES-CCMP (128-bit minimum).
c. WEP and TKIP are prohibited.
d. Wireless authentication certificates are issued by the Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC internal PKI.
e. Nathan Cruz reviews wireless encryption configurations quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and maintains wireless encryption and authentication.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any wireless network in violation of these standards must be taken offline immediately.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
