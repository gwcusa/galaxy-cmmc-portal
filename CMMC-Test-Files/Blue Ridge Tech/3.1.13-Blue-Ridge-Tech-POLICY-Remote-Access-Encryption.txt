ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.13 - Remote Access Encryption
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to employ cryptographic mechanisms to protect the confidentiality of remote access sessions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.13.

2. SCOPE
All remote access connections to Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All remote access sessions use TLS 1.2 or higher encryption.
b. VPN connections use AES-256 encryption minimum.
c. Weak cipher suites (RC4, DES, 3DES, TLS 1.0, TLS 1.1) are disabled.
d. Remote desktop sessions require NLA and TLS encryption.
e. Nathan Cruz reviews VPN and TLS cipher configurations quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and maintains encryption settings for remote access.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted remote access connections are blocked and reported.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
