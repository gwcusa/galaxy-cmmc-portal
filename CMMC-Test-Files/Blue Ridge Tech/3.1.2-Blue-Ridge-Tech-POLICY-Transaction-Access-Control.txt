ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.2 - Transaction and Function Access Limits
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC system access to the types of transactions and functions that authorized users are permitted to execute, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.2.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC information systems and users who access CUI or CUI-supporting systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. User accounts are assigned roles that restrict access to only required functions.
b. Role assignments are documented and reviewed semi-annually by Nathan Cruz.
c. No user shall have access beyond what is required for their job function.
d. Administrative roles are restricted to personnel with a documented operational need.
e. Role changes require written approval before implementation.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, manages role assignments. Victor Osei approves elevated access requests.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized function access is treated as a policy violation subject to disciplinary action.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
