SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.14.4 - Anti-Virus Signature Updates
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to update malware protection mechanisms when new releases are available, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.4.

2. SCOPE
All anti-malware software on Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI endpoints.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Microsoft Defender signatures are updated at least daily (automated via Intune).
b. Definition updates occur automatically; users cannot defer or block updates.
c. Nathan Cruz verifies definition currency via Intune reports weekly.
d. Definitions older than 3 days trigger an alert to Nathan Cruz.
e. Endpoints with outdated definitions are restricted from CUI until updated.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, ensures anti-malware signatures are current on all CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Endpoints with definitions older than 3 days are isolated from the CUI network.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
