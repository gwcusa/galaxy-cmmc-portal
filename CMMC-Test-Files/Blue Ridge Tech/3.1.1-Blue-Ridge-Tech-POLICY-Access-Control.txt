ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.1 - Authorized User Access
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC information system access to authorized users, processes acting on behalf of authorized users, and authorized devices, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.1.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC systems, personnel, and contractors that process, store, or transmit Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Maintain a current authorized user list for each CUI system.
b. Require documented approval before provisioning any account.
c. Nathan Cruz reviews the authorized user list quarterly and removes stale entries.
d. Shared or generic accounts are prohibited without documented exception.
e. Disable accounts within 24 hours of termination or contract expiration.
f. All system access events are logged and monitored.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, is responsible for implementation. Victor Osei, Director of Operations, holds overall accountability.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Policy violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
