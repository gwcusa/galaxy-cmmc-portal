MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.8.7 - Removable Media Use Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the use of removable media on system components, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.7.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI endpoints and the removable media used with them.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Only Nathan Cruz-approved removable media may be used on CUI endpoints.
b. Approved media must be encrypted (AES-256 hardware or software).
c. Intune device control policies block unapproved USB storage devices.
d. A register of approved removable media is maintained by Nathan Cruz.
e. Approved media is inventoried and audited quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, manages the approved media register and Intune device control policies.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved removable media connected to CUI endpoints is blocked and generates a security alert.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
