AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.3.9 - Audit Management Access
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits management of audit logging to a subset of privileged users, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.9.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue audit logging infrastructure and SIEM tools.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Management of audit log configuration is restricted to Mike Sadler only.
b. Standard users have no access to configure, modify, or disable audit logging.
c. Audit management access is reviewed quarterly.
d. Any changes to audit logging configuration require documented justification.
e. Changes to logging configuration generate alerts to Randy Bowles.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, is the sole administrator of the audit logging system.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized changes to audit logging configuration are security incidents.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
