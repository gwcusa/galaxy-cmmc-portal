MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.8.4 - CUI Media Marking
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to mark media with necessary CUI markings and distribution limitations, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.4.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty physical and digital media containing CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Physical media containing CUI is labeled with "CUI" on the media and its container.
b. CUI documents include CUI header/footer markings.
c. Digital files containing CUI include CUI designation in the file name or metadata.
d. Media labels are applied by Sandra Okonkwo before the media is used to store CUI.
e. Sandra Okonkwo verifies CUI markings quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, applies and verifies CUI markings on all CUI media.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unmarked CUI media is a compliance finding requiring immediate remediation.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
