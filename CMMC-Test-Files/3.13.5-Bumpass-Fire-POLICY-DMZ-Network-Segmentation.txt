1. PURPOSE
This policy implements subnetworks for publicly accessible
system components that are physically or logically separated
from internal networks containing CUI.

2. NETWORK SEGMENTATION DESIGN

2.1 Internal Network (CUI Zone)
  VLAN 10 - 192.168.10.0/24
  - CUI workstations and servers
  - Domain controllers
  - NAS with CUI data
  - No direct internet access (all internet via proxy/firewall)

2.2 Guest Network (Public Zone)
  VLAN 20 - 192.168.20.0/24
  - Visitor WiFi
  - Internet access only
  - NO access to VLAN 10 (CUI zone)
  - Firewall rule: DENY VLAN 20 -> VLAN 10 (explicit)

2.3 IoT / Physical Security Zone
  VLAN 30 - 192.168.30.0/24
  - Security cameras
  - Smart locks and access control hardware
  - Internet access to vendor cloud only (limited ports)
  - NO access to VLAN 10 or VLAN 20

2.4 No Public-Facing Servers
  BFR does not host any publicly accessible web servers or
  services on-premises. The public website is externally
  hosted (SaaS). Therefore, no DMZ is required for servers.
  Public-facing content managed by vendor (no CUI).

3. FIREWALL ENFORCEMENT
  Cisco ASA controls inter-VLAN routing.
  VLANs are separate broadcast domains (Cisco Meraki switches).
