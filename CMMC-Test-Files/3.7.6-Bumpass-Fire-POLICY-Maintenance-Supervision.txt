MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.7.6 - Maintenance Personnel Supervision
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to supervise maintenance activities of personnel without required access authorization, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.6.

2. SCOPE
All maintenance personnel who do not have CUI access authorization but require physical or logical access to perform maintenance.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Unauthorized maintenance personnel are supervised by Mike Sadler at all times.
b. Supervision includes monitoring all activities within CUI areas.
c. Supervised personnel may not be left unattended with CUI systems.
d. If supervision is not possible, CUI systems are powered off and locked before maintenance begins.
e. Supervision activities are documented in the maintenance log.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, is responsible for supervising all unauthorized maintenance personnel.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unattended unauthorized maintenance in CUI areas is a security incident.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
