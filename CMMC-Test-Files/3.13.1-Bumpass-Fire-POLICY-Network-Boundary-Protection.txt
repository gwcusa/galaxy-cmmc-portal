SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.13.1 - Network Boundary Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to monitor, control, and protect communications at external and internal network boundaries, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.1.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue network boundaries separating CUI systems from external networks.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Firewalls protect all network boundaries between CUI systems and external networks.
b. Intrusion Prevention System (IPS) monitors traffic at network boundaries.
c. Traffic is logged and reviewed for anomalies.
d. Default-deny rules are in place; only approved traffic is allowed.
e. Firewall rules are reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, manages all boundary protection devices and configurations.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized network boundary changes are security incidents requiring immediate investigation.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
