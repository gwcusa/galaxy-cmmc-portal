ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.8 - Unsuccessful Logon Attempt Limits
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits unsuccessful logon attempts at Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to protect against brute-force attacks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.8.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC systems and devices that authenticate users with CUI system access.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed logon attempts.
b. Lockout duration is 30 minutes or until manually unlocked by Nathan Cruz.
c. Account lockout thresholds are enforced via Group Policy and Azure AD.
d. Lockout events generate a security alert reviewed by Nathan Cruz.
e. Failed logon attempts are retained in audit logs for 90 days.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and monitors account lockout settings.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Tampering with lockout configurations is a security policy violation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
