CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.4.8 - Application Allowlisting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to apply deny-by-default and allow-by-exception policies to prevent use of unauthorized software, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.8.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI endpoints and servers.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Only approved applications may execute on CUI endpoints.
b. Application allowlisting is enforced via Windows Defender Application Control (WDAC).
c. All new software requests require review and approval by Nathan Cruz and Victor Osei.
d. The approved application list is reviewed and updated quarterly.
e. Attempts to execute unapproved software generate security alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, creates and maintains the application allowlist and WDAC policy.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Execution of unapproved software is blocked automatically and generates an alert.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
