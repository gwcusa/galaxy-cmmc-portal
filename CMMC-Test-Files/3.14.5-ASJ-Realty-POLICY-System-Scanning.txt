SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.14.5 - System and File Scanning
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to perform periodic scans of information systems and real-time scans of files from external sources as files are downloaded, opened, or executed, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.5.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty CUI endpoints and files.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Real-time scanning is enabled on all CUI endpoints (files scanned on access).
b. Weekly full-disk scans are scheduled on all CUI workstations.
c. Files downloaded from email or internet are scanned before execution.
d. Scan results are reviewed by Sandra Okonkwo weekly.
e. Scan history is retained for 90 days.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, configures and monitors scanning on all CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Endpoints with scanning disabled are isolated from CUI until remediated.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
