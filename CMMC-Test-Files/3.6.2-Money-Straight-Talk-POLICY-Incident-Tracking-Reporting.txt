1. PURPOSE
This policy requires MST to track, document, and report
security incidents to appropriate officials within required
timeframes.

2. INCIDENT DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
For every security incident, the following must be documented:
  - Date and time of discovery
  - Date and time of occurrence (if different)
  - Description of the incident
  - Systems and data affected
  - CUI involved (yes/no and what type if yes)
  - Immediate actions taken
  - Root cause (once determined)
  - Resolution and recovery steps
  - Lessons learned and corrective actions

3. INTERNAL REPORTING CHAIN
  - Discovery: Any staff member
  - Immediate notification: Terrell Banks (within 1 hour)
  - Escalation: Camille Odom (within 2 hours)
  - For CUI-involved incidents: Government Contracting Officer
    must be notified within 72 hours (per DFARS 252.204-7012)

4. EXTERNAL REPORTING
  - DoD/DIBNet report: submitted within 72 hours of discovering
    a cyber incident affecting CUI
  - US-CERT report: if required by incident type

5. TRACKING
  All incidents tracked in the MST IT Incident Register
  (SharePoint: IT/Incident-Register.xlsx). Incidents remain
  open until resolution is confirmed and documented.
