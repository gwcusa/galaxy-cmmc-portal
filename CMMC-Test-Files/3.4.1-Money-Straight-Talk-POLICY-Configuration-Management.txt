CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.4.1 - Configuration Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Money Straight Talk LLC's requirements for maintaining baseline configurations of information systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.1.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC hardware, software, and information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Terrell Banks maintains an inventory of all hardware and software in the CUI environment.
b. Baseline configurations are documented for all CUI systems.
c. The inventory is reviewed and updated quarterly.
d. New devices must be approved and configured per baseline before connecting to CUI networks.
e. Deviations from baseline require documented justification and approval.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, maintains the asset inventory and baseline configurations.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized devices on the CUI network are removed immediately upon detection.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
