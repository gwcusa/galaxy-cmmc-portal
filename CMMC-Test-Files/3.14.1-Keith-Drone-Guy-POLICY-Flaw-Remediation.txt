SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.14.1 - Flaw Remediation
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to identify, report, and correct information system flaws in a timely manner, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.1.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC CUI systems and software.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All software and operating systems receive security patches per vulnerability SLA.
b. Critical patches (CVSS 9.0+): applied within 72 hours.
c. High patches (CVSS 7.0-8.9): applied within 7 days.
d. Medium/Low: applied within 30-90 days.
e. Patch status is monitored via Intune and reported monthly to Keith Merritt.
f. Unpatched systems are isolated from CUI until patched.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, manages patch deployment. Keith Merritt reviews monthly patch reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with critical unpatched vulnerabilities beyond 72 hours are removed from CUI network.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
