MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.7.5 - Remote Maintenance MFA
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to require MFA to establish remote maintenance sessions and to terminate such connections after maintenance, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.5.

2. SCOPE
All remote maintenance sessions on Money Straight Talk LLC CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Remote maintenance sessions require MFA before the session is established.
b. Session credentials are unique to the maintenance session and not reused.
c. Remote maintenance connections are terminated immediately after the session ends.
d. Remote sessions are logged with start time, end time, and actions performed.
e. Terrell Banks monitors all remote maintenance sessions in real-time.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages and monitors remote maintenance sessions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Remote maintenance sessions without MFA are blocked and investigated.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
