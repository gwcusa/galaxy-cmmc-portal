MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.8.6 - CUI Encryption in Transit
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to implement cryptographic mechanisms to protect CUI during transmission, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.6.

2. SCOPE
All CUI transmitted electronically or physically by Keith the Drone Guy LLC.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI transmitted over networks uses TLS 1.2 or higher encryption.
b. CUI emailed externally uses S/MIME or is protected via encrypted secure file transfer.
c. CUI on portable media uses hardware encryption (AES-256 minimum).
d. Unencrypted transmission of CUI is prohibited.
e. Keith Merritt verifies transmission encryption controls quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures and verifies encryption for all CUI transmission methods.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unencrypted CUI transmission is a serious policy violation.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
