INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.6.3 - Incident Response Testing
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to test incident response capabilities to determine their effectiveness, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.3.

2. SCOPE
The Money Straight Talk LLC Incident Response Plan and all personnel with IR responsibilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. An IR tabletop exercise is conducted annually.
b. The exercise tests at least one realistic threat scenario relevant to Money Straight Talk LLC's environment.
c. Exercise results are documented and reviewed by Camille Odom.
d. Identified gaps are tracked in a remediation plan.
e. Remediation items are completed within 90 days of the exercise.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, designs and facilitates IR exercises. Camille Odom participates and approves results.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Annual IR testing is mandatory; failure to test results in a security program finding.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
