MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.7.6 - Maintenance Personnel Supervision
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to supervise maintenance activities of personnel without required access authorization, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.6.

2. SCOPE
All maintenance personnel who do not have CUI access authorization but require physical or logical access to perform maintenance.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Unauthorized maintenance personnel are supervised by Keith Merritt at all times.
b. Supervision includes monitoring all activities within CUI areas.
c. Supervised personnel may not be left unattended with CUI systems.
d. If supervision is not possible, CUI systems are powered off and locked before maintenance begins.
e. Supervision activities are documented in the maintenance log.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, is responsible for supervising all unauthorized maintenance personnel.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unattended unauthorized maintenance in CUI areas is a security incident.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
