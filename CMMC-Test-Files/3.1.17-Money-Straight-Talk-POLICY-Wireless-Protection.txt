ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.1.17 - Wireless Access Authentication and Encryption
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to protect wireless access using authentication and encryption, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.17.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC wireless networks that support access to CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI wireless networks use WPA3-Enterprise (WPA2-Enterprise minimum) with 802.1X.
b. Encryption uses AES-CCMP (128-bit minimum).
c. WEP and TKIP are prohibited.
d. Wireless authentication certificates are issued by the Money Straight Talk LLC internal PKI.
e. Terrell Banks reviews wireless encryption configurations quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, configures and maintains wireless encryption and authentication.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any wireless network in violation of these standards must be taken offline immediately.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
