PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.10.3 - Visitor Escort and Monitoring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to escort visitors and monitor visitor activity in CUI areas, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.3.

2. SCOPE
All visitors (non-ASJ Realty employees) entering CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All visitors are signed in via the visitor log before entering CUI areas.
b. Visitors are escorted by authorized ASJ Realty personnel at all times in CUI areas.
c. Visitors are never left unattended with CUI systems or documents.
d. Visitor log records name, organization, purpose, escort, entry/exit times.
e. Visitor log is retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Anthony James or Sandra Okonkwo escorts all visitors in CUI areas. Sandra Okonkwo maintains visitor log.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unescorted visitors in CUI areas are a security incident.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
