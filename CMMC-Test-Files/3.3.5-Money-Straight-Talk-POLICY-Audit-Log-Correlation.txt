AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.3.5 - Audit Log Correlation and Analysis
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to correlate audit record review, analysis, and reporting across organizational processes to gain situational awareness, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.5.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC audit log sources and CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit records from multiple sources are correlated in a centralized SIEM.
b. Correlation rules detect multi-source attack patterns.
c. Terrell Banks reviews correlation findings weekly.
d. Cross-source incidents are escalated immediately to Camille Odom.
e. Correlation rules are reviewed and updated quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, maintains and monitors the SIEM correlation infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Gaps in log correlation coverage are remediated within 30 days of identification.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
