SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.14.6 - Security Monitoring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to monitor information systems to detect attacks and indicators of potential attacks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.6.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems and the networks through which CUI flows.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Microsoft Sentinel provides continuous security monitoring and attack detection.
b. Sentinel analytics rules are configured to detect known attack patterns (TTPs).
c. Defender for Endpoint monitors endpoint behavior for attack indicators.
d. Mike Sadler triages Sentinel incidents within 2 hours during business hours.
e. After-hours critical incidents trigger automated alert to Mike Sadler via SMS/email.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and monitors attack detection controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unreviewed Sentinel incidents beyond SLA are escalated to Randy Bowles.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
