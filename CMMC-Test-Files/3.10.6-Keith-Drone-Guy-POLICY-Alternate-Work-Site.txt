PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.10.6 - Alternate Work Site Security
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes security requirements for Keith the Drone Guy LLC personnel working at alternate work sites (e.g., home offices), per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.6.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC personnel who work remotely or from alternate sites with access to CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Personnel must sign a remote work agreement before accessing CUI from alternate sites.
b. Remote work environments must include: locked workspace, encrypted devices, VPN use.
c. Printers at alternate sites may not be used to print CUI without explicit approval.
d. CUI documents may not be left visible in unsecured home office areas.
e. Keith Merritt conducts annual remote work security reviews for all remote-capable staff.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, provides remote work security guidance and Keith Merritt approves alternate work arrangements.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Personnel working from unsecured alternate sites with CUI are in violation of this policy.
