AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.3.3 - Audit Event Review, Analysis, and Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to review, analyze, and report audit logs to identify inappropriate or unusual activity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.3.

2. SCOPE
All CUI system audit logs collected by ASJ Realty.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logs are reviewed weekly by Sandra Okonkwo for anomalous activity.
b. Automated correlation rules detect and alert on suspicious patterns.
c. Anomalies are investigated and documented within 24 hours.
d. Monthly audit summary reports are provided to Anthony James.
e. Review findings are retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, conducts weekly log reviews and responds to alerts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to review logs per schedule is reported to Anthony James for corrective action.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
