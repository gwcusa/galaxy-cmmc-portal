1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes requirements for developing and
implementing Plans of Action and Milestones (POA&M) to
correct deficiencies in security controls and reduce
or eliminate vulnerabilities.

2. POA&M REQUIREMENT
A POA&M must be created for every security control deficiency
identified through:
  - Annual security control assessments
  - Vulnerability scans
  - Incident investigations
  - Audits or inspections

3. POA&M CONTENTS
Each POA&M item must include:
  a) Control reference (e.g., 3.1.1, 3.5.3)
  b) Weakness or deficiency description
  c) Current risk level (Critical/High/Medium/Low)
  d) Planned mitigation/corrective action
  e) Assigned owner
  f) Target completion date
  g) Resources required (budget, staff)
  h) Milestone schedule (intermediate steps for multi-month items)
  i) Current status (Open / In Progress / Closed)

4. POA&M REVIEW
  - Sandra Okonkwo reviews open POA&M items monthly
  - Anthony James reviews the POA&M quarterly
  - All POA&M items must have a target date no more than
    12 months after identification (critical: 30 days)
  - Closed items are retained with evidence of completion

5. STORAGE
  POA&M document maintained in SharePoint: IT/CMMC/POA&M.xlsx
