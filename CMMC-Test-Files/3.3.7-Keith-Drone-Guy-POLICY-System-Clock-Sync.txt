AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.3.7 - System Clock Synchronization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to provide a system capability that compares and synchronizes internal system clocks with an authoritative source, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.7.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC information systems that generate audit records.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All systems synchronize clocks with an authoritative NTP time source.
b. NTP source: time.windows.com or NIST time servers (time.nist.gov).
c. Maximum clock drift is 1 second from authoritative source.
d. NTP configuration is enforced via Group Policy on Windows endpoints.
e. Keith Merritt verifies clock synchronization quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures and verifies NTP synchronization on all systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with clock drift exceeding 5 minutes are quarantined from CUI access.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
