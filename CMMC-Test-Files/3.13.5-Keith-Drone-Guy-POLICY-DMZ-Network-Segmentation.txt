SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.13.5 - Network Segmentation
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to implement subnetworks for publicly accessible system components that are physically or logically separated from internal networks, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.5.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC network segments in the CUI environment.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI systems reside in a separate, isolated network segment (CUI VLAN).
b. Guest/public WiFi is isolated from CUI network on a separate VLAN.
c. Publicly accessible systems (if any) are placed in a DMZ, separate from CUI.
d. VLAN segmentation is enforced by managed switches and firewall rules.
e. Keith Merritt reviews VLAN configurations quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, designs and maintains network segmentation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
CUI systems on non-CUI network segments must be remediated within 24 hours.
