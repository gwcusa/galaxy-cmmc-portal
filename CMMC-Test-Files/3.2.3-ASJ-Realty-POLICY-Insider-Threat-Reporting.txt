AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.2.3 - Insider Threat Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures ASJ Realty provides security training on recognizing and reporting potential insider threats, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.2.3.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty personnel who may observe or have knowledge of insider threat indicators.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Personnel are trained to recognize physical, behavioral, and technical indicators of insider threat.
b. Reporting mechanisms are clearly communicated and accessible to all personnel.
c. Anonymous reporting is available and confidentiality is maintained.
d. Reports are reviewed by Anthony James within 24 hours.
e. Training on reporting procedures is provided annually.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo administers technical monitoring. Anthony James reviews insider threat reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Retaliation against personnel who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
