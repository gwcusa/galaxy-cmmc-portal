MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.8.3 - Media Sanitization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to sanitize or destroy information system media before disposal or reuse, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.3.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC digital and physical media prior to disposal, reuse, or transfer.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Media sanitization follows NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1 Guidelines for Media Sanitization.
b. Hard drives and SSDs are wiped using DoD 5220.22-M standard or secure erase.
c. Media that cannot be reliably sanitized is physically destroyed.
d. Destruction/sanitization is documented in the media disposal log.
e. A certificate of destruction is obtained for third-party media disposal.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, performs and documents all media sanitization activities.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unsanitized media disposal is a serious security violation.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
