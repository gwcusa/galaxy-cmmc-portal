AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.3.9 - Audit Management Access
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits management of audit logging to a subset of privileged users, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.9.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC audit logging infrastructure and SIEM tools.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Management of audit log configuration is restricted to Terrell Banks only.
b. Standard users have no access to configure, modify, or disable audit logging.
c. Audit management access is reviewed quarterly.
d. Any changes to audit logging configuration require documented justification.
e. Changes to logging configuration generate alerts to Camille Odom.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, is the sole administrator of the audit logging system.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized changes to audit logging configuration are security incidents.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
