MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.7.1 - System Maintenance
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC's requirements for performing maintenance on information systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.1.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC hardware and software systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Maintenance activities are scheduled and documented in advance.
b. Security patches are applied within 30 days of release (critical: 72 hours).
c. Maintenance windows are communicated to Victor Osei before scheduled downtime.
d. Post-maintenance verification confirms system functionality and security settings.
e. All maintenance activities are logged with date, type, and personnel involved.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, schedules and performs all system maintenance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Overdue patches trigger automated alerts and escalation to Victor Osei after 30 days.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
