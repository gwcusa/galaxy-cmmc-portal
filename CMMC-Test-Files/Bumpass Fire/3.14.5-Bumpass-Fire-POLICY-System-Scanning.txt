SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.14.5 - System and File Scanning
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to perform periodic scans of information systems and real-time scans of files from external sources as files are downloaded, opened, or executed, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.5.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI endpoints and files.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Real-time scanning is enabled on all CUI endpoints (files scanned on access).
b. Weekly full-disk scans are scheduled on all CUI workstations.
c. Files downloaded from email or internet are scanned before execution.
d. Scan results are reviewed by Mike Sadler weekly.
e. Scan history is retained for 90 days.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and monitors scanning on all CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Endpoints with scanning disabled are isolated from CUI until remediated.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
