INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.6.1 - Incident Response Capability
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Bumpass Fire & Rescue's capability to handle information security incidents, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.1.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue information systems and personnel.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Bumpass Fire & Rescue maintains a documented Incident Response Plan (IRP).
b. The IRP defines roles, responsibilities, and procedures for incident handling.
c. Mike Sadler is the primary incident responder; Randy Bowles provides management escalation.
d. The IRP is tested annually via tabletop exercise.
e. Incident response contacts include US-CERT reporting capability for CUI-related incidents.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, leads incident response. Randy Bowles approves the IRP and provides executive escalation.

5. ENFORCEMENT
All personnel must report suspected security incidents to Mike Sadler within 1 hour of discovery.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
