SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.13.12 - Collaborative Computing Devices
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to prohibit remote activation of collaborative computing devices and provide indication of use to present users, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.12.

2. SCOPE
All collaborative computing devices (cameras, microphones, screen sharing) at Bumpass Fire & Rescue.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Cameras and microphones cannot be remotely activated without user awareness.
b. Physical privacy covers are used on CUI workstations.
c. Camera hardware indicators (LED) must be active when in use.
d. Screen sharing requires explicit user action and consent.
e. CUI spaces do not have always-on listening devices (smart speakers, voice assistants).

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, enforces collaborative device policies through GPO and physical controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized camera/microphone activation is a critical incident.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
