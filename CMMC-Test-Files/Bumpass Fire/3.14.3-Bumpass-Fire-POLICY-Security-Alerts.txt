SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.14.3 - Security Alerts and Advisories
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to monitor security alerts and advisories from authoritative sources and take appropriate actions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.3.

2. SCOPE
All security threat intelligence and advisory monitoring for Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Mike Sadler subscribes to US-CERT/CISA alerts and advisories.
b. Microsoft Security Response Center (MSRC) advisories are monitored for M365 and Windows.
c. Relevant alerts are reviewed within 24 hours of receipt.
d. Emergency/critical advisories trigger immediate response per the Incident Response Plan.
e. Advisory responses are logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, monitors security alerts and coordinates response.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to respond to critical advisories within 24 hours is escalated to Randy Bowles.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
