AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.3.2 - User Activity Audit Traceability
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Bumpass Fire & Rescue audit logs can be traced to individual users and processes to support accountability, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.2.

2. SCOPE
All CUI systems at Bumpass Fire & Rescue where user activity occurs.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit records include user identity, timestamp, event type, source IP, and outcome.
b. Shared accounts are prohibited to ensure individual traceability.
c. User session IDs are included in all audit events.
d. Audit records are sufficient to reconstruct user actions during an investigation.
e. Mike Sadler tests traceability during quarterly log reviews.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, ensures log fields capture sufficient data for traceability.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with insufficient audit traceability must be remediated within 30 days.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
