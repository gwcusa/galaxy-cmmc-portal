ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.1 - Authorized User Access
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits Bumpass Fire & Rescue information system access to authorized users, processes acting on behalf of authorized users, and authorized devices, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.1.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue systems, personnel, and contractors that process, store, or transmit Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Maintain a current authorized user list for each CUI system.
b. Require documented approval before provisioning any account.
c. Mike Sadler reviews the authorized user list quarterly and removes stale entries.
d. Shared or generic accounts are prohibited without documented exception.
e. Disable accounts within 24 hours of termination or contract expiration.
f. All system access events are logged and monitored.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, is responsible for implementation. Randy Bowles, Fire Chief, holds overall accountability.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Policy violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
