CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.4.9 - User-Installed Software Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls and monitors user-installed software on Bumpass Fire & Rescue information systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.9.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI endpoints and systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Standard users do not have local administrator rights on CUI endpoints.
b. All software must be installed by Mike Sadler from the approved software list.
c. Mike Sadler scans endpoints monthly for unapproved or newly installed software.
d. Unapproved software is removed within 24 hours of detection.
e. Personnel are prohibited from installing personal software on Bumpass Fire & Rescue systems.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, controls software installation and conducts monthly software audits.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Personnel who install unauthorized software receive disciplinary action.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
