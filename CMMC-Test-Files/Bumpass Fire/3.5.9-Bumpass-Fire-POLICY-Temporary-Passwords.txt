IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.5.9 - Temporary Password Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy allows Bumpass Fire & Rescue to employ temporary passwords with an immediate change requirement upon first use, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.9.

2. SCOPE
All temporary, one-time, or provisioning passwords issued by Bumpass Fire & Rescue.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Temporary passwords require immediate change upon first login.
b. Temporary passwords expire within 24 hours if not used.
c. Temporary passwords are communicated to users via secure channel (encrypted email or in-person).
d. Mike Sadler logs all temporary password issuances.
e. Accounts with temporary passwords that expire unused are reviewed within 48 hours.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, issues and tracks temporary passwords.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Temporary passwords that are not changed within 24 hours result in account lockout.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
