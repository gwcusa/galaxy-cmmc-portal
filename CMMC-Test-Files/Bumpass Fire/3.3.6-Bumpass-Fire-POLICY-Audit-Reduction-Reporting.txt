AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.3.6 - Audit Reduction and Report Generation
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to provide audit reduction and report generation capabilities that support on-demand analysis and reporting without altering the original audit records, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.6.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue audit log sources and SIEM infrastructure.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A SIEM is deployed capable of filtering, sorting, and summarizing audit records.
b. Reports are generated without altering original log records.
c. Mike Sadler can produce audit reports on demand for specific users, timeframes, or events.
d. Audit reduction tools operate on copies, not originals.
e. Report generation capability is tested quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, maintains audit reduction and reporting capabilities.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Audit reduction tools must not modify original log data under any circumstances.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
