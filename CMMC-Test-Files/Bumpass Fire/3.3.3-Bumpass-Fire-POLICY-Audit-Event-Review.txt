AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.3.3 - Audit Event Review, Analysis, and Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to review, analyze, and report audit logs to identify inappropriate or unusual activity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.3.

2. SCOPE
All CUI system audit logs collected by Bumpass Fire & Rescue.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logs are reviewed weekly by Mike Sadler for anomalous activity.
b. Automated correlation rules detect and alert on suspicious patterns.
c. Anomalies are investigated and documented within 24 hours.
d. Monthly audit summary reports are provided to Randy Bowles.
e. Review findings are retained for 1 year.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, conducts weekly log reviews and responds to alerts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to review logs per schedule is reported to Randy Bowles for corrective action.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
