SECURITY ASSESSMENT POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.12.2 - Plan of Action and Milestones
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to develop and implement plans of action to correct deficiencies and reduce vulnerabilities in CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.12.2.

2. SCOPE
All identified deficiencies in Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI system security controls.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A POA&M is maintained for all identified security control deficiencies.
b. Each POA&M entry includes: finding, remediation plan, responsible party, and milestone dates.
c. POA&M is reviewed and updated quarterly.
d. Completed items are marked closed with evidence of remediation.
e. Randy Bowles approves the POA&M quarterly and accepts residual risk.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, maintains the POA&M. Randy Bowles reviews and approves it quarterly.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Overdue POA&M items without approved extensions are escalated immediately to Randy Bowles.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
