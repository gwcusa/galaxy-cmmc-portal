CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.4.6 - Least Functionality
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to configure information systems to provide only essential capabilities, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.6.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue information systems and devices in the CUI environment.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Systems are configured with only the functions and services required for their mission.
b. Unnecessary services, ports, and applications are disabled or removed.
c. Default accounts and sample applications are disabled before deployment.
d. Mike Sadler audits running services on all CUI systems quarterly.
e. Any service additions require documented approval from Randy Bowles.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures systems to least functionality standard and maintains service inventories.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized services discovered during audits are disabled within 24 hours.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
