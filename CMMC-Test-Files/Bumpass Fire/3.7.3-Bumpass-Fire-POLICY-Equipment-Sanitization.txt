MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.7.3 - Equipment Sanitization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to ensure that equipment removed for off-site maintenance is sanitized before release, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.3.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI system equipment that is removed from the facility for repair or maintenance.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All CUI data is removed from equipment before it leaves Bumpass Fire & Rescue custody.
b. If data cannot be removed, the equipment is escorted and not left unattended.
c. Sanitization follows NIST SP 800-88 Guidelines for Media Sanitization.
d. Sanitization is documented in the equipment maintenance log.
e. Equipment returning from off-site maintenance is inspected and re-scanned before reconnection.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, performs or verifies equipment sanitization before off-site release.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Equipment leaving the facility with CUI without sanitization is a security incident.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
