1. PURPOSE
Bumpass Fire & Rescue handles sensitive federal grant data,
personnel records, and emergency communications systems covered
under CUI. This policy ensures all personnel access only the
minimum information and system capabilities required to perform
their job duties.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all firefighters, EMTs, administrative staff,
volunteers, and any contractors accessing department systems
including the CAD (Computer-Aided Dispatch) system, records
management system (RMS), and grant administration portal.

3. LEAST PRIVILEGE REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Role Definitions
The following access roles are defined for department systems:

  ROLE: Firefighter/EMT
  - Read access to incident reports for own station
  - No access to personnel records or financial data
  - CAD: Respond/Update own calls only

  ROLE: Station Captain
  - Read/write access to incident reports for own station
  - Read access to personnel records for own crew
  - CAD: Full access for assigned station

  ROLE: Administrative Staff
  - Access to grant portal and financial systems only
  - No access to CAD dispatch functions
  - Read-only access to incident statistics

  ROLE: IT Coordinator
  - Full system administration access
  - Maintains separate standard user account for daily tasks
  - Admin account used only for administrative functions

  ROLE: Fire Chief
  - Read access to all systems
  - Approve access change requests

3.2 Privileged Accounts
- Privileged accounts are limited to the IT Coordinator only
- A separate non-privileged account is used for email and
  daily non-administrative tasks
- Privileged sessions are logged and reviewed monthly

3.3 Access Review
- Access roles are reviewed every 6 months
- Any role not used in 60 days is flagged for removal
- New roles require Chief approval before creation

3.4 Separation
- No single user may have both financial approval AND
  payment processing rights
- Dispatch and personnel records access cannot be combined
  in any single non-IT account

4. ENFORCEMENT
Violations result in immediate account suspension pending
review by the Fire Chief and IT Coordinator.
