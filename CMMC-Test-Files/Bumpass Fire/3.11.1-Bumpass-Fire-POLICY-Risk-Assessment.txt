RISK ASSESSMENT POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.11.1 - Risk Assessment
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to periodically assess the risk to organizational operations from the operation of information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.11.1.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue information systems and processes involving CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A formal risk assessment is conducted at least annually.
b. The risk assessment identifies threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and impact.
c. Risks are documented in a Risk Register maintained by Mike Sadler.
d. Randy Bowles reviews and approves the risk assessment findings.
e. Risk assessment results drive remediation priorities.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, conducts the risk assessment. Randy Bowles reviews and approves results.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to complete annual risk assessment is reported to Randy Bowles as a compliance gap.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
