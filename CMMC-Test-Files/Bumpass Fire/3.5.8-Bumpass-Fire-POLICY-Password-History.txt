IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.5.8 - Password Reuse Prohibition
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy prohibits password reuse for a specified number of generations at Bumpass Fire & Rescue, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.8.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue user accounts with access to CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Users may not reuse any of their previous 24 passwords.
b. Password history is enforced technically and cannot be bypassed by users.
c. Administrators may not manually set a password that matches a historical password.
d. Password history enforcement is verified during quarterly access reviews.
e. Service account passwords are rotated every 90 days and history is maintained.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures password history enforcement via Azure AD.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Password history is technically enforced; no override is permitted.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
