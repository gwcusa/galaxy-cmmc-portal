AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.3.7 - System Clock Synchronization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to provide a system capability that compares and synchronizes internal system clocks with an authoritative source, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.7.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue information systems that generate audit records.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All systems synchronize clocks with an authoritative NTP time source.
b. NTP source: time.windows.com or NIST time servers (time.nist.gov).
c. Maximum clock drift is 1 second from authoritative source.
d. NTP configuration is enforced via Group Policy on Windows endpoints.
e. Mike Sadler verifies clock synchronization quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and verifies NTP synchronization on all systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with clock drift exceeding 5 minutes are quarantined from CUI access.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
