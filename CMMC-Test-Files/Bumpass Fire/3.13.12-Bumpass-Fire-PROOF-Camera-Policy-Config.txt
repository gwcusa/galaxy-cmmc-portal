1. SUMMARY
Evidence that Bumpass Fire & Rescue controls collaborative computing devices to prevent unauthorized remote activation.

2. CAMERA AND MICROPHONE CONTROLS

Group Policy - Camera Control:
GPO: CollaborativeDevice-Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Setting: Allow applications to access the camera
Path: Computer Configuration > Policies > Windows Settings > Security Settings > Application Control
Status: Camera accessible ONLY to approved apps (Microsoft Teams). All others blocked.

Physical Controls:
BFR-WS-001: Logitech C920 webcam with physical privacy shutter (shutter closed when not in meeting)
WS-002: Built-in laptop camera - Physical privacy cover installed (manufacturer's physical switch)

3. MICROPHONE CONTROLS
Microphone access restricted to Microsoft Teams only via GPO (Intune app permission policy)
Microsoft Teams settings: Camera and microphone require user to manually enable in each meeting

4. SMART SPEAKER PROHIBITION
Policy statement: No smart speakers (Amazon Echo, Google Home) or always-on voice assistants permitted in CUI work areas.
Attestation signed by: All personnel (stored in HR file)

5. SCREEN SHARING CONTROLS
Screen sharing disabled by default in Microsoft Teams (host must enable per meeting)
Screen sharing requires: Presenter role explicitly assigned by meeting organizer

6. CAMERA LED INDICATOR VERIFICATION
Test: 2026-06-30  |  Tester: Mike Sadler
Result: Camera LED activates on BFR-WS-001 when Teams meeting is joined. LED off at all other times. PASS.

7. CONCLUSION
Bumpass Fire & Rescue controls collaborative devices via GPO restrictions, physical privacy covers, and user-consent-only activation per control 3.13.12.
