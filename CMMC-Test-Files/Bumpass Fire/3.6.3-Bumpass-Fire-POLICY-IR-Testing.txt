INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.6.3 - Incident Response Testing
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to test incident response capabilities to determine their effectiveness, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.3.

2. SCOPE
The Bumpass Fire & Rescue Incident Response Plan and all personnel with IR responsibilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. An IR tabletop exercise is conducted annually.
b. The exercise tests at least one realistic threat scenario relevant to Bumpass Fire & Rescue's environment.
c. Exercise results are documented and reviewed by Randy Bowles.
d. Identified gaps are tracked in a remediation plan.
e. Remediation items are completed within 90 days of the exercise.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, designs and facilitates IR exercises. Randy Bowles participates and approves results.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Annual IR testing is mandatory; failure to test results in a security program finding.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
