AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.2.1 - Security Awareness Training
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Bumpass Fire & Rescue provides security awareness training to personnel to make them aware of security risks associated with their activities and organizational policies, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.2.1.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue personnel, contractors, and any individual with access to CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All personnel complete security awareness training upon hire and annually thereafter.
b. Training covers CUI handling, phishing recognition, password hygiene, and reporting.
c. Training completion is documented and tracked by Mike Sadler.
d. Personnel who do not complete training within 30 days of the due date have CUI access suspended.
e. Training content is reviewed and updated annually.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, administers the training program. Randy Bowles ensures organizational compliance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
CUI access is revoked for personnel with overdue training.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
