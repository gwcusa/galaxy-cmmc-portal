ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.22 - CUI on Publicly Accessible Systems
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls CUI posted or processed on publicly accessible Bumpass Fire & Rescue systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.22.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue publicly accessible systems including websites, social media, and public-facing portals.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI is prohibited from being posted on any publicly accessible system.
b. Randy Bowles must review and approve all content before it is published publicly.
c. Mike Sadler conducts quarterly scans of public-facing systems for inadvertent CUI exposure.
d. Discovered CUI on public systems is removed within 2 hours and reported to management.
e. Staff are trained annually on CUI handling and prohibition of public posting.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler scans public systems. Randy Bowles approves public content. All staff are responsible for not posting CUI.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Posting CUI to publicly accessible systems is a serious policy violation subject to immediate investigation.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
