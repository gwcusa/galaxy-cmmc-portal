1. PURPOSE
This policy requires BFR to identify, report, and correct
information system flaws in a timely manner.

2. FLAW IDENTIFICATION
Flaws are identified through:
  - Microsoft Defender Vulnerability Management (continuous)
  - Microsoft Update / Windows Update for Business
  - CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog alerts
  - Vendor security advisories (Microsoft, Cisco, etc.)
  - Security awareness (staff reporting unexpected behavior)

3. REPORTING
  - Flaws identified by automated tools: alerts to Mike Sadler
    via email (Sentinel alert rules) and Defender portal
  - Flaws reported by staff: to Mike Sadler via Teams/email
  - Mike Sadler reports critical flaws to Chief Bowles same day

4. CORRECTION TIMEFRAMES
  Severity    Timeline
  --------    -------------------------
  Critical    72 hours (3 business days)
  High        30 calendar days
  Medium      90 calendar days
  Low         180 calendar days (or accept)

5. PATCH MANAGEMENT
  - Patches deployed via Intune Windows Update for Business
  - Standard patch cycle: Second Tuesday of the month
  - Emergency patches: deployed out-of-cycle within 72 hours
  - Application updates: deployed via Intune app management

6. TRACKING
  All flaws tracked in the BFR Vulnerability Tracking Log
  (SharePoint: IT/Vulnerability-Log.xlsx) with status updates.
