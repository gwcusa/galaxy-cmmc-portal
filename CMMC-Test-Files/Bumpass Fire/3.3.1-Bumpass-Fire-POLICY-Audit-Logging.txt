AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.3.1 - System Audit Logging
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to create, protect, and retain information system audit records to enable monitoring, analysis, investigation, and reporting of unlawful or unauthorized activity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.1.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue information systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logging is enabled on all CUI systems for login, logout, access, and change events.
b. Audit logs are retained for a minimum of 90 days online and 1 year archived.
c. Log integrity is protected to prevent tampering.
d. Mike Sadler configures log collection to the centralized SIEM.
e. Logging failures generate immediate alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and maintains the logging infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Disabling or tampering with audit logs is a serious security violation.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
