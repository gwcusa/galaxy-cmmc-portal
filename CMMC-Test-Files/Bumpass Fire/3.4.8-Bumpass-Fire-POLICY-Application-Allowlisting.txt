CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.4.8 - Application Allowlisting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to apply deny-by-default and allow-by-exception policies to prevent use of unauthorized software, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.8.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI endpoints and servers.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Only approved applications may execute on CUI endpoints.
b. Application allowlisting is enforced via Windows Defender Application Control (WDAC).
c. All new software requests require review and approval by Mike Sadler and Randy Bowles.
d. The approved application list is reviewed and updated quarterly.
e. Attempts to execute unapproved software generate security alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, creates and maintains the application allowlist and WDAC policy.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Execution of unapproved software is blocked automatically and generates an alert.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
