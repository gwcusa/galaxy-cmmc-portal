ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.19 - CUI Encryption on Mobile Devices
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to encrypt CUI on mobile devices and mobile computing platforms, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.19.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue mobile devices and portable computing platforms that store or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All mobile devices storing CUI must enable full-device encryption.
b. Windows laptops use BitLocker with TPM (AES-256).
c. iOS devices use hardware AES encryption (enabled by default with passcode).
d. Android devices enable full-disk encryption.
e. Encryption status is verified through Intune compliance reports.
f. Non-encrypted devices are blocked from CUI access until remediated.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, enforces encryption requirements via Intune compliance policies.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Non-compliant unencrypted devices are quarantined from CUI access within 2 hours.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
