AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.3.8 - Audit Log Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy protects Bumpass Fire & Rescue audit information and tools from unauthorized access, modification, and deletion, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.8.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue audit records, logging infrastructure, and audit management tools.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Audit logs are stored in a system where operational users cannot delete or modify records.
b. Access to raw audit log data is restricted to Mike Sadler and authorized assessors.
c. Audit log storage uses append-only or immutable storage mechanisms.
d. Backup copies of audit logs are stored separately from production systems.
e. Log access attempts are themselves logged.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, manages audit log access controls and immutability settings.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any modification or deletion of audit records is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
