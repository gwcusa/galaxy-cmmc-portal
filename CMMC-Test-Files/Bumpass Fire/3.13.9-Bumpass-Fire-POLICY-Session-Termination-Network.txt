SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.13.9 - Network Session Termination
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to terminate network connections associated with communications sessions after a defined period of inactivity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.9.

2. SCOPE
All network sessions in Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems including VPN, web, and application sessions.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Network sessions terminate after 60 minutes of inactivity.
b. VPN sessions terminate after 60 minutes idle or 8 hours maximum.
c. Web application sessions (SharePoint) terminate after 30 minutes idle.
d. Terminated sessions require full re-authentication.
e. Session timeout settings are enforced at the network/application level.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures session termination on all network services.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Network services without compliant session timeout settings must be remediated within 72 hours.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
