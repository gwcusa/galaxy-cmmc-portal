IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.5.5 - MFA for Privileged Accounts
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to employ multifactor authentication for network access to privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.5.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue privileged accounts (Global Admin, Security Admin, IT Administrator roles).

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. MFA is required every time a privileged account authenticates for network access.
b. Privileged accounts are enrolled in MFA before being activated.
c. PIM-gated privileged access requires MFA at activation.
d. No persistent privileged sessions are permitted; re-authentication is required.
e. Failed privileged account MFA attempts generate immediate alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and enforces MFA for all privileged accounts.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any privileged account session without verified MFA is blocked and alerted.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
