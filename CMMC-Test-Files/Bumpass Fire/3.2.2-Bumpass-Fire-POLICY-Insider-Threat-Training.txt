1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures all BFR personnel receive training on
recognizing and reporting potential insider threat indicators.

2. REQUIRED TRAINING
All personnel with access to CUI-related systems complete:
  - Annual Insider Threat Awareness training (KnowBe4 module)
  - Phishing simulation tests (quarterly)
  - Social engineering awareness module (annual)

3. TRAINING CONTENT
The insider threat module covers:
  - Definition and types of insider threats
    (malicious, negligent, compromised)
  - Behavioral indicators: unusual data access, after-hours
    activity, large downloads, removable media use
  - Social engineering tactics: pretexting, vishing, phishing
  - Reporting procedures: who to contact, how to report
    (email to Chief Bowles + Mike Sadler)
  - Consequences of insider threat activity

4. REPORTING
Personnel who suspect insider threat activity must:
  - Report immediately to Chief Randy Bowles
  - Report to Mike Sadler for technical investigation
  - Reports are confidential and protected from retaliation

5. COMPLETION TRACKING
Training completion tracked in KnowBe4 platform.
Non-completers are escalated to the Chief within 7 days
of the training deadline.
