AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.2.3 - Insider Threat Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Bumpass Fire & Rescue provides security training on recognizing and reporting potential insider threats, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.2.3.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue personnel who may observe or have knowledge of insider threat indicators.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Personnel are trained to recognize physical, behavioral, and technical indicators of insider threat.
b. Reporting mechanisms are clearly communicated and accessible to all personnel.
c. Anonymous reporting is available and confidentiality is maintained.
d. Reports are reviewed by Randy Bowles within 24 hours.
e. Training on reporting procedures is provided annually.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler administers technical monitoring. Randy Bowles reviews insider threat reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Retaliation against personnel who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
