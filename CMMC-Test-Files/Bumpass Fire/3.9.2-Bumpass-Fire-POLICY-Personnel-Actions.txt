1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures CUI systems are protected during and after
personnel actions such as terminations and transfers.

2. TERMINATION PROCEDURES

2.1 Voluntary Termination
  On the employee's last day:
    a) IT access disabled by Mike Sadler (AD account disabled)
    b) Azure AD account disabled and all sessions revoked
    c) M365 license removed or converted to shared mailbox
    d) Physical access removed (key fob deactivated, codes changed)
    e) Company devices (laptop, phone) collected and inventoried
    f) Personal data removed from company devices per data handling policy

2.2 Involuntary Termination
  Access revocation occurs SIMULTANEOUSLY with notification:
    a) HR/Command notifies Mike Sadler BEFORE informing the employee
    b) Mike disables AD account and revokes M365 sessions
    c) Physical access revoked
    d) All above completed before the separation meeting ends

3. TRANSFER PROCEDURES
  When an employee changes roles:
    a) Former role access is removed within 24 hours
    b) New role access is granted per the new role definition
    c) The principle of least privilege is re-evaluated
    d) Mike Sadler updates group memberships accordingly

4. ACCESS REVIEW TRIGGER
  Any personnel action triggers an immediate access review
  by Mike Sadler to ensure no access is carried over
  inappropriately.

5. DOCUMENTATION
  All termination/transfer access changes logged in the
  IT Action Log and HR personnel file.
