PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.10.5 - Physical Access Device Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to control and manage physical access devices (keys, key cards, PINs), per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.5.

2. SCOPE
All physical access devices used to access Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. A register of all physical access devices (keys, PINs, badges) is maintained.
b. Physical access devices are issued only to authorized personnel.
c. Lost or stolen access devices are reported and deactivated within 2 hours.
d. Access PINs are changed quarterly or upon compromise.
e. Access devices are collected upon personnel termination.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Randy Bowles, Fire Chief, approves access device issuance. Mike Sadler maintains the access device register.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unreported loss of physical access devices is a policy violation.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
