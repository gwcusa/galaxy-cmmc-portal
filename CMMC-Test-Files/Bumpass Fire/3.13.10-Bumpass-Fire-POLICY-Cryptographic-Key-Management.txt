SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.13.10 - Cryptographic Key Management
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to establish and manage cryptographic keys for required cryptography, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.10.

2. SCOPE
All cryptographic keys used in Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Cryptographic keys are generated, stored, and managed in Azure Key Vault.
b. Keys are rotated at least annually or upon personnel departure.
c. Access to Key Vault is restricted to Mike Sadler and specific service principals.
d. Key operations are logged and audited.
e. Compromised keys are immediately revoked and replaced.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, manages all cryptographic keys for CUI systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unmanaged or expired keys are a critical finding requiring immediate remediation.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
