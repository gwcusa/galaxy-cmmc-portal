1. SUMMARY
Evidence that Bumpass Fire & Rescue prohibits use of portable storage without an identifiable owner.

2. TECHNICAL CONTROLS
Intune Device Control Policy: USBDeviceControl-Bumpass Fire & Rescue
- Only allowlisted devices (by hardware ID and serial number) may connect
- Each allowlisted device has a documented owner in the Approved Media Register
- Devices without registered owner: BLOCKED

This ensures no ownerless device can be used on CUI endpoints, even if someone attempts to connect one.
