MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.7.5 - Remote Maintenance MFA
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to require MFA to establish remote maintenance sessions and to terminate such connections after maintenance, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.5.

2. SCOPE
All remote maintenance sessions on Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Remote maintenance sessions require MFA before the session is established.
b. Session credentials are unique to the maintenance session and not reused.
c. Remote maintenance connections are terminated immediately after the session ends.
d. Remote sessions are logged with start time, end time, and actions performed.
e. Mike Sadler monitors all remote maintenance sessions in real-time.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, manages and monitors remote maintenance sessions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Remote maintenance sessions without MFA are blocked and investigated.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
