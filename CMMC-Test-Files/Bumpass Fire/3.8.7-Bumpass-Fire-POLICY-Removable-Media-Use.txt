MEDIA PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.8.7 - Removable Media Use Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the use of removable media on system components, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.8.7.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI endpoints and the removable media used with them.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Only Mike Sadler-approved removable media may be used on CUI endpoints.
b. Approved media must be encrypted (AES-256 hardware or software).
c. Intune device control policies block unapproved USB storage devices.
d. A register of approved removable media is maintained by Mike Sadler.
e. Approved media is inventoried and audited quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, manages the approved media register and Intune device control policies.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unapproved removable media connected to CUI endpoints is blocked and generates a security alert.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
