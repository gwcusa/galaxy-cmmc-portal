INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.6.2 - Incident Tracking and Reporting
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to track, document, and report incidents to appropriate officials, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.6.2.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue security incidents involving CUI systems or data.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All incidents are tracked in the IT helpdesk ticketing system.
b. Incidents are classified by severity (Low/Medium/High/Critical).
c. High and Critical incidents are reported to Randy Bowles within 1 hour.
d. CUI-related incidents are reported to DoD/US-CERT as required.
e. Incident records are retained for 3 years.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, tracks and documents all incidents. Randy Bowles receives escalation reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to report a known incident is a policy violation subject to disciplinary action.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
