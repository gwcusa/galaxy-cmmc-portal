1. PURPOSE
This policy controls the connection of mobile devices
(smartphones, tablets, and personal laptops) to Bumpass
Fire & Rescue networks and systems.

2. APPROVED MOBILE DEVICES
  Only the following mobile devices are permitted to connect
  to BFR's corporate network or access BFR systems:
    - Company-issued mobile devices enrolled in Intune MDM
    - Currently issued: 4 department iPhones (dispatch and command)
    - Personally owned devices: NOT permitted on corp network
      (BYOD is prohibited for CUI access)

3. ENROLLMENT REQUIREMENTS
  All approved mobile devices must:
    - Be enrolled in Microsoft Intune MDM
    - Have a minimum 6-digit PIN or biometric lock
    - Have the BFR Authenticator app installed (for MFA)
    - Have automatic OS updates enabled
    - Have data encryption enabled (enforced by Intune)

4. NETWORK SEGMENTATION
  - Company iPhones connect to BRFD-Mobile SSID (separate VLAN)
  - This VLAN has access to email (M365) and dispatch app only
  - No access to file servers or CUI storage from mobile SSID

5. LOST/STOLEN DEVICES
  Any lost or stolen device must be reported to Mike Sadler
  immediately. Intune remote wipe will be performed within
  1 hour of notification.
