SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.13.4 - Shared Resource Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to prevent unauthorized and unintended information transfer via shared system resources, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.4.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue systems with shared resources (memory, storage, network) in the CUI environment.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Virtual machines and containers are isolated to prevent data leakage between workloads.
b. Temporary storage is cleared between user sessions.
c. Printer spooler directories are cleared after each job.
d. Memory is overwritten or not re-used across security boundaries without clearing.
e. Mike Sadler verifies isolation controls quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and verifies shared resource isolation controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Shared resource isolation failures are treated as potential data leakage incidents.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
