MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.7.1 - System Maintenance
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Money Straight Talk LLC's requirements for performing maintenance on information systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.1.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC hardware and software systems that process, store, or transmit CUI.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Maintenance activities are scheduled and documented in advance.
b. Security patches are applied within 30 days of release (critical: 72 hours).
c. Maintenance windows are communicated to Camille Odom before scheduled downtime.
d. Post-maintenance verification confirms system functionality and security settings.
e. All maintenance activities are logged with date, type, and personnel involved.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, schedules and performs all system maintenance.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Overdue patches trigger automated alerts and escalation to Camille Odom after 30 days.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
