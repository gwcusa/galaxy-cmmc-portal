ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.1.22 - CUI on Publicly Accessible Systems
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls CUI posted or processed on publicly accessible Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.22.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC publicly accessible systems including websites, social media, and public-facing portals.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI is prohibited from being posted on any publicly accessible system.
b. Victor Osei must review and approve all content before it is published publicly.
c. Nathan Cruz conducts quarterly scans of public-facing systems for inadvertent CUI exposure.
d. Discovered CUI on public systems is removed within 2 hours and reported to management.
e. Staff are trained annually on CUI handling and prohibition of public posting.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz scans public systems. Victor Osei approves public content. All staff are responsible for not posting CUI.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Posting CUI to publicly accessible systems is a serious policy violation subject to immediate investigation.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
