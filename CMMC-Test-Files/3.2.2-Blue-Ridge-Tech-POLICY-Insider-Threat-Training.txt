AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.2.2 - Insider Threat Awareness Training
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC trains personnel to recognize and report potential indicators of insider threat, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.2.2.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC personnel with access to CUI systems or facilities.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Insider threat awareness training is completed annually by all personnel.
b. Training covers behavioral indicators, reporting channels, and non-retaliation protections.
c. Personnel are trained on the Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC anonymous reporting mechanism.
d. Training records are maintained for 3 years.
e. Victor Osei receives quarterly insider threat awareness briefings.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, administers insider threat training. Victor Osei oversees the insider threat program.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to complete insider threat training results in CUI access suspension.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
