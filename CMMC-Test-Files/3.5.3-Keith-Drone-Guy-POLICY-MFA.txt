IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.5.3 - Multifactor Authentication
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to use multifactor authentication for local and network access to privileged accounts and network access to non-privileged accounts, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.3.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC personnel accessing CUI systems locally or remotely.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. MFA is required for all network access to CUI systems.
b. MFA is required for local logon to privileged accounts.
c. MFA uses at least two factors from: something you know, something you have, something you are.
d. MFA enrollment is completed within 24 hours of account provisioning.
e. MFA bypass is prohibited except during documented disaster recovery scenarios.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, enforces MFA through Conditional Access policies.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Access without MFA is technically blocked by Conditional Access.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
