ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.1.6 - Use of Non-Privileged Accounts
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty personnel to use non-privileged accounts when accessing non-security functions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.6.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty personnel who hold or have access to administrative or privileged accounts.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Users with administrative accounts must maintain separate standard accounts for daily work.
b. Email, web browsing, and productivity applications must be used via non-privileged accounts.
c. Privileged accounts shall not be used to access social media or personal email.
d. Sandra Okonkwo shall enforce dual-account policy through technical controls where feasible.
e. Compliance with this policy is verified during quarterly access reviews.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, maintains dual-account configurations. Anthony James enforces the policy organizationally.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Using a privileged account for non-administrative tasks is a policy violation subject to corrective action.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
