ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.12 - Remote Access Monitoring and Control
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes Bumpass Fire & Rescue's requirements for monitoring and controlling remote access sessions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.12.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue personnel and contractors who access Bumpass Fire & Rescue systems remotely.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All remote access sessions are conducted through the approved VPN solution.
b. Remote sessions are logged including user identity, source IP, and duration.
c. Mike Sadler reviews remote access logs weekly.
d. Simultaneous remote sessions for a single account are limited to 1.
e. Remote access from non-managed devices is blocked.
f. Anomalous remote access patterns generate immediate security alerts.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, manages the VPN solution and reviews remote access logs.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized remote access attempts are treated as security incidents.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
