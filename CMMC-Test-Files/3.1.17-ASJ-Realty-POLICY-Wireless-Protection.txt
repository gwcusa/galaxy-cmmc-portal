1. PURPOSE
This policy requires that wireless access at ASJ Realty is
protected using strong authentication and encryption to
prevent unauthorized access and eavesdropping.

2. ENCRYPTION REQUIREMENTS
  - All corporate wireless networks must use WPA3-Enterprise
    or, at minimum, WPA2-Enterprise (AES/CCMP encryption)
  - WEP, WPA (TKIP), and WPA2-Personal (PSK) are prohibited
    on any network that carries company or CUI data
  - Guest networks may use WPA2-Personal with a minimum
    16-character PSK rotated weekly

3. AUTHENTICATION REQUIREMENTS
  - Corporate SSID: 802.1X authentication required
    (user credentials validated against Active Directory)
  - Devices must present a valid machine certificate issued
    by the company CA (via Intune SCEP profile)
  - MFA is required for initial device enrollment but not
    for each WiFi connection (certificate handles this)

4. PROHIBITED CONFIGURATIONS
  - Open (unencrypted) WiFi: PROHIBITED
  - WEP encryption: PROHIBITED
  - Default router/AP credentials: PROHIBITED
  - SSID broadcasting on IoT/management networks: PROHIBITED
    (SSIDs for management are hidden)

5. ACCESS POINT SECURITY
  - All APs managed via Meraki cloud dashboard
  - AP admin access restricted to Sandra Okonkwo
  - Firmware auto-updated within 7 days of release
