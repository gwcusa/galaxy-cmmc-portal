ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.1.14 - Managed Access Control Points for Remote Access
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to route all remote access through managed access control points, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.14.

2. SCOPE
All remote access to Bumpass Fire & Rescue CUI systems and networks.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All remote access is routed through a centrally managed VPN gateway.
b. Split tunneling is disabled; all traffic routes through Bumpass Fire & Rescue gateway.
c. Direct internet access to internal CUI systems from outside the VPN is blocked.
d. The VPN gateway is managed exclusively by Mike Sadler.
e. Firewall rules enforce that CUI systems are reachable only via VPN.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, maintains the VPN gateway and access control infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Attempts to bypass the managed access point are security incidents.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
