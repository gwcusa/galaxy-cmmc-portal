SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.13.1 - Network Boundary Protection
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to monitor, control, and protect communications at external and internal network boundaries, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.1.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC network boundaries separating CUI systems from external networks.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Firewalls protect all network boundaries between CUI systems and external networks.
b. Intrusion Prevention System (IPS) monitors traffic at network boundaries.
c. Traffic is logged and reviewed for anomalies.
d. Default-deny rules are in place; only approved traffic is allowed.
e. Firewall rules are reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, manages all boundary protection devices and configurations.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized network boundary changes are security incidents requiring immediate investigation.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
