SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.13.9 - Network Session Termination
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to terminate network connections associated with communications sessions after a defined period of inactivity, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.9.

2. SCOPE
All network sessions in ASJ Realty CUI systems including VPN, web, and application sessions.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Network sessions terminate after 60 minutes of inactivity.
b. VPN sessions terminate after 60 minutes idle or 8 hours maximum.
c. Web application sessions (SharePoint) terminate after 30 minutes idle.
d. Terminated sessions require full re-authentication.
e. Session timeout settings are enforced at the network/application level.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, configures session termination on all network services.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Network services without compliant session timeout settings must be remediated within 72 hours.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
