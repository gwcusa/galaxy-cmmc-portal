SYSTEM AND INFORMATION INTEGRITY POLICY
Organization: ASJ Realty
Control: 3.14.1 - Flaw Remediation
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director  |  Approved: Anthony James, Office Manager

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires ASJ Realty to identify, report, and correct information system flaws in a timely manner, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.14.1.

2. SCOPE
All ASJ Realty CUI systems and software.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All software and operating systems receive security patches per vulnerability SLA.
b. Critical patches (CVSS 9.0+): applied within 72 hours.
c. High patches (CVSS 7.0-8.9): applied within 7 days.
d. Medium/Low: applied within 30-90 days.
e. Patch status is monitored via Intune and reported monthly to Anthony James.
f. Unpatched systems are isolated from CUI until patched.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Sandra Okonkwo, IT Director, manages patch deployment. Anthony James reviews monthly patch reports.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with critical unpatched vulnerabilities beyond 72 hours are removed from CUI network.

Signed: Anthony James, Office Manager  |  ASJ Realty  |  2026-01-15
