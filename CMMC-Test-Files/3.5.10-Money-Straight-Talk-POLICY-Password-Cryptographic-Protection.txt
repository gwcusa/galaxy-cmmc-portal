IDENTIFICATION AND AUTHENTICATION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.5.10 - Cryptographic Password Storage
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Money Straight Talk LLC to store and transmit only cryptographically protected passwords, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.5.10.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC systems that store or transmit user passwords.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.
b. Password storage uses strong one-way hashing (bcrypt, PBKDF2, or Argon2).
c. Reversible encryption of passwords is prohibited and technically disabled.
d. Passwords are transmitted only over encrypted channels (TLS 1.2+).
e. Terrell Banks verifies cryptographic password protection settings quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Terrell Banks, Systems Admin, ensures password storage and transmission uses cryptographic protection.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems storing passwords in plaintext or reversible encryption are immediately remediated.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
