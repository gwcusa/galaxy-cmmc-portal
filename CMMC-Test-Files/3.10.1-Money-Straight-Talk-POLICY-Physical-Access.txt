PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Money Straight Talk LLC
Control: 3.10.1 - Physical Access Authorization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Terrell Banks, Systems Admin  |  Approved: Camille Odom, CEO

1. PURPOSE
This policy limits physical access to Money Straight Talk LLC CUI systems to authorized individuals, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.1.

2. SCOPE
All Money Straight Talk LLC facilities where CUI systems are located.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI system areas are access-controlled with electronic key card or coded entry.
b. A list of authorized personnel with physical access is maintained by Camille Odom.
c. The authorized access list is reviewed and updated quarterly.
d. Visitors are not permitted unescorted access to CUI areas.
e. Access rights are revoked within 24 hours of termination.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Camille Odom, CEO, approves physical access authorizations. Terrell Banks maintains technical controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized physical access to CUI areas is a security incident.

Signed: Camille Odom, CEO  |  Money Straight Talk LLC  |  2026-01-15
