PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.10.2 - Physical Monitoring
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to protect and monitor the physical facility and support infrastructure for CUI systems, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.10.2.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC facilities where CUI systems are located.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Security cameras cover all entry and exit points to CUI areas.
b. Camera footage is retained for 30 days.
c. Keith Merritt reviews camera footage weekly or upon any incident report.
d. A security alarm is installed and armed during non-business hours.
e. Alarm events are responded to within 15 minutes by designated personnel.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, manages the physical monitoring infrastructure.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any detected physical security breach is treated as a security incident.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
