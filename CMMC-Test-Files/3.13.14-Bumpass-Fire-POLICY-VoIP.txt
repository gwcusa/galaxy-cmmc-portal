SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.13.14 - Voice over IP
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to protect the authenticity of communications sessions for VoIP and other session-based communications, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.14.

2. SCOPE
All VoIP and collaboration communications at Bumpass Fire & Rescue involving CUI discussion.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. VoIP communications use encrypted channels (TLS/SRTP minimum).
b. Microsoft Teams is the approved VoIP and collaboration platform for CUI discussions.
c. Unapproved VoIP applications may not be used for CUI-related communications.
d. Meeting recordings involving CUI require explicit consent from all participants.
e. Teams meeting settings are configured to prevent unauthorized guest access.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and manages Teams VoIP security settings.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Use of unapproved VoIP for CUI discussions is a policy violation and security incident.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
