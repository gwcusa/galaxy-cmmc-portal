CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.4.6 - Least Functionality
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to configure information systems to provide only essential capabilities, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.6.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC information systems and devices in the CUI environment.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Systems are configured with only the functions and services required for their mission.
b. Unnecessary services, ports, and applications are disabled or removed.
c. Default accounts and sample applications are disabled before deployment.
d. Keith Merritt audits running services on all CUI systems quarterly.
e. Any service additions require documented approval from Keith Merritt.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures systems to least functionality standard and maintains service inventories.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized services discovered during audits are disabled within 24 hours.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
