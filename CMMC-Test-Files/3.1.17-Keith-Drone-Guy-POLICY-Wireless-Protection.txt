ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Organization: Keith the Drone Guy LLC
Control: 3.1.17 - Wireless Access Authentication and Encryption
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Approved: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Keith the Drone Guy LLC to protect wireless access using authentication and encryption, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.1.17.

2. SCOPE
All Keith the Drone Guy LLC wireless networks that support access to CUI systems.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. CUI wireless networks use WPA3-Enterprise (WPA2-Enterprise minimum) with 802.1X.
b. Encryption uses AES-CCMP (128-bit minimum).
c. WEP and TKIP are prohibited.
d. Wireless authentication certificates are issued by the Keith the Drone Guy LLC internal PKI.
e. Keith Merritt reviews wireless encryption configurations quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator, configures and maintains wireless encryption and authentication.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Any wireless network in violation of these standards must be taken offline immediately.

Signed: Keith Merritt, Owner-Operator  |  Keith the Drone Guy LLC  |  2026-01-15
