SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.13.15 - Session Authenticity
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Bumpass Fire & Rescue to protect the authenticity of communications sessions, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.15.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue communications sessions, including email, web sessions, and API connections.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Email authenticity is enforced via SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records.
b. Web sessions use TLS certificates with proper authentication.
c. DMARC policy enforces rejection of spoofed email from bumpassfire.org.
d. DKIM is configured for all outbound email.
e. Email authentication records are reviewed quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures and maintains email and session authentication controls.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Failed email authentication checks result in mail rejection per DMARC policy.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
