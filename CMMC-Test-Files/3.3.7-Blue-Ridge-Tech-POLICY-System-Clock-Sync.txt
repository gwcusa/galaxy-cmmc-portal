AUDIT AND ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.3.7 - System Clock Synchronization
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to provide a system capability that compares and synchronizes internal system clocks with an authoritative source, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.3.7.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC information systems that generate audit records.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. All systems synchronize clocks with an authoritative NTP time source.
b. NTP source: time.windows.com or NIST time servers (time.nist.gov).
c. Maximum clock drift is 1 second from authoritative source.
d. NTP configuration is enforced via Group Policy on Windows endpoints.
e. Nathan Cruz verifies clock synchronization quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and verifies NTP synchronization on all systems.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Systems with clock drift exceeding 5 minutes are quarantined from CUI access.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
