SYSTEM AND COMMUNICATIONS PROTECTION POLICY
Organization: Bumpass Fire & Rescue
Control: 3.13.7 - Split Tunneling Prohibition
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator  |  Approved: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief

1. PURPOSE
This policy prevents Bumpass Fire & Rescue remote access VPN clients from using split tunneling, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.13.7.

2. SCOPE
All Bumpass Fire & Rescue remote access VPN connections.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Split tunneling is disabled on all VPN profiles.
b. When VPN is connected, all traffic (including internet) routes through the Bumpass Fire & Rescue gateway.
c. VPN configuration is enforced by managed Intune profiles and cannot be changed by users.
d. Mike Sadler verifies split tunneling is disabled quarterly.
e. Any detected split tunnel configuration is immediately remediated.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Mike Sadler, IT Coordinator, configures VPN profiles with split tunneling disabled.

5. ENFORCEMENT
VPN connections with split tunneling enabled are disconnected and reconfigured.

Signed: Randy Bowles, Fire Chief  |  Bumpass Fire & Rescue  |  2026-01-15
