1. PURPOSE
This policy requires monitoring, controlling, and protecting
organizational communications at external boundaries and
key internal boundaries.

2. EXTERNAL BOUNDARY PROTECTION
  - Cisco Meraki MX68 serves as the external boundary device
    (firewall, IDS/IPS, and VPN gateway)
  - All internet traffic flows through the MX68
  - Default deny-all inbound rule (only VPN traffic allowed in)
  - Outbound traffic filtered by URL category (Meraki Content
    Filtering: blocks malicious sites, adult content, etc.)
  - Meraki Threat Prevention: IPS/IDS signatures (updated daily)

3. INTERNAL BOUNDARY PROTECTION
  - VLANs segment the internal network:
    VLAN 10: Corporate (CUI workstations)
    VLAN 20: Guest (internet only, isolated)
    VLAN 30: IoT devices (isolated)
  - Inter-VLAN routing controlled by Meraki firewall rules
  - Guest VLAN has no access to Corporate VLAN

4. MONITORING
  - Meraki Security Center: logs all IPS/IDS events
  - DNS content filtering: Cisco Umbrella (blocks malicious DNS)
  - Traffic anomalies forwarded to Microsoft Sentinel

5. MANAGEMENT TRAFFIC
  - Out-of-band management where possible
  - Meraki dashboard accessible only from VPN or
    registered admin MAC/IP
