CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.4.7 - Restrict Software Functions
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy restricts, disables, or prevents the use of programs, functions, ports, protocols, and services not required at Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.4.7.

2. SCOPE
All Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems, endpoints, and network devices.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Firewall rules block all ports and protocols not required for business operations.
b. Software restriction policies prevent execution of unapproved applications.
c. Removable media autorun is disabled on all endpoints.
d. Macro execution in Office documents is restricted to digitally signed macros only.
e. Nathan Cruz reviews firewall rules and software restriction policies quarterly.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, configures and maintains software and network function restrictions.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unauthorized software functions discovered in audits are disabled within 24 hours.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
