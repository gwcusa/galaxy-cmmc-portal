MAINTENANCE POLICY
Organization: Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC
Control: 3.7.2 - Controlled Maintenance
Version: 1.2  |  Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Nathan Cruz, IT Manager  |  Approved: Victor Osei, Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy requires Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC to provide controls on tools, techniques, mechanisms, and personnel who perform maintenance, per NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 control 3.7.2.

2. SCOPE
All maintenance activities on Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC CUI systems by internal staff or vendors.

3. REQUIREMENTS
a. Vendor maintenance personnel are pre-approved by Victor Osei before being granted access.
b. Vendor access is escorted by Nathan Cruz throughout the maintenance activity.
c. Vendor tools and diagnostic software are inspected before use.
d. Remote vendor access is monitored in real-time by Nathan Cruz.
e. All vendor maintenance is logged with contact details, scope, and outcome.

4. RESPONSIBILITY
Nathan Cruz, IT Manager, controls and supervises all maintenance activities.

5. ENFORCEMENT
Unsupervised vendor access to CUI systems is prohibited.

Signed: Victor Osei, Director of Operations  |  Blue Ridge Technology Solutions LLC  |  2026-01-15
